# How to Use This Pack

This folder is a self-study pack for the **MPT (MCQ-Based Preliminary Test)**, the screening test you must pass before you can apply for the CSS written examination. It was built on 23 Nov 2026 for a candidate sitting the MPT for **CSS 2028**, expected around Sep–Oct 2027.

Everything marked **[verify]** could not be confirmed from an official FPSC document and must be checked against the CSS 2028 MPT notice and your admission certificate when they come out (expected about Aug 2027).

---

## 1. The test at a glance

| Item | What applies | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Questions and marks | 200 MCQs, 200 marks, one mark each | CSS CE Rules 2019, Appendix-III (S.R.O. 1516(I)/2021) |
| Options per question | Four (A–D) in the 2026 and 2027 papers | Recalled papers |
| Islamic Studies (non-Muslims may choose Civics and Ethics) | 20 marks | Appendix-III |
| Urdu (grammar usage and translation) | 20 marks | Appendix-III |
| English (vocabulary, grammar usage, comprehension) | 50 marks | Appendix-III |
| General Abilities (SSC-level arithmetic, algebra, geometry; logical, analytical and mental abilities) | 60 marks | Appendix-III |
| General Knowledge (everyday science, current affairs, Pakistan affairs) | 50 marks | Appendix-III |
| Pass mark | 33% overall = **66 out of 200**. No separate pass mark per section. | Rule 10A(5) |
| Duration | 200 minutes **[verify]** | FPSC's first MPT notice (2022), as quoted by several sites; one site says 180 minutes. Not stated in the 2027 notice. |
| Negative marking | None **[verify]** | Same 2022 notice as quoted by several sites. Not stated in the rules or the 2027 notice. |
| Mode | Paper based, answers marked on an OMR sheet | FPSC general instructions and recalled papers |
| Validity | Only for the CSS examination of the same year | Rule 10A(4) |
| Attempts | No limit on MPT attempts while you are within the age limit. The MPT does not count as a CSS attempt. | Rule 10A(6) |
| Fee | Rs 250, paid online through a PSID | Appendix-II and the 2027 notice |
| Does the score count later? | No. It only decides who may apply for the written examination. | Rule 10A(1) |

The marks split in your brief (20 / 20 / 50 / 60 / 50) is **correct**. Sites that give a different split (for example 15 marks for Urdu and 15 for Civics) are wrong. See `01-Syllabus-and-Pattern.md` for the sources.

## 2. Test-day rules

From the FPSC rules, the 2027 MPT notice and the FPSC general instructions (updated 12 Aug 2026):

1. Download your **admission certificate** from the FPSC website. It is posted at least 10 days before the test. Nothing is sent by post.
2. Bring the admission certificate and your **original CNIC** (or passport). Without identity you are marked absent.
3. Report **45 minutes before** the start. The hall doors close **15 minutes before** the start, and no one is let in after that.
4. Use only a **black ballpoint pen** on the OMR sheet. Gel pens, ink pens, pointers and pencils are not allowed.
5. A **clipboard** is allowed.
6. Mobile phones and any communication device are banned in the hall. Having one counts as misconduct.
7. Calculators: assume they are **not allowed** **[verify]**. All practice in this pack is designed to be done by hand.
8. Mark one circle per question. A question with two circles filled is treated as wrong. You cannot erase ballpoint ink, so decide first and fill second.
9. The test centre you pick in the form cannot be changed later.
10. In earlier years the answer sheet had a carbon copy that candidates could take home to check against the key **[verify]**.

## 3. Strategy

### Targets

You need 66. Aim for **120 or more (60%)**. A realistic split for your profile:

| Section | Marks | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 50 | 40 | Your strong area. Vocabulary is the only part that needs memory work. |
| General Abilities | 60 | 48 | Your strong area and the biggest section. SSC-level maths and standard reasoning. |
| Islamic Studies | 20 | 13 | Weak area, but a small and learnable fact base. |
| Urdu | 20 | 13 | Grammar and vocabulary at school level. |
| General Knowledge | 50 | 30 | Weak area. Science is the easiest third to lift. |
| **Total** | **200** | **144** | Leaves a margin of more than 20 marks above the 120 target. |

English and General Abilities together are 110 marks. If you score 85 or more there, you have passed before the other sections are counted. That is why the plan starts with them.

### Guessing policy

With no negative marking, **answer every question**. Never leave a circle empty. If the 2028 notice introduces negative marking, change the rule: with a penalty of 0.25 per wrong answer and four options, guess only when you can rule out at least one option. Check this on the notice before test day.

### Order of sections and time

The paper runs in this order: Islamic Studies (1–20), Urdu (21–40), English (41–90), General Abilities (91–150), General Knowledge (151–200). You may answer in any order. Suggested order and time for a 200-minute test:

| Order | Section | Questions | Time | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Islamic Studies | 20 | 10 min | 30 s each. You know it or you do not. |
| 2 | Urdu | 20 | 12 min | 35 s each. |
| 3 | General Knowledge | 50 | 28 min | 35 s each. Do not sit on a fact question. |
| 4 | English | 50 | 40 min | Vocabulary 30 s; passages get about 12 minutes. |
| 5 | General Abilities | 60 | 85 min | About 85 s each. This is where spare time pays. |
| 6 | Review | — | 25 min | Return to marked questions. Fill every blank. |

Rules while working:

- **Two passes.** On the first pass, answer what you can in the time above. Put a small mark on the question paper beside anything slow, and move on.
- **Fill the OMR circle as you go**, section by section, not all at the end. Check the question number every tenth question.
- Do the fact sections first. They are quick, and finishing 90 questions in 50 minutes settles the nerves and leaves the long block for maths.
- In maths, if a question has not opened up in 2 minutes, mark it, fill your best guess and leave.
- In the last 5 minutes, stop solving. Check that all 200 circles are filled.

If the duration turns out to be 180 minutes, cut English to 35, General Abilities to 75 and review to 20.

## 5. How to study from these files

1. **Read the notes once, actively.** Cover the right-hand side of a table and recall it. Do not highlight.
2. **Do the practice MCQs in blocks of 25, timed.** Give yourself 15 minutes per block for fact subjects and 35 minutes for General Abilities.
3. **Mark the block from the key.** For every wrong answer, write the fact in one line in an error log (a notebook or `02-Practice-and-Tracking/Error-Log.md` in the main CSS folder).
4. **Re-read the error log every third day.** Most of your gain comes from this step.
5. **Repeat a block only after a week**, never the next day.
6. Take the mock tests only in weeks 5 and 6, in one sitting, at the real time of day, with a black ballpoint and a printed or hand-drawn answer grid.

Time available in the plan below: about 2 hours on each working day and 5 hours on each weekend day, which is about 20 hours a week.

### If you fall behind

Drop work in this order: redo-sessions first, then the last 50 questions of each fact section, then Mock 4. Never drop the General Abilities and English practice, the error log, or Mocks 1 and 2.

### If you only have four weeks

Merge weeks 1 and 2 (do every second block of English and GA), keep weeks 3 and 4 as they are but on a 5-day rhythm, and take two mocks instead of four.

## 7. A warning about the paper's direction

The CSS 2027 MPT (held 10 Oct 2026) was noticeably more **conceptual and applied** than the 2026 paper. Islamic Studies asked about ideas (Ijtihad, Shura, Hisbah, Mudarabah, Iqbal, Muhammad Asad) more than dates. General Knowledge asked short scenario questions and current affairs from the three months before the test. Details are in `01-Syllabus-and-Pattern.md`. The practice sets here include both the classic fact style and the newer applied style. Do not prepare from one-line fact lists alone.

## 7. Refresh checklist for Aug 2027

Do this in the week before the six-week sprint starts. Two evenings are enough.

1. **Office holders.** Recheck every row of section 1. Certain to have changed: the UN Secretary-General (new from 1 Jan 2027). Likely to matter: any change of Chief Justice, army or services chiefs, State Bank governor, provincial governments, and leaders after elections abroad.
2. **Pakistan, last 12 months.** Budget 2027–28 (date, outlay, growth target, tax changes); Economic Survey 2026–27 (GDP growth, inflation, per-capita income); State Bank policy rate after each meeting; IMF programme status; any new constitutional amendment or major Act; relations with India, Afghanistan, Iran, Saudi Arabia, China and the USA; CPEC; census or NFC news; major court judgments.
3. **World, last 12 months.** Wars and ceasefires (Iran, Gaza, Ukraine, Yemen, Sudan); summits of 2027 (SCO, BRICS, G20, G7, NATO, OIC, SAARC if any, COP32) with host cities; UN General Assembly's 82nd session and its President; new members of organisations; new UN Security Council non-permanent members.
4. **Awards and sport.** Nobel Prizes 2027 are announced in early October and may fall before or after your test. Also: Cricket World Cup 2027, Asia Cup, PSL 2027, Champions League, Wimbledon, any Olympic bid decisions.
5. **The three months before the test** deserve the most care. Read one monthly current-affairs digest for June, July, August and September 2027 (see `10-Resources.md`).

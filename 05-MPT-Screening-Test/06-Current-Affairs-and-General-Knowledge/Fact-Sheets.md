## 1. The United Nations

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | 24 October 1945 (UN Day); Charter signed at San Francisco, 26 June 1945 |
| Headquarters | New York; European office in Geneva |
| Members | 193 (latest: South Sudan, 2011); observers: the Holy See and Palestine |

Secretaries-General in order: Trygve Lie, Dag Hammarskjöld, U Thant, Kurt Waldheim, Javier Pérez de Cuéllar, Boutros Boutros-Ghali, Kofi Annan, Ban Ki-moon, António Guterres (2017–2026).

## 3. Treaties, groupings and terms

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| NPT | Signed 1968, in force 1970; India, Pakistan and Israel never signed; North Korea withdrew |
| CTBT | 1996; not yet in force |
| New START | USA-Russia limits on strategic weapons; expired 5 Feb 2026 |
| Non-attack agreement (Pakistan-India) | Signed 1988; lists of nuclear sites exchanged every 1 January |
| UNFCCC | Rio, 1992; yearly Conferences of the Parties (COP) |
| Kyoto Protocol | 1997, in force 2005; binding cuts for developed countries only ("common but differentiated responsibilities") |
| Paris Agreement | 2015; hold warming well below 2°C and aim for 1.5°C; every country files its own pledge (NDC); global stocktake every five years; Loss and Damage Fund agreed at COP27 |
| Montreal Protocol | 1987; ozone-depleting substances |
| Belt and Road Initiative | Announced 2013: Silk Road Economic Belt plus 21st Century Maritime Silk Road; CPEC is its flagship in Pakistan |
| Quad | USA, India, Japan, Australia |
| AUKUS | Australia, UK, USA (2021) |
| I2U2 | India, Israel, UAE, USA |
| RCEP | Asia-Pacific trade pact: ASEAN states plus China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand |
| Abraham Accords | 2020: Israel with UAE, Bahrain, then Morocco and Sudan |
| Camp David Accords | 1978: Egypt and Israel |
| Oslo Accords | 1993: Israel and the PLO |
| Balfour Declaration | 1917 |
| SDGs | 17 goals, 2015–2030 (the MDGs were 8 goals, 2000–2015) |
| One-China principle | There is one China and Taiwan is part of it; Pakistan supports it |

History dates: League of Nations 1920; Second World War 1939–1945; Truman Doctrine 1947; NATO 1949; Warsaw Pact 1955–1991; Cuban Missile Crisis 1962; Soviet invasion of Afghanistan 1979; fall of the Berlin Wall 1989; end of the USSR 1991; 11 September 2001; Arab Spring from Dec 2010; UK leaves the EU 2020; Taliban return to Kabul 15 Aug 2021; Russia invades Ukraine 24 Feb 2022; Gaza war from 7 Oct 2023; fall of Assad 8 Dec 2024.

Terms: veto; sanctions; deterrence; hybrid warfare (force mixed with disinformation, cyber attacks and economic pressure); soft power; non-alignment; Global South; persona non grata; plebiscite; strategic depth; balance of power; containment.

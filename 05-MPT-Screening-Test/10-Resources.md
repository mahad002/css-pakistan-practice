# Resources for the MPT

This pack is meant to be enough for a comfortable pass. Use outside resources for three things only: official notices, real past papers, and fresh current affairs.

The web-search allowance for this session ran out during research, so book editions, prices and some site addresses below were **not rechecked** on 23–24 Nov 2026. Items marked **[verify]** should be confirmed before you buy or rely on them.

---

## 1. Official sources (check these first)

| Source | Address | Use |
|---|---|---|
| FPSC website | https://www.fpsc.gov.pk | MPT public notice (expected about Aug 2027), admission certificate, result. Check weekly from July 2027. |
| FPSC MPT rules | https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/MPTandMAIN?section=MPT%20Rules | The legal text of the marks split and the 33% rule |

| State Bank of Pakistan | https://www.sbp.org.pk | Monetary policy statements: the policy rate after each meeting |

Some tools and browsers reject the FPSC site's security certificate. If a link fails, open it in another browser.

## 2. Past papers

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| FPSC's own question papers and keys, when posted | The only official version. Do the 2025, 2026 and 2027 MPT papers under time in week 5, between your mocks. |
| Recalled papers on coaching sites (for example cssprep.com.pk) | Useful for pattern and topics. They are rebuilt from memory and contain wrong keys, so check any surprising answer. |
| CSS Forum, https://www.cssforum.com.pk | Past-paper threads and candidates' discussion of each paper. Quality varies. |
| A printed "CSS MPT solved papers" book from any major publisher (JWT, Dogar, HSM, ILMI) **[verify latest edition]** | Buy one edition that includes the Oct 2026 paper. One book is enough. |

How to use a past paper: do it timed, mark it, and add every wrong answer to your error log. Do not read solved papers like a textbook.

## 3. Books, by section

You do not need all of these. The first row in each section is the one to get if the fact sheets leave you short.

### English

| Book | Use |
|---|---|
| "Word Power Made Easy", Norman Lewis | Roots and word families; 20 minutes a day for a month |
| "High School English Grammar and Composition", Wren and Martin | Reference for any grammar rule you get wrong twice |
| "English Grammar in Use", Raymond Murphy | Quick units with exercises on tenses, conditionals and prepositions |
| "Exploring the World of English", Saadat Ali Shah | Widely used by CSS candidates for idioms, pairs of words and corrections |
| A GRE word list (if you are also preparing for the GRE) | The MPT's hardest synonym and antonym items are at this level, so that preparation counts twice |

### General Abilities

| Book | Use |
|---|---|
| Class 9 and 10 mathematics textbooks (any board) | The syllabus says "SSC level". Skim the chapters on algebra, sets and geometry. |
| "Quantitative Aptitude", R.S. Aggarwal | Extra drill by topic: percentages, ratio, time and work, speed |
| "A Modern Approach to Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning", R.S. Aggarwal | Series, coding, blood relations, directions, and the figure-based questions this pack cannot show |
| GRE or GAT quantitative practice (if you have it) | Same skills at a higher level |

### Islamic Studies

| Book | Use |
|---|---|
| A one-volume Islamiat MCQs book (for example by M. Imtiaz Shahid, Advanced Publishers) **[verify edition]** | Large bank of fact questions; do 30 a day in week 2 |
| Class 9 to 12 Islamiat textbooks | Clean, reliable basics |
| "Islam: Its Meaning and Message", ed. Khurshid Ahmad | Concepts for the newer style: Deen, society, economy, state |
| A summary of Iqbal's "Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam" and of Muhammad Asad's "Islam at the Crossroads" | Both were asked in the 2027 paper. A ten-page summary of each is enough. |

### Pakistan Affairs

### Everyday Science

| Book | Use |

### General knowledge and current affairs

| Resource | Use |

## 4. Free MCQ sites and apps

| Site or app | Use | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| TestPoint, https://www.testpointpk.com | Topic-wise MCQs and FPSC past-paper quizzes | Keys are user-entered; some are wrong |
| PakMcqs, https://pakmcqs.com | Very large bank for general knowledge, Pakistan affairs, Islamiat, science, English | Check doubtful answers |
| CSS Forum, https://www.cssforum.com.pk | Past papers and discussion | Old threads carry outdated facts |
| IndiaBIX, https://www.indiabix.com | Aptitude and reasoning practice with explanations | Ignore India-specific general knowledge |
| Khan Academy, https://www.khanacademy.org | Refresh any school maths topic in ten minutes | None |
| Anki (app) | Spaced-repetition flashcards. Put the fact-sheet tables into cards in week 1 and review 15 minutes a day. | Make your own cards; shared decks have errors |
| Vocabulary.com or Merriam-Webster | Meaning and usage of hard words | None |

Rule for every free MCQ site: if an answer contradicts this pack or a textbook, check a third source before you change what you have learned. Recalled papers and crowd-sourced keys are the main source of wrong "facts" in this exam.

## 5. A minimal kit

If you want to buy as little as possible:

1. This folder.
2. One book of real MPT past papers that includes the Oct 2026 paper.
3. One monthly current-affairs magazine for June to September 2027, plus Dawn.
4. Anki on your phone.

## 6. What to avoid

- Thick "MPT complete guide" books that reprint the whole CSS syllabus. The test needs breadth at MCQ depth.
- Memorising lists of rare words from recalled papers. They do not repeat.
- Starting more than eight weeks before the test, unless you are far below 33% on a first mock. The written examination deserves that time.
- Coaching-academy mock series that charge for what FPSC past papers give free.

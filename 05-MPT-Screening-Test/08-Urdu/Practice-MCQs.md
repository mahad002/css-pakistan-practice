# Urdu: Practice MCQs (اردو مشقی سوالات)

Written for the MPT Urdu section (20 marks). Urdu is confirmed as part of the test by the FPSC rules ("Urdu, including its grammar usage and translation"). The questions follow the official syllabus: parts of speech, compound verbs, tenses, singular and plural, sentence structure, pairs of words, gender, synonyms and antonyms, and translation. Idioms, proverbs and a few questions on the language and its writers are added because the real papers ask them.

Total questions: **150**. Each question has one correct option. The answer key with one-line explanations is at the end of this file.

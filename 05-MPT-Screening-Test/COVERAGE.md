## 2. How the answer keys were made and checked

- **Option order and key letters are produced by a script.** Every question was written with the correct answer first; a script shuffled the options and wrote the key. This removes the usual cause of wrong keys, a mistyped letter.
- **Mock tests were checked by script** for the right number of questions per part (20/20/50/60/50) and for any question repeated from the practice sets or from another mock: none.
- **General Abilities (540 questions in all).** 462 were generated by a program that computes each answer and writes the worked solution; 78 reasoning puzzles were written and solved by hand. After generation, **385 of the computed questions were recomputed by two separate checking scripts** that parse the question text and work out the answer independently. This found one error (a selling price that had been rounded down), which was fixed, and the affected types were rechecked. The remaining computed types (number and letter series, ordering puzzles, direction turns, data-table questions, number analogies) were checked by reading one worked sample of each type, not by a second script.
- **Fact questions (about 2,000).** Written from established knowledge and standard Pakistani exam conventions. They were **not individually checked against web sources**, because the web-search allowance for this session ran out after the research on the test format (about ten searches). A number of draft questions with debatable answers were removed or reworded. A small error rate may remain: if a key contradicts a textbook, trust the textbook and note it here.

### Current affairs (all dated "as of Nov 2026")

Everything about late 2025 and 2026 comes from Wikipedia pages read on 23 Nov 2026 and was **not cross-checked**. This includes: the US-Israel strikes on Iran and the death of Ali Khamenei; Pakistan's role in US-Iran talks; the fighting with Afghanistan; the UK change of prime minister; elections in Bangladesh, Nepal, Hungary and Brazil; the US mid-terms; the Nobel Prizes of 2026; World Cup and T20 World Cup results; the UAE leaving OPEC; New START's expiry; the ICJ ruling on Myanmar; governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; the 5G auction; the budget figures.

Specific weak points: the State Bank governor's name; the country of the UN General Assembly President; the names "Mecca Defence Alliance" and "Badr agreement"; the PSL 2025 winner; the policy rate figure for July 2026; the economic snapshot figures.

The UN Secretary-General for 2027 had not been chosen when this was written.

### Standing facts with known variation

- Islamic Studies: counts such as verses (6,236 or 6,666) and Rukus; the exact dates of Mi'raj and of the Prophet's birth; the year Hajj became obligatory. The pack follows the convention of Pakistani exam keys and says so.
- Pakistan: border lengths; the number of Senate seats after the FATA merger (96 used); the number of World Heritage Sites (6 used); whether the 26th and 27th Amendments changed details such as who appoints whom beyond what is stated.
- Urdu: the gender of a few words differs between Delhi and Lucknow usage; "ہامی" and "حامی" are both seen in print.
- English: a few usage points (for example "differ with") follow traditional grammar books, as FPSC keys do.

## 5. What is missing

1. **Civics and Ethics.** Not covered, because the candidate takes Islamic Studies.
2. **Figure-based reasoning.** Mirror images of shapes, rotations, paper folding and figure series cannot be shown in plain text. The 2027 paper had at least one such item. Use the non-verbal part of a reasoning book.
3. **Current affairs after 23 Nov 2026.** The file must be refreshed in Aug 2027; the checklist is in the current affairs file.
4. **Real past papers.** Not reproduced here. Get the official papers or one solved-papers book.
5. **Longer translation practice.** Only nine Urdu-English translation items across the pack (five in the Urdu practice set, one in each mock).
6. **Only nine comprehension passages** (five in the practice set, four in the mocks).
7. **A printable OMR sheet.** The mocks tell you to draw a 200-box grid.
8. **Flashcards.** The fact-sheet tables are ready to be turned into Anki cards, but no deck is included.
9. **A second opinion on the fact questions.** See section 2.

## 6. Before you rely on this pack in 2027

1. Read the CSS 2028 MPT notice and the admission certificate. Confirm duration, negative marking and the marks split. If anything differs, correct `00-How-to-Use-This-Pack.md` first.
2. Refresh the current affairs file and questions 206–250 of the GK practice set.
3. Do one real past paper before Mock 1 to calibrate difficulty.

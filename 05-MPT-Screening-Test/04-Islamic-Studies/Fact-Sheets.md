# Islamic Studies: Fact Sheets

20 marks. Target 13. The same facts feed the 20 MCQs of the Islamic Studies written paper.

**A note on figures.** Pakistani exam books repeat some figures that scholars count differently (for example the number of verses or Rukus of the Quran, or the exact date of an event). Where this happens, this sheet gives the figure normally used in FPSC keys and marks it "conventional". Dates in the Seerah are given by the commonly accepted account.

### Compilation and translation

- First Persian translation in the Subcontinent: Shah Waliullah (Fath-ur-Rahman). First Urdu translations: Shah Rafiuddin (literal) and Shah Abdul Qadir (idiomatic, Muzih-ul-Quran).

### Lessons the newer papers ask about

- **Charter of Madinah:** a written agreement that made Muslims, Jews and others one political community with shared defence and freedom of religion.
- **Hudaybiyyah:** hard terms accepted for peace turned into a "clear victory"; shows patience, keeping one's word and the value of negotiation.
- **Conquest of Makkah:** general amnesty; forgiveness in victory.
- **Farewell Sermon:** sanctity of life, property and honour; equality of all people; rights of women; end of interest and blood feuds; hold fast to the Quran and Sunnah.

### The five pillars

1. **Shahadah.**
2. **Salah.** Fard Rak'ats: Fajr 2, Zuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3, Isha 4 (17 in all). Friday prayer: 2 Fard. Funeral prayer: Fard Kifayah, 4 Takbirs. Special prayers: Istisqa (rain), Kusuf (solar eclipse), Khusuf (lunar eclipse), Khauf (fear), Tahajjud (late night), Tarawih (Ramadan nights). Wudu has 4 Fard acts (Al-Maidah 6). Tayammum replaces it when there is no water.
3. **Zakat.** 2.5% on savings above the Nisab held for a lunar year. Nisab: 7.5 tolas of gold or 52.5 tolas of silver. Made obligatory in 2 AH. Eight heads of spending (At-Tawbah 60). Ushr: one-tenth on rain-fed produce, one-twentieth on irrigated. Khums: one-fifth of spoils.
4. **Sawm.** Ramadan is the 9th month. Made obligatory in 2 AH. Kaffarah for deliberately breaking a fast: 60 consecutive fasts or feeding 60 poor people. I'tikaf in the last ten days. Sadaqat-ul-Fitr before the Eid prayer.
5. **Hajj.** Made obligatory in 9 AH. Performed 8–12/13 Zil-Hajj.

### Hajj in order

| Day | Rite |
|---|---|
| 8 Zil-Hajj (Yawm-ut-Tarwiyah) | Ihram; go to Mina |
| 9 Zil-Hajj (Yawm-e-Arafah) | Stay at Arafat (the chief pillar of Hajj); night at Muzdalifah |
| 10 Zil-Hajj (Yawm-un-Nahr) | Stoning of the large Jamrah, sacrifice, shaving, Tawaf-e-Ziyarat |
| 11–13 Zil-Hajj (Ayyam-e-Tashriq) | Stoning of the three Jamarat at Mina |
| Before leaving | Tawaf-e-Wida |

Terms: Miqat (boundary for Ihram; Zul-Hulaifah for Madinah, Yalamlam for those coming from Yemen and the south), Talbiyah, Tawaf (seven circuits; one circuit is a Shawt), Sa'i (seven rounds between Safa and Marwa), Ramal, Istilam (kissing or touching the Black Stone), Wuquf (staying). Types of Hajj: Ifrad (Hajj only), Qiran (Umrah and Hajj in one Ihram), Tamattu (Umrah, then a new Ihram for Hajj).

### Calendar

Months: Muharram, Safar, Rabi-ul-Awwal, Rabi-us-Sani, Jumada-al-Ula, Jumada-as-Saniya, Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal, Zil-Qa'dah, Zil-Hajj. Sacred months: Zil-Qa'dah, Zil-Hajj, Muharram, Rajab. Eid-ul-Fitr 1 Shawwal; Eid-ul-Adha 10 Zil-Hajj; Ashura 10 Muharram. The Hijri calendar was introduced by Hazrat Umar.

## 6. Fiqh, terms and concepts

### Sources and schools

Sources of law: **Quran, Sunnah, Ijma (consensus), Qiyas (analogy)**. Secondary methods: Ijtihad, Istihsan (juristic preference), Istislah or Masalih Mursala (public interest), Urf (custom). Taqlid is following a jurist's ruling.

| School | Founder |
|---|---|
| Hanafi | Imam Abu Hanifa (Numan bin Thabit, 80–150 AH, Kufa) |
| Maliki | Imam Malik bin Anas (Madinah) |
| Shafi'i | Imam Muhammad bin Idris al-Shafi'i; wrote Al-Risala |
| Hanbali | Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal (Baghdad) |
| Jafari | Imam Jafar Sadiq |

Imam Abu Yusuf, student of Abu Hanifa, wrote Kitab-ul-Kharaj and was the first chief justice.

### Grades of acts

Fard (obligatory; Fard Ayn on each person, Fard Kifayah on the community), Wajib, Sunnah, Mustahab (recommended), Mubah (permitted), Makruh (disliked), Haram (forbidden).

### Concepts in the 2027 style

- **Deen** is a complete way of life covering belief, worship, morals, social dealings, economy and government. "Religion" in the narrow sense means only private belief and ritual.
- **Ibadat** are acts of worship; **Mu'amalat** are dealings among people (trade, contracts, family matters).
- **Huquq Allah** and **Huquq-ul-Ibad:** duties to God and to people.
- The **family** is the basic unit of Islamic society.
- **Tolerance:** no compulsion in religion (Al-Baqarah 256); protection of the life, property and worship of non-Muslims.
- **Accountability:** office is a trust (Amanah); rulers answer to Allah and to the people.
- **Equality:** the only measure of honour is Taqwa (Al-Hujurat 13).
- **Ijtihad** needs deep knowledge of the Quran, Sunnah, Arabic and legal method. A new problem is handled by going back to the sources and their aims, not by opinion alone.
- **Islamic economics** allows private ownership as a trust, rejects interest, hoarding and gambling, and requires circulation of wealth through Zakat, inheritance and charity. It differs from capitalism (no unlimited freedom) and socialism (no abolition of private property).
- **Iqbal** called for rethinking Islamic thought in the light of modern knowledge while staying true to the Quran.
- **Muhammad Asad** argued that Muslims should not blindly imitate Western civilisation and should recover their own moral and social foundations.

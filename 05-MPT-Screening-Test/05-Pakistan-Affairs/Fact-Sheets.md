How the plans differ (asked in 2027):

- **Cripps Mission (1942):** dominion status after the war; provinces could opt out.
- **Wavell Plan (1945):** an Executive Council with equal caste-Hindu and Muslim members; failed over who names the Muslims.
- **Cabinet Mission (1946):** no partition; a weak union with three groups of provinces.
- **3rd June Plan (1947):** partition into two dominions; Punjab and Bengal divided; referendums in NWFP and Sylhet.

Leaders: Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah (born 25 Dec 1876, Karachi; died 11 Sep 1948). Allama Iqbal (born 9 Nov 1877, Sialkot; died 21 Apr 1938; Bang-e-Dra, Bal-e-Jibril, Zarb-e-Kalim, Asrar-e-Khudi, Payam-e-Mashriq, Javid Nama). Liaquat Ali Khan (finance minister in the Interim Government; "poor man's budget").

### Water

- Rivers: the Indus (longest; rises in Tibet) with eastern tributaries Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej (joining at Panjnad) and western tributaries Kabul, Swat, Kurram, Gomal.
- Dams: Tarbela (Indus), Mangla (Jhelum, Mirpur), Warsak (Kabul), Diamer-Bhasha and Dasu (Indus, under construction), Mohmand (Swat, under construction).
- Barrages: Sukkur (1932), Guddu, Kotri, Taunsa, Chashma, Jinnah.
- Lakes: Manchar (largest freshwater), Keenjhar, Saif-ul-Muluk, Satpara, Hanna, Attabad (formed by a landslide in 2010).

| Item | Fact |

Economic terms the newer papers use: policy rate (set by the State Bank's Monetary Policy Committee; changes work with a lag), fiscal deficit, current account, circular debt (unpaid bills building up through the power chain), direct and indirect taxes (heavy reliance on consumption taxes falls harder on the poor), tax-to-GDP ratio, remittances.

Sufi poets: Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai (Shah Jo Risalo), Sachal Sarmast, Bulleh Shah, Waris Shah (Heer), Sultan Bahu, Baba Farid, Khushal Khan Khattak, Rehman Baba, Mian Muhammad Bakhsh (Saif-ul-Muluk). Shrines: Data Ganj Bakhsh (Lahore), Bahauddin Zakariya and Shah Rukn-e-Alam (Multan), Lal Shahbaz Qalandar (Sehwan). Languages: Brahui is Dravidian; Burushaski (Hunza) is a language isolate; Balti, Shina and Khowar are spoken in the north.

# MPT Syllabus and Pattern

Checked on 23 Nov 2026. Anything marked **[verify]** was not confirmed from an FPSC document.

---

## 1. The legal basis

The MPT was added to the CSS Competitive Examination Rules, 2019 by **S.R.O. 1516(I)/2021 dated 27 Nov 2021**. The first MPT was held for CSS 2022. The notification is on the FPSC site as a scanned PDF; it was read for this pack by optical character recognition, so the wording below is a close paraphrase, not a quotation.

Rule 10A says:

- The Preliminary Test is held to shortlist candidates for the written examination.
- It is held every year, before the written examination, on a date the Commission decides.
- Its result is valid only for the competitive examination of the same year.
- The qualifying threshold is **33% marks**.
- There is no limit on the number of MPT attempts, subject to the age rule (Rule 6).
- Applications are online only. No hard copies of documents are needed for the MPT.
- The fee is Rs 250 (Appendix-II).
- Schedule, centres and results are published only on the FPSC website.
- If applicants are many, the Commission may hold the test in **batches**.
- A candidate must be eligible for the CSS examination of that year. A candidate found ineligible is rejected even after passing the MPT.
- Only candidates who pass the MPT may sit the written examination of the same year (Rule 11(1A)).

## 2. Subject-wise marks (Appendix-III of the Rules)

| No. | Subject as worded in the Rules | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Islamic studies. Non-Muslims may opt for either civics and ethics or Islamic studies. | 20 |
| 2 | Urdu, including its grammar usage and translation | 20 |
| 3 | English, including its vocabulary, grammar usage and comprehension | 50 |
| 4 | General abilities, including basic arithmetic (SSC level), algebra (SSC level) and geometry (SSC level), logical problem solving, analytical abilities and mental abilities | 60 |
| 5 | General knowledge, including everyday science, current affairs and Pakistan affairs | 50 |
| | **Total** | **200** |

The Rules do not divide the 60 marks of General Abilities or the 50 marks of General Knowledge any further. The split seen in real papers is in section 5 below.

## 3. Syllabus (Annexure-C and the FPSC "MPT Syllabi" page)

The FPSC syllabus sheet says the MPT subjects follow the existing CSS syllabus of the compulsory subjects, and gives separate syllabi only for Civics and Ethics and for Urdu.

**Urdu** (translated from the Urdu text of the sheet):

- Use of grammar (sarf-o-nahw): parts of speech, compound verbs, tenses, singular and plural, sentence structure, pairs of words, masculine and feminine, synonyms and antonyms.
- Translation.

Note that poets, books and literary history are **not** in the official Urdu syllabus. The 2027 paper still asked two questions on the history and status of the Urdu language, so a short list is included in `08-Urdu/`.

**Civics and Ethics** (for non-Muslim candidates who choose it):

- Civics: definition, nature and significance; the state and its elements; organs of the state; fundamental human rights; rights and obligations of non-Muslims under the 1973 Constitution; the UN's aims, main organs and agencies.
- Ethics: human values and the role of family, society and schools; integrity, impartiality, objectivity, dedication to public service, empathy, tolerance and compassion; emotional intelligence in administration.

This pack does not cover Civics and Ethics, because the candidate will take Islamic Studies.

**Other subjects**: for Islamic Studies, English, General Abilities and General Knowledge, read the syllabus of the matching compulsory CSS paper (Islamic Studies; English Precis and Composition; General Science and Ability; Current Affairs; Pakistan Affairs). The MPT tests these at MCQ depth.

## 5. What the real papers looked like

This analysis is based on candidates' recalled papers for the CSS 2026 MPT and the CSS 2027 MPT (Batch 1 of 10 Oct 2026) as collected by a coaching site, plus general reports for the earlier years. The question lists were read for topics only. No questions were copied into this pack. Recalled papers are not official and contain errors.

### Section order and question numbers

| Section | 2026 paper | 2027 paper |
|---|---|---|
| Islamic Studies | 1–20 | 1–20 |
| Urdu | 21–40 | 21–40 (some translation items) |
| English | 41–90 | 41–90 |
| General Abilities | 91–150 | 91–150 (quantitative about 91–116, reasoning about 117–150) |
| General Knowledge | 151–200 | 151–200 |

Inside General Knowledge, the rough count was: everyday science and technology 16–22, current affairs and international relations 11–14, Pakistan affairs 16–20.

### Islamic Studies (20)

- **2026 paper: recall of facts.** Which Surah contains a given ruling, counts (disjointed letters, mentions of a name), Hadith terms, names and titles of Companions, battles and their opponents, places, authors of Seerah books, Muslim scientists, and fiqh terms such as Zihar, the science of inheritance, and types of Ijma.
- **2027 paper: ideas and application.** The meaning and scope of Deen, the four sources of law, Hajj dates, the family as the basic unit of society, tolerance, Hisbah, accountability, Ijtihad and who is qualified for it, what Hudaybiyyah teaches about diplomacy, Islamic economics (Mudarabah, Murabaha, Maysir, property), Shura in administration, Mu'amalat, and the thought of Iqbal ("Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam") and Muhammad Asad ("Islam at the Crossroads"). Some were short scenarios. Questions were printed in English with an Urdu version.
- What repeats: Surah-and-topic matching, Hudaybiyyah, the Caliphs' main acts, Hadith terminology, fiqh terms, Muslim thinkers and their books.

### Urdu (20)

- Meanings of words and phrases, synonyms (mutaradif), antonyms (mutazad), plurals (jama), feminine forms, idioms (muhavare) and their meanings, choosing the correct sentence, tense and compound verbs, pairs of similar-sounding words used correctly.
- The 2027 paper added: the origin of the word "Urdu", the 2015 Supreme Court judgment on Urdu as the official language, a line of Ghalib with a phrase to explain, and translation between Urdu and English (also counted inside the English section).

### English (50)

- **Vocabulary (about 15–20):** synonyms and antonyms of hard words. The 2026 paper used rare words; the 2027 paper put the word in a short sentence, which helps.
- **Grammar and usage (about 10–15):** choose the correct sentence. Recurring points: dangling modifiers, parallel structure, subject-verb agreement, conditionals and the subjunctive, inversion, perfect tenses, "lest ... should", passive infinitives.
- **Prepositions (about 5):** fixed combinations such as "at variance with", "in lieu of", "anterior to".
- **Idioms (about 4–5):** some common, some rare. The 2026 paper had several very unusual ones that nobody could prepare for.
- **Analogies (about 4)** and **sentence completion with two blanks (about 4)**, including confusing pairs such as elicit/illicit and disinterested/uninterested.
- **Comprehension (5–10):** one or two passages, five questions each: main idea, meaning of a word in context, inference, title, what the author rejects or recommends.
- **Translation (up to 7 in 2027):** an Urdu sentence with four English renderings; the right one keeps the tense and the condition exactly.

### General Abilities (60)

- **Quantitative (about 26–40):** all at school level. Ratio and proportion, percentages, discount, profit and loss, averages, ages, simple and simultaneous equations, quadratics, indices, work and time (including "men leave, days change"), speed and trains, sets with two overlapping groups, angles of triangles and polygons, perimeter and area of circles and semicircles, volume of a cuboid in litres, cylinder surface area, simple probability, map scale, a sequence defined by a rule.
- **Reasoning (about 20–34):** blood relations, direction sense, seating in a row or circle, ordering by height or amount, scheduling with conditions, syllogisms, logical equivalents of "if ... then" statements, coding and decoding, number and letter series, odd one out, dictionary order, clock and bus-timetable puzzles, what conclusion follows from a short statement, and one data-sufficiency item.
- The level is easy to moderate. The danger is time, not difficulty.

### General Knowledge (50)

- **Everyday science (16–22):** 2026 was recall (transformers, gases in air, radiation, vitamins, printers, machine learning, renewable energy). 2027 added applied items: why seasons occur, why Pluto was reclassified, virus versus bacteria, hepatitis A prevention, arsenic in well water, landfill siting, iron absorption and tea, heat islands, glacial lake floods, plus computing terms (blockchain, HDMI, SaaS, LLM, GIS).
- **Current affairs and international relations (11–14):** organisations and their abbreviations, climate agreements (Kyoto, Paris), China's initiatives (BRI, AIIB), SCO and SAARC membership, nuclear agreements, strategic geography (Djibouti, Hormuz, Horn of Africa). The 2027 paper asked about events dated **June to August 2026**, two to four months before the test: a new defence law, the State Bank's policy rate decision, a prime-ministerial visit, a UN forum presidency.
- **Pakistan affairs (16–20):** constitutional history (1935 Act, 3rd June Plan, Bogra Formula, amendments), who appoints whom, the no-confidence motion, glaciers, islands, the Makran coast, Siachen, UN resolutions on Kashmir, economy and tax questions framed as short scenarios, and well-known firsts.

### What this means for preparation

1. English and General Abilities are stable from year to year and reward practice. Bank them.
2. Islamic Studies now needs understanding of concepts as well as facts.
3. Current affairs must be refreshed in the last three months. A file written in Nov 2026 cannot cover a test in Oct 2027.
4. Rare vocabulary and odd idioms cannot be fully prepared. Learn to rule out options and move on.

Reading the numbers: between 70% and 86% of those who sit the MPT pass it, and about a third of applicants do not turn up. The written examination is the real filter: 476 passed out of 17,351 who appeared in CSS 2026 (2.74%). The MPT is a hurdle to clear with a margin, not a place to spend months.

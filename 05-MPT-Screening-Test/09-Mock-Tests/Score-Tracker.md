# Mock Test Score Tracker

Four full-length mocks are in this folder. Each has 200 new questions in the official proportions (Islamic Studies 20, Urdu 20, English 50, General Abilities 60, General Knowledge 50). None repeats a question from the practice sets.

Take them in weeks 5 and 6 of the sprint, in one sitting each, with a hand-drawn answer grid and a black ballpoint.

## 3. Reading your result

| Total | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Below 80 | Too close to the pass mark | Redo English and General Abilities practice; these are the quickest marks |
| 80–110 | Safe pass, below target | Find the weakest section in the table and reread its fact sheet |
| 110–140 | On target | Work only from the error log |
| Above 140 | Comfortable | Keep the facts fresh; spend remaining time on current affairs |

A section score more than five marks below its target for two mocks in a row is a real weakness, not bad luck.

Sort the reasons after each mock:

- **Did not know:** add the fact to your revision list.
- **Misread:** slow down on the question stem; underline "not", "except", "opposite".
- **Slow:** redo five questions of that type against the clock.
- **Careless:** check the grid number every tenth question.

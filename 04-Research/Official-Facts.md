# CSS Competitive Examination — Official Facts

Researched on 23 Nov 2026 for a first attempt at CSS 2028.

**How to read the confidence tags**

- **Official** — read directly from a document on fpsc.gov.pk (rules, notices, press releases, annual report, bulletin).
- **Official (derived)** — calculated or counted from an FPSC document; FPSC does not state the figure itself.
- **News** — a newspaper or news site; FPSC original not opened.
- **Coaching-site** — a prep or education site.
- **Not confirmed** — could not be verified; best available source given.

**Note on FPSC links.** FPSC rebuilt its website around December 2025. Old links of the form `fpsc.gov.pk/assets/media/...` and `fpsc.gov.pk/sites/default/files/...` no longer open. Every FPSC link below is from the new site and opened on 23 Nov 2026.

## 3. Written examination

### Compulsory papers (600 marks)

| # | Paper | Marks | MCQs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | English Essay | 100 | None |
| 2 | English (Precis and Composition) | 100 | 20 |
| 3 | General Knowledge Paper I: General Science and Ability | 100 | 20 (General Science: 20 MCQ + 40 subjective; Ability: 40 subjective) |
| 4 | General Knowledge Paper II: Current Affairs | 100 | 20 |
| 5 | General Knowledge Paper III: Pakistan Affairs | 100 | 20 |
| 6 | Islamic Studies, or Comparative Study of Major Religions (non-Muslims may choose either) | 100 | 20 |

Source: [CE Rules 2019, Appendix-I](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/uploads/mpt-rules/1766991508848_Final_Approved_Rules_CE-2019_and_onwards.pdf) and [Syllabus for CE-2016 and onwards](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/uploads/syllabus/1767002683737_Syllabus-for-CE-2016-and-onwards.pdf), Notes 2–5. Tag: Official.

### Pass rules

| Rule | Detail | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compulsory subjects | At least 40% in each compulsory subject (Rule 12(1)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| General Knowledge counted together | "The three papers of general knowledge (GK-I, GK-II and GK-III) are to be passed collectively by scoring 120 marks out of 300" (Appendix-I, para 5). So Essay, Precis and Islamic Studies each need 40 out of 100; the three GK papers need 120 out of 300 combined. | CE Rules 2019, Appendix-I | Official |
| Optional subjects | At least 33% in each optional subject. | CE Rules 2019, Rule 12(1) | Official |
| Aggregate | At least 50% in the aggregate, that is 600 out of 1,200. | CE Rules 2019, Rule 12(1) | Official |
| Marks below the pass line | Marks under 40% in a compulsory subject or under 33% in an optional subject are not counted in the total (Rule 16(16)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| Viva voce | 300 marks; at least 100 needed. Psychological assessment carries no separate marks but is reflected in the viva marks (Rules 14, 15). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| Final merit | Written marks plus viva marks. Ties go to higher viva marks, then higher compulsory marks, then the older candidate (Rule 18). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| No grace marks, no re-checking | Only a recount, within one month, at Rs 500 per paper (Rule 16(28), (30)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |

### Paper format

| Fact | Detail | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | Each paper is 3 hours. Subjects of 200 marks have two papers of 100 marks each. | CE Rules 2019, Appendix-I para 2 | Official |
| MCQs | 20 MCQs in every compulsory paper except Essay, and in every optional paper except Pure Mathematics and Applied Mathematics. | Syllabus, Note-3; CE Rules Appendix-I para 3 | Official |
| MCQ answer sheet | MCQs are answered on a separate OMR sheet, collected after the allotted time (Rule 16(23)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| Calculators | Allowed only in Applied Mathematics, Pure Mathematics, Accountancy, Statistics, Business Administration, Physics and Chemistry. Not allowed in General Science and Ability (Rule 16(22)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| Ink | Blue or black only (Rule 16(27)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |
| Extra time | 15 minutes per hour for visually impaired candidates only (Rule 13(4)). | CE Rules 2019 | Official |

### Optional subjects (600 marks to be chosen)

| Group | Selection rule | Subjects (code, marks) |
|---|---|---|
| I | One subject of 200 marks | 11 Accountancy and Auditing (200); 12 Economics (200); 13 Computer Science (200); 14 Political Science (200); 15 International Relations (200) |
| II | One 200-mark subject from codes 16–17, or two 100-mark subjects from codes 18–21 | 16 Physics (200); 17 Chemistry (200); 18 Applied Mathematics (100); 19 Pure Mathematics (100); 20 Statistics (100); 21 Geology (100) |
| III | One subject of 100 marks | 22 Business Administration; 23 Public Administration; 24 Governance and Public Policies; 25 Town Planning and Urban Management |
| IV | One subject of 100 marks | 26 History of Pakistan and India; 27 Islamic History and Culture; 28 British History; 29 European History; 30 History of USA |
| V | One subject of 100 marks | 31 Gender Studies; 32 Environmental Sciences; 33 Agriculture and Forestry; 34 Botany; 35 Zoology; 36 English Literature; 37 Urdu Literature |
| VI | One subject of 100 marks | 38 Law; 39 Constitutional Law; 40 International Law; 41 Muslim Law and Jurisprudence; 42 Mercantile Law; 43 Criminology; 44 Philosophy |
| VII | One subject of 100 marks | 45 Journalism and Mass Communication; 46 Psychology; 47 Geography; 48 Sociology; 49 Anthropology; 50 Punjabi; 51 Sindhi; 52 Pashto; 53 Balochi; 54 Persian; 55 Arabic |

Source: CE Rules 2019, Appendix-I para 7, and Syllabus pages 1–3. Tag: Official.

**Selection rules that follow from the table**

- Total must be exactly 600 marks. Groups I–VII offer up to 900, so not every group is used. Tag: Official.
- At most one subject from each of Groups I, III, IV, V, VI and VII. That is the only "forbidden combination" rule in the current scheme: two subjects from the same group cannot be taken together (for example Political Science with International Relations, two history papers, or Law with Criminology). Tag: Official.
- In Group II, Physics and Chemistry cannot be taken together, and neither can be combined with the 100-mark subjects of that group. Tag: Official.
- The rule wording for each group is "can be opted". The rules do not say in so many words that Group I is mandatory. Coaching sites usually present it as required. Tag: Not confirmed.
- The standard is "that of an honour's degree of a university in Pakistan". Tag: Official.
- A wrong combination leads to rejection of candidature, and subjects cannot be changed after the closing date (Rule 11(13), (14)). Tag: Official.

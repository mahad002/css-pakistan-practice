## 2. MPT day checklist

**Bring:**
- [ ] Printed admission certificate
- [ ] Original CNIC
- [ ] Two **black** ballpoints (FPSC's general test instructions say black only)

**Leave at home:** phone, smartwatch, calculator, bags. Check the admission certificate for the exact list.

**On the day:**
- [ ] Arrive at least 45 minutes early. FPSC closes the doors 15 minutes before the start.
- [ ] Strategy: there is no negative marking (reported), so **answer every question**. Do your strongest sections first. One pass for sure answers, a second pass for the rest, and guess in the last 10 minutes. Keep an eye on bubble alignment.
- [ ] Pace: 200 minutes for 200 MCQs. Be at question 100 by minute 90.

## 3. Written exam checklist

**Bring every day:**
- [ ] Admission certificate, original CNIC
- [ ] Several blue/black pens of the same kind you practised with
- [ ] Pencil, ruler and eraser (for maps and diagrams)
- [ ] A watch without smart features, if the admission certificate allows it
- [ ] Water

No calculator for General Science & Ability. Calculators are allowed only in the listed optionals (Applied/Pure Maths, Statistics and a few others).

**The night before:**
- [ ] Read the one-page summary for tomorrow's paper.
- [ ] Re-read your top three essay or answer faults from the error log.
- [ ] Sleep 7+ hours.

**In the paper:**
- [ ] Do the MCQs first. They're on a separate OMR sheet that is collected after the set time.
- [ ] Time per question = (180 min − MCQ time) ÷ number of questions. Write it on the question paper and stick to it.
- [ ] Attempt every required question. A missing answer costs more than a weak one.
- [ ] Leave 10 minutes to number answers correctly and cross out rough work neatly.
- [ ] Remember: **missing any paper** after you've started the exam bars you from the rest, and the attempt is still used.

## 4. Choosing group preferences (on the written form)

- List all 12 groups in your real order of preference. Allocation follows merit, quota and that order. Preferences can be revised once, at the viva.
- Think about: posting (PAS and PSP are all over Pakistan; FSP is abroad), career speed, the kind of work, and your long-term plans. Decide by Oct 2027 and note the reasons here.

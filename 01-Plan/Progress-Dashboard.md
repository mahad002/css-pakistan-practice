## 6. Repair-priority rule

When choosing next week's repair block, take the first that applies:

1. A paper below its **pass line** on the latest timed attempt (one failed paper ends the attempt).
2. The **Essay**, if its last-four average is under 45.
3. A repeated content gap (the same topic missed twice).
4. A repeated method fault (structure, time, too short).
5. A paper above its pass line but below target.
6. A topic you just dislike.

Add no more than two repair blocks a week, and pay for them from the lowest-priority reading, never from the essay or the review.

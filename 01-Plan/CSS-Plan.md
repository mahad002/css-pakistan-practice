# CSS 2028 master plan

Last updated: 24 Nov 2026. The rules behind every number here are in `Exam-Rules.md`; the week-by-week work is in `Study-Schedule.md`.

## 1. Where you stand

Fill in your own position in `My-Situation.md` (a private file, not published). Check these five things first:

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Age 21–30 on 31 Dec of the year before the exam (+2 years for listed categories). Work out your last eligible exam year. |
| Attempts | 3 written attempts. Sitting the MPT does **not** use one; appearing in any written paper does. |
| Degree | Bachelor's, 2nd division or better, HEC-recognised, completed by the cut-off date. |
| Strengths and gaps | Which papers suit you (English? maths? reading?), and which you start from zero. |
| Constraints | Job, other exams, applications, and travel: every exam sitting is in Pakistan. |

## 2. Strategy in one paragraph

Pass every compulsory paper first, because one failed paper ends the attempt. Put the most time into the **Essay** (failed by 87–99% of candidates) and the **Précis** (39–92%), where strong English gives a real edge. Make **General Science & Ability** a high-scoring paper: its Ability half is school-level maths and reasoning. Cover Pakistan Affairs, Current Affairs and Islamic Studies thoroughly but efficiently, using the shared material: the same facts serve the MPT, three GK papers and the Essay. Choose optional subjects on evidence (FPSC scoring data), not reputation. Treat CSS 2028 as a serious first attempt with decision dates, not a blind commitment.

## 5. Decision dates

| Date | Decision | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **30 Apr 2027**: Checkpoint 1 | Are you on track? | Need: 6 essays written and marked; Essay pack method understood; first pass of Précis, GSA notes and Pakistan Affairs history done; optionals chosen. If more than 3 weeks behind, cut the optional reading first, never the essays. |
| **1 Aug 2027**: Go / no-go for CSS 2028 | Register for the MPT or not | **Go** if you will be in Pakistan or the Gulf in Feb 2028 and no funded admission or offer abroad clashes with it. **No-go** if you have a funded Fall 2028 admission abroad or a visa job offer: CSS can wait (eligible to 2032). Registering costs Rs 250 and uses no attempt, so if unsure, register. |
| **~Late Nov 2027**: Written application | Lock subjects and group preferences | Re-check optional-subject data (`Optional-Subjects.md`). Wrong combinations are rejected and subjects cannot change after the closing date. |
| **Mid Jan 2028**: Mock week | Sit, or save the attempt | Sit if your mock essay scores 45+ on the rubric in two of the last three essays **and** your mock compulsory papers clear 40 each. Otherwise consider not appearing: an attempt is used only by appearing in a written paper, so staying away saves it for CSS 2029. |

## 6. How the six compulsory papers share material

| Material | Used in |
|---|---|
| Dawn reading + Current Affairs backgrounders | Current Affairs, Pakistan Affairs (contemporary half), Essay evidence, MPT General Knowledge, interview |
| Pakistan Affairs history notes | Pakistan Affairs, MPT, Essay (Pakistan topics) |
| Islamic Studies notes and reference bank | Islamic Studies, MPT Islamic section, Essay (ethics and society topics) |
| Précis vocabulary and grammar | Précis, MPT English (50 marks), Essay language |
| GSA Ability section | GSA, MPT General Abilities (60 marks) |
| Political Science / Public Administration (if chosen) | Optionals plus Current Affairs, Pakistan Affairs and the Essay |

## 7. Logistics that can sink an attempt

All in `Exam-Day-and-Logistics.md`:

- MPT registration window is only about 2–3 weeks in August, and you can't change your centre later.
- Written application window is about 15 days in November, and the hard copy must reach FPSC by early December.
- Missing any written paper after sitting the first one means you can't sit the rest, and the attempt is still used.
- Book leave and flights for the MPT and the full written window (about 12 days in 2026) once dates are announced.

## 8. Fitting CSS around other commitments

- Put any exam or deadline that comes before 1 Feb 2027 first; CSS work starts on 1 Feb 2027.
- If you're also applying for jobs or degrees, write applications in Aug–Sep 2027, before the MPT sprint peaks.
- Job or university interviews take precedence on the day: move that day's CSS block, don't drop it.
- If your plans change (a job or admission abroad), stop at the next decision date. Your notes and packs stay valid for later attempts (re-check Current Affairs and the syllabus).

On the website, open **Downloads** for one-click download buttons.

## 3. Standard books that are not free

Buy these from a bookshop or as e-books; don't use pirated copies. Each pack's resources file (`08-Resources.md`, `10-Resources.md` or `12-Resources.md`) names the best one or two for that paper, with how to use them. The FPSC syllabus PDF above lists the official suggested readings for every subject.

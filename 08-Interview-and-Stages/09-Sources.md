## Access problems (they affect the grades)

- **fpsc.gov.pk** failed for every page with a certificate error. The CSS Rules were read from FPSC's `cp.fpsc.gov.pk` mirror. All other FPSC content came through `04-Research/Official-Facts.md` (opened on 23 Nov 2026), search summaries, or sites that quote FPSC.
- **reddit.com** is blocked to my tools, so no Reddit threads were used.
- **cssforum.com.pk** pages returned 404 or empty pages. Forum content is from search summaries only.
- **jworldtimes.com** old-site pages loaded as an empty shell. Topper interviews are from search summaries only.
- globalvillagespace.com and theprint.in did not resolve.

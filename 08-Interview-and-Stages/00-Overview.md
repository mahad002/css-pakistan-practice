# CSS: every stage from the MPT to the Civil Services Academy

Compiled 24 Nov 2026 for a first attempt at CSS 2028. Source numbers like [S1] point to `09-Sources.md`.

## How to read the labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Read in an FPSC or government document: the CSS Competitive Examination Rules 2019 [S1], FPSC notices, or `04-Research/Official-Facts.md` [S2]. |
| **FPSC (via secondary)** | FPSC wording, but I could only see it quoted on another site or in a search summary. The FPSC website would not load for me (certificate error), so I could not open the original. |
| **Reported** | First-hand accounts by people who sat the stage: officers, qualifiers, forum posters. |
| **Coaching / opinion** | Prep sites, columnists, or my own reasoning. Useful, but not evidence. |

## The path at a glance

| # | Stage | What happens | Scored? | Typical timing (CSS 2028 projection) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MPT** (screening test) | 200 MCQs, 200 marks, pass at 66 | Pass or fail only; marks not counted in merit | Late Sep to mid Oct 2027 |
| 2 | **Written exam** | 6 compulsory + 600 marks of optionals, 1,200 marks, 12 papers over about 12 days | Yes: 1,200 marks | Late Jan to mid Feb 2028 |
| 3 | **Written result** | About 2.5–3% of those who sit pass | — | Aug to Sep 2028 (6–7 months after the exam) |
| 4 | **Medical board** | Physical examination by an FPSC medical board | Pass, defer or unfit | About 1–2 months after the written result |
| 5 | **Psychological assessment** | Written psychological tests, group tasks, interview with a psychologist | No separate marks; feeds into the viva | Starts 3–8 weeks after the written result and runs in phases |
| 6 | **Viva voce** | Interview with an FPSC board; group preferences revised once here | Yes: 300 marks, at least 100 needed | Runs in phases over about 3–7 months, centre by centre |
| 7 | **Final result** | Written + viva marks give final merit | — | Roughly Apr to May 2029 |
| 8 | **Allocation** | Groups assigned by merit, quota, preference and suitability | — | With or soon after the final result |
| 9 | **Joining and CTP** | Common Training Programme at the Civil Services Academy, Walton, Lahore | Graded in training | Several months after allocation (timing has varied) |
| 10 | **Specialised training and probation** | Group academy, then first field posting at BS-17 | Final passing-out exam | About 2 years in total before confirmation |

## Stage by stage

### 1. MPT (MCQ-based Preliminary Test)

- **Verified [S2]:** 200 MCQs across Islamic Studies 20, Urdu 20, English 50, General Abilities 60, General Knowledge 50. Pass mark 33% (66/200). Valid only for the same year's exam. Fee Rs 250. Does not use one of your three written attempts. No hard copies needed at this stage.
- **Reported / news [S2]:** 200 minutes; no negative marking; OMR answer sheet (OMR not confirmed in an FPSC document).
- **Pass rates [S2, Official derived]:** 70% (CSS 2025), 85% (CSS 2026), about 91% (CSS 2027).
- **What can sink you:** Missing the online application window; picking a centre you cannot reach (centres cannot be changed); not downloading the admission certificate; not passing, which costs you a year because the pass does not carry over. Details in `01-MPT-Advice.md`.

### 2. Written examination

- **Verified [S1, S2]:** 12 papers of 3 hours each. You need 40% in Essay, Précis and Islamic Studies, 120/300 across the three GK papers combined, 33% in each optional, and 600/1,200 overall. Since CSS 2026, one compulsory paper a day, then optionals in two sessions a day.
- **Verified [S1]:** "A candidate who misses any paper may not sit the remaining papers" (Rule 11(15)). Sitting even one paper uses up an attempt.
- **What can sink you:** Essay above all (87–99% failed it in 2019–2023 [S2]); missing or walking out of a paper; a wrong optional combination (rejection of candidature); marks under 40%/33% in a paper, which are then not counted towards the total. Details in `02-Written-Exam-Advice.md`.

### 3. Written result

- **Verified [S2]:** Published about 6–7 months after the exam (CSS 2024 was a slow outlier at 14 months). Pass rates of written-exam sitters: 1.9–3.1% for 2019–2026.
- **What to do while waiting:** Start viva and psychological preparation quietly in about month 3 of the wait. Your form (the details you entered in the application) is what the board reads, so know it line by line. Prepare documents now (see below).

### 4. Medical examination

- **Verified [S1, Rule 13 and Annex-A]:** Every candidate who passes the written exam is examined by a medical board. Fees: Rs 100 by treasury challan plus Rs 1,400 paid at the board. Bring CNIC and admission certificate. If you are absent twice, your candidature is rejected and the attempt counts as used. You can appeal an "unfit" finding to FPSC within 14 days, with two certificates from doctors of standing.
- **Verified [S1]:** Minimum height 1,524 mm (5 ft). Vision rules are strictest for Police, then Railways, then other groups. Contact lenses do not count towards meeting the vision standard.
- **Verified (notice titles) [S5]:** FPSC issues separate schedules for "medically deferred and absent candidates", so deferral and re-examination are a normal route.
- **What can sink you:** Missing the medical twice; vision that falls below the Police standard (this can rule you out of PSP but not other groups); being deferred and not fixing the issue before re-examination. Details in `03-Medical-Board.md`.

### 5. Psychological assessment

- **Verified [S1, Rule 14]:** It consists of "written tests and group tasks". It has no separate marks, but the 300 viva marks "also reflect" it. Bring CNIC and two recent photographs. **If you are absent from the psychological assessment, you are not allowed to sit the viva.**
- **FPSC (via secondary) [S3, S9]:** Recent FPSC instructions describe one working day: written tests, one group discussion, a 2-minute extempore speech, a group problem, and a brief interview with a psychologist. Latecomers are not allowed in; centre and date changes are refused; FPSC does not pay travel or lodging.
- **Reported [S10, S11]:** Older first-hand accounts describe two days: timed ability and personality tests on day 1, then group discussion, speech, command task and psychologist interview on day 2.
- **What can sink you:** Arriving late or without documents; answers that contradict your form or each other; behaving in the group in a way that reads as either domineering or absent. Details in `04-Psychological-Assessment.md`.

### 6. Viva voce

- **Verified [S1, Rule 15]:** 300 marks; fewer than 100 means you fail the whole exam. The board has your academic and extra-curricular record in front of it. It weighs moral and ethical qualities, intelligence and alertness, vigour and strength of character, and leadership potential, and takes sports, debates and hobbies into account. Questions on Islam and Pakistan may be asked (non-Muslims are not asked about Islam). No re-evaluation of viva marks is allowed (Rule 16(29)).
- **Verified [S1, Rule 20]:** You may revise your group preferences once, at the viva, and that revision is final. The board can declare you "not suitable" for a particular group, and you will then not be considered for it.
- **Verified [S1, Rule 3(5)]:** Viva centres are Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Multan, Peshawar and Quetta.
- **Verified (schedule) [S2, S8]:** CSS 2026: psychological assessment began on 21 Sep 2026 in Islamabad. Viva phases are scheduled from October 2026 to early April 2027. (`Official-Facts.md` says "Oct–Dec 2026"; the revised schedule of 16 Oct 2026 runs well past that.)
- **What can sink you:** Under 100 marks; not producing original certificates (FPSC says the board will refuse to interview you [S3]); contradicting your form; bluffing; a panel judging you unsuitable for your top group. Details in `05-Viva-Voce.md` and `06-Interview-Question-Bank.md`.

### 7. Final result

- **Verified [S1, Rule 18]:** Merit = written marks + viva marks. Ties go to higher viva marks, then higher compulsory marks, then the older candidate.
- **Verified [S2]:** CSS 2025 final result came out 30 Apr 2026, about 14 months after the written exam; CSS 2024 about 19.5 months.

### 8. Allocation to a group

- **Verified [S1, Rules 20–21]:** Candidates are recommended in merit order up to the number of vacancies. You are only considered for groups you listed. FPSC "reserves the right to allocate a candidate against any Occupational Group and Service irrespective of his preferences, in the public interest". Unfilled seats carry over to the next exam. A waiting list is kept until the CTP starts, and non-joiners' seats go to the next candidate on merit.
- **Verified [S2]:** Quota: merit 7.5%, Punjab 50%, Sindh 19%, KP 11.5%, Balochistan 6%, ex-FATA 3%, AJK 2%, GB 1%. Women and minority seats come out of the provincial shares.
- **News [S32]:** CSS 2025 had 327 vacancies (169 open merit, 35 women, 123 minorities). Details in `07-Group-Preferences.md`.

### 9–10. Joining, CTP, specialised training, probation

- **Verified (CSA site) [S28]:** The CTP is run at the Walton campus in Lahore for all 12 groups together. Probationers spend about six months in Lahore. Training includes lectures, syndicate research (about 15 weeks), field attachments and study tours.
- **News [S25, S26, S27]:** The 53rd CTP opened on 22 Jul 2025 with 137 probationers and finished in Apr 2026. The 54th began with a virtual pre-phase, and its residential phase was due from end-March 2026. A 12-week military attachment was revived for the 52nd CTP in Apr 2025.
- **Then:** Each group goes on to its own specialised training (for example, the Foreign Service Academy's nine-month course [S31]), then its first BS-17 posting. Details in `08-After-Allocation.md`.

## Documents: start a folder now

These come from FPSC's psychological assessment and viva instructions as quoted by secondary sources [S3, S9], and from Rule 14 [S1]. Check the current FPSC list when your call letter arrives.

- [ ] Originals: Matric certificate, all degrees (BS transcript and degree, HEC attestation), self-domicile certificate, CNIC, admission certificate
- [ ] The signed online application form, with attested photocopies of every supporting document
- [ ] Educational profile proforma and undertaking (downloaded from FPSC and filled in)
- [ ] Two recent passport-size photographs (more is safer)
- [ ] Proof of extra-curricular activities, job experience and publications. FPSC asks for these. For example: an employment letter, university certificates, any project or open-source evidence.
- [ ] Final Preference Form (FPSC posts it with the viva schedules [S4])
- [ ] Stationery: blue ballpoint, lead pencil, sharpener, eraser (one older sheet asks for two 2B pencils [S9])

## The five things that sink most people after the written exam

1. **Missing a stage.** Absent from the psychological assessment means no viva (Verified). Absent twice from the medical means rejection (Verified). Late arrival means exclusion (FPSC via secondary).
2. **Missing original documents** on the day (FPSC via secondary).
3. **Inconsistency** between your form, your psychological answers and what you say to the board (Reported, repeatedly [S10, S19, S20]).
4. **A viva under 100/300** (Verified rule; how often it happens is not published).
5. **Badly ordered preferences.** You can revise them only once, at the viva (Verified), and you will never be given a group you left off the list.

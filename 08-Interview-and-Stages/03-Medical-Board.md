# Medical board

Compiled 24 Nov 2026. Source numbers point to `09-Sources.md`. Most of this file is **Verified** from the CSS Rules 2019, Rule 13, Appendix-II and Annex-A ("Instructions for physical examination") [S1]. First-hand accounts are thin and old, and are labelled.

## 1. Process

| Step | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Who is examined | Every candidate who passes the written exam, by "the medical board constituted for the purpose" (Rule 13(7)) | Verified [S1] |
| Timing | FPSC posts a medical schedule after the written result: for CSS 2024, schedules dated May–June 2025 (written result 28 Apr 2025); for CSS 2025, a schedule notice dated 22 Sep 2025 (written result 22 Aug 2025), plus a Phase 2 schedule in Jan 2026 | FPSC notice titles via search [S5] |
| Where | By centre; one recent FPSC schedule header lists FPSC Headquarters, Aga Khan Road, F-5/1, Islamabad, as a venue. Your call letter names the place | FPSC via secondary [S5] |
| Order with other stages | Old forum posts say medicals start about a month after the written result, with some candidates examined before the viva and others after | Reported (cssforum, 2008–2010) [S16] |
| What is checked | Height, weight, chest, eyesight (each eye, distance and near, colour, field), urine (passed in front of the examiner), hearing, speech, teeth, heart and lungs, abdomen, hernia, varicose veins, skin, limbs and joints, congenital defects, signs of chronic disease, vaccination, communicable disease | Verified, Annex-A [S1] |
| Tests candidates remember | Blood, urine, chest X-ray, height, weight, ear and eye checks | Reported (cssforum, 2008) [S16] |
| Outcome | Fit, unfit, or deferred (re-examined later) | Verified: deferral is in Appendix-II and FPSC posts separate schedules for "medically deferred and absent candidates" [S1, S5] |
| Absence | **Absent twice and your candidature is rejected automatically, no appeal, and the attempt counts as used** (Rule 13(7)) | Verified [S1] |
| Appointment | You cannot be appointed unless found fit, except for the special-candidate categories (Rule 13(1)–(2)) | Verified [S1] |

## 3. Standards that most often matter

### Height and weight (Verified, Annex-A para 4 [S1])

- Minimum height **1,524 mm (5 ft 0 in)** for any BS-17 post.
- Women: 1,473 mm (4 ft 10 in) for completely sedentary posts, if weight is at least 43.5 kg.
- Police: minimum chest measurements by height band, with at least 51 mm expansion (Annex-A para 5(b)).
- Weight is recorded. Correlation of age, height and chest is left to the board except for Police.

### Eyesight (Verified, Annex-A paras 9–20 [S1])

| Group | Standard (summary) |
|---|---|
| **Police (PSP)** | Strictest. 6/6 in each eye (Standard I), or 6/6 in the better eye with limits on the worse eye with and without glasses (Standard II). "No relaxation of the standards of vision will be allowed." |
| **Railways** | Better eye 6/12 and worse eye 6/19 without glasses; 6/6 and 6/9 with glasses. **Any defect of colour perception or field of vision means rejection.** Night-blindness test |
| **All other groups** | Without glasses 6/24 each eye (marked "temporarily lowered to 6/60"); with glasses 6/6 better eye and 6/12 worse eye. Colour blindness is *not* a reason for rejection, but it is recorded |
| **Everyone** | Squint or disease of the eyes or lids that could make you unfit leads to rejection. **Contact lenses do not count**: your vision must meet the standard with glasses (para 20) |

**Practical meaning (Derived):** Weak eyesight usually closes PSP, and colour blindness closes Railways, but neither closes the other groups. Factor this into your preference list (`07-Group-Preferences.md`).

### Other points (Verified, Annex-A para 22 [S1])

- Hearing good in each ear; speech without impediment (except special candidates).
- Teeth in good order; well-filled teeth count as sound. **PSP:** 10 sound teeth in the upper jaw opposing 10 in the lower, including two molars in each jaw.
- No hernia ("not ruptured"), hydrocele, severe varicocele, varicose veins or polyps. PSP candidates who have had a successful operation are accepted.
- Limbs, hands and feet well formed with free movement of all joints (except special candidates).
- No chronic skin disease, congenital malformation, signs of an impaired constitution, or communicable disease.
- Marks of vaccination. PSP candidates must show a medical officer's certificate of recent vaccination.

## 4. Common reasons for "unfit" or "deferred"

FPSC publishes no statistics on why candidates are declared unfit. The list below is what the standards and forum posts point to, **not** a measured ranking.

| Reason | Basis |
|---|---|
| Eyesight below the group standard (mostly affects PSP and Railways) | Verified standards [S1]; forum questions about eyesight eligibility are the most common medical threads [S16] |
| Overweight or underweight: candidates report being **deferred** and given time to lose or gain weight rather than rejected outright | Reported (cssforum: a CSS 2009 qualifier deferred for weight; other posters say time is given) [S16] |
| Blood pressure, abnormal urine results or other findings that need a repeat test | Derived from the tests performed; not separately documented |
| Hernia, hydrocele, varicocele, varicose veins (the rules say whether an operation would fix the condition) | Verified, Annex-A paras 22–23 [S1] |
| Colour blindness (Railways only) | Verified [S1] |
| Missing the examination twice | Verified [S1] |

## 5. If you are declared unfit

**Verified, Annex-A paras 24–25 [S1]:**

1. FPSC tells you, in writing, which defect or defects led to the finding.
2. You may appeal to FPSC, Islamabad, **within 14 days** of receiving the letter.
3. The appeal must include **two certificates from doctors of standing.** Each must say they know you were declared unfit by the Central Medical Board, and that they have read the physical fitness rules.
4. FPSC "do not bind themselves to constitute a second Medical Board"; it decides as it sees fit.
5. The appellate decision is final.

Rule 13(8) suggests getting examined by a government civil surgeon *before applying* "to prevent disappointment".

# After allocation: CSA, the Common Training Programme, specialised training, probation, first postings

Compiled 24 Nov 2026. Source numbers point to `09-Sources.md`.

## 1. From final result to joining

| Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Final result | FPSC announces those who qualified and recommends them to the Establishment Division by merit, group and quota | Verified [S1, S2] |
| Vacancies | Group-wise vacancies are notified with Establishment Division approval (Rule 19). For CSS 2025 they came out about 16 Apr 2026, just before the final result (30 Apr) | Verified rule [S1]; date from FPSC file name via search [S32] |
| Appointment | Made by the appointing authority, only after it is satisfied "after such enquiry as may be necessary" that you are suitable. Success "confers no right to appointment" (Rule 20(2)) | Verified [S1]. The "enquiry" is commonly understood to include character and antecedents verification; I did not find an FPSC document that details it |
| Waiting list | Kept until the CTP begins. Non-joiners reported before the CTP are replaced from the list; after it starts, their seats carry over (Rule 21) | Verified [S1] |
| Governing rules | Training and probation fall under the Occupational Groups and Services (Probation, Training and Seniority) Rules, 1990 | Verified that these rules apply (named in Rule 2 of the CSS Rules [S1]); **I could not read their text** |

**How long from final result to the CTP? It has varied (News, interpreted by me):**

- The 53rd CTP opened on 22 Jul 2025 with 137 probationers and ended in Apr 2026 with 136 [S26].
- The 54th CTP began with a virtual pre-phase in early 2026, with the residential programme due from end-March 2026 [S27].
- CSS 2024's final result was 15 Oct 2025 [S2]. If the 54th CTP is the CSS 2024 batch (likely but not confirmed), the gap was about 5 months.

**For CSS 2028 (projection, Opinion):** final result about Apr–May 2029, then a CTP start somewhere in mid-2029 to early 2030.

## 2. The Civil Services Academy (CSA)

| Fact | Source and status |
|---|---|
| Set up in 1948 as the Pakistan Administrative Services Academy; renamed the Civil Services Academy; now part of the National School of Public Policy | Wikipedia [S29] |
| **Walton campus** (Lahore) is used for the **Common Training Programme** of all 12 groups. **Mall Road campus** hosts the PAS Specialised Training Programme and mid-career courses | CSA site and Wikipedia [S28, S29] |
| Run by a Director General. The 53rd CTP's DG was Farhan Aziz Khawaja and its Director CTP was Dr Syed Shabbir Akbar Zaidi | APP, Jul 2025 [S26] |
| The academy's motto is given as "Khidmat-un-Naas" (service to the people) | APP [S26] |

## 3. The Common Training Programme (CTP)

### Length

| Source | Length |
|---|---|
| CSA's training methodology page: probationers are "stationed in Lahore for about six months" | Off [S28] |
| 53rd CTP: opened 22 Jul 2025 [S26], common phase reported to end Jan 2026, concluded Apr 2026 [S26] | News. About 6 months in Lahore plus attachments, about 9 months in all |
| Coaching sites: 6–9 or 9–10 months | Coach [S43] |

**Reading:** roughly six months of residential training in Lahore plus attachments and tours, for about nine months in total. Expect changes: the 54th CTP introduced reforms [S27].

### What happens

| Component | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Teaching | Lectures, class discussions, extension lectures by senior civil servants, seminars, panel discussions, case studies, workshops, presentations | CSA [S28] |
| Presentations | Public speaking, current-issue presentations reviewed by faculty, book reviews, synopses of extension lectures | CSA [S28] |
| **Syndicate research** | About 15 weeks in advisor-led groups, ending in a research paper presented to the whole batch and faculty | CSA [S28] |
| Field exercises | "Learning from Community" (household and school visits on health, education, sanitation); school attachment (mentoring government-school students); "Learning from Heritage" (Lahore heritage evaluation) | CSA [S28] |
| **Military attachment** | Revived in Apr 2025 for the 52nd CTP after a three-year gap: 12 weeks, 231 probationers from 12 groups (85 women), designed with the specialised training institutes and the military | Dawn, 13 Apr 2025 [S25] |
| Study tours and other attachments (53rd CTP) | Flood relief work, public-school attachments, community work with Akhuwat, an Islamabad study tour on federal governance, a military attachment, sessions at the Punjab Judicial Academy, interfaith visits, a NUST executive course, a parliamentary workshop | APP, Apr 2026 [S26]; Daily Times and PIPS headlines via search |
| **54th CTP reforms** | A pre-training phase (reported as virtual) including psychological profiling with the Health Services Academy and parental engagement; emphasis on social sciences, analytical reasoning, data-driven and evidence-based policy | The News, Feb and Apr 2026 [S27] |
| Evaluation | The orientation of the 53rd CTP covered "performance evaluation", sports, co-curricular activities and professional ethics. Grading criteria are not published on the pages I read | APP [S26]; CSA [S28] |

**Batch size:** 52nd CTP 231 probationers; 53rd 137, of whom 76 were women (women outnumbered men for the first time) [S25, S26].

## 5. Probation and the bond

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Officers are probationers from appointment until confirmation, governed by the 1990 Probation, Training and Seniority Rules | Verified that these rules apply [S1]; text not read |
| Probation lasts **two years**, and cannot end until the CTP, specialised training and a final passing-out examination are completed | Coach [S43]. **Not verified** in the rule text |
| Probationers sign a **bond with a surety** to repay training costs if they fail to complete training or resign during probation or within three years after | Coach [S43]. **Not verified** |
| Your training results affect seniority within the group | Commonly said; **not verified** |

**If you are working, especially abroad (Opinion):** if you are selected, plan a clean exit before the CTP. The waiting-list rule means a no-show is replaced, and the bond (if confirmed) makes leaving during training costly.

## 6. First postings (typical, by group)

All typical patterns from coaching sources and Wikipedia, not official job descriptions [S21, S30].

| Group | Typical first role |
|---|---|
| PAS | Assistant Commissioner (sub-division) |
| PSP | Assistant Superintendent of Police (sub-division) under training, then independent charge |
| FSP | Desk officer at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Islamabad; first posting abroad after a few years |
| IRS | Assistant Commissioner Inland Revenue at a regional tax office |
| Customs | Assistant Collector at a port, dry port or airport |
| PAAS | Assistant Accountant General / Audit Officer |
| OMG | Section Officer, federal secretariat |
| Information | Information Officer (PID or a ministry) |
| Commerce and Trade | Officer at the Ministry of Commerce or TDAP |
| MLCG | Cantonment Executive Officer (assistant level) |
| Postal | Assistant Superintendent / Divisional post office management |
| Railways | Assistant Traffic or Commercial Officer in a railway division |

All start in **BS-17** (Wikipedia [S30]; FPSC recommends for BS-17 posts, Rule 19 [S1]).

## 7. What to do now about this stage (Opinion)

- Nothing until the final result, except: keep your documents, degree attestation and CNIC current, and **avoid anything that could complicate a character or antecedents check** (pending cases, unpaid fines).
- When you reach the viva, ask officers of your preferred groups about their CTP and STP. It also gives you real material for group-preference questions.

# Mock tests: sequence, rules and decisions

## 1. Sequence

| When | Mock | Material | Decision it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every 4th Saturday, Phase 2 | Full past Essay paper (choose a topic in 10 minutes, write for 3 hours) | `03-Past-Papers/Essay/` | Essay trend |
| W25 | One full Précis past paper, timed | `03-Past-Papers/Precis/` | Précis readiness |
| MPT −4 to MPT −1 | **MPT Mocks 1–4** (200 MCQs, 200 minutes, OMR-style answer sheet) | `05-MPT-Screening-Test/` mock tests | MPT readiness |
| W44–W48 | Full timed compulsory papers (2024, 2025) | `03-Past-Papers/` | Weakest-paper repair |
| W51 | **Mock week**: all six compulsory papers, one a day | 2026 papers or unseen practice papers | **Sit or save** |

## 2. Rules for every mock

1. Same conditions as the exam: a 3-hour timer (200 minutes for the MPT), by hand, no notes, no phone, a desk.
2. MCQs first, on a separate sheet, as the OMR sheet works.
3. Write the time budget at the top before you start.
4. Never pause the clock. If interrupted, note it and carry on.
5. Mark the same day or the next with `Scorecards.md`, and take as long to review as you took to write.
6. Log every lost mark in `Error-Log.md`.

## 3. MPT mock decision rule

| Mocks 3–4 average | Action |
|---|---|
| 60%+ (120/200) | On track. Keep revising fact sheets lightly |
| 45–59% | Safe to pass (33% needed), but repair the two weakest sections before test day |
| Under 45% | Daily 100-MCQ sets on the weakest section plus fact-sheet revision until test day. Passing is still likely (91% passed in 2027), but treat it as a warning about the GK papers |

## 4. Written mock-week rule (W51)

**Sit CSS 2028** if all of these hold:
- Essay: 45+ on the rubric in two of your last three timed essays.
- Précis and Islamic Studies: 45+ in the mock.
- The three GK papers total 150+ in the mock (the pass line is 120).
- At least three optional subjects are at second-pass level.

**Consider saving the attempt** (don't appear, so no attempt is used) if two or more fail, especially the Essay. That means sitting CSS 2029 instead, and you're eligible until 2032. Decide calmly by 23 Jan, and write the reasons down.

# CSS error log

Log every mark you lose: wrong or guessed MCQs, weak answers, essay faults, grammar slips, and facts you didn't know. The point isn't to collect mistakes but to close them.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Typical example |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | Didn't know or recall the fact, rule or argument | Year of the Lahore Resolution; Article 140-A; a verse reference |
| **Question** | Answered the general subject, not the exact question | Wrote about "democracy" when the question asked about "local government's role in democracy" |
| **Structure** | Weak outline, no headings, ideas in a poor order, no conclusion | 20-mark answer as one long block |
| **Evidence** | Claim with no fact, data, example, reference or map | "Pakistan's economy is weak" with no figures |
| **Language** | Grammar, vocabulary, spelling, punctuation | Subject–verb agreement; "discuss about" |
| **Time** | Ran out of time, too short, or unfinished | Essay of 1,600 words; last answer not attempted |
| **Careless** | Misread, wrong bubble, wrong option | Marked B, meant C |

## Closure rule

An item is fixed only after two correct redos on different days:

1. **Redo 1:** 2–3 days after the miss (re-answer the MCQ, rewrite the paragraph or recall the fact).
2. **Redo 2:** 7–10 days later, mixed in with other work.

If either redo fails, write a better prevention rule and restart.

## Reading the tally

- **Mostly Content:** re-read the notes, then make Anki cards for the facts. Use the pack's quick-revision sheet.
- **Mostly Question:** before every answer, underline the command word (discuss, evaluate, critically examine) and every limiter (time, place, aspect). Write one sentence that answers the exact question before you outline.
- **Mostly Structure:** use the answer template in the pack's `00-How-to-Use-This-Pack.md` for every answer, even outlines.
- **Mostly Evidence:** learn 10 facts a week from the evidence bank and facts sheets. Each heading needs one fact or reference.
- **Mostly Language:** keep a personal list of your 10 most frequent errors and read it before every essay. Do PRE `03` drills.
- **Mostly Time:** measure your handwriting speed (words per minute). Write the time budget at the top of every paper, and practise finishing ugly over polishing half an answer.
- **Mostly Careless:** name the exact behaviour (for example, not reading the last option). "Be careful" is not a rule.

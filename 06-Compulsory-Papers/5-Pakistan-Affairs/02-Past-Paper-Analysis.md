# Past Paper Analysis — Pakistan Affairs, 2016 to 2026

Twelve papers: CSS 2016 to CSS 2026, plus the special CSS exam held in 2023. Each has seven long questions (Q2–Q8). Q1 is the 20-MCQ part.

The questions are described **in my own words**. For the exact wording, open the linked page or the FPSC past-papers page. The descriptions come from the question lists on cssprepforum.com and cssprep.com.pk, which reproduce the FPSC papers. I could not open fpsc.gov.pk from my tools, so spot-check two or three years against the official PDFs.

Tags: **H** = pre-1947 history and ideology. **P** = post-1947 political/constitutional history and the state. **E** = economy, resources, society. **F** = foreign relations and security.

Mixed questions are counted under their main tag, so the last three columns are approximate. The first column is firm.

What this shows:

- **2016 was the last history-heavy paper.** Since 2017 there have been at most two pre-1947 questions, usually one, and in three years none (2017, 2022 and 2025).
- The one history question is nearly always from four pools: a reformer (Sirhindi, Shah Waliullah), Aligarh, ideology/Iqbal/Jinnah, or 1937–47.
- Post-1947 history has returned as a steady source: 1947–58, the pacts, 1971, the Simla Agreement, Bhutto's nationalisation, the Indus Waters Treaty.
- Papers swing. 2019 was almost all economy and resources. 2024 was almost all politics and federalism. Prepare all four columns.

## 4. Patterns in how questions are framed

1. **A quotation or claim, then "comment" or "critically evaluate".** Example: population as a bomb (2019), Kashmir as unfinished business (2025). You must take a position on the claim.
2. **Two-part questions.** Causes and remedies; terms and assessment; role and way forward. Give each part its own headings.
3. **An old topic through a new lens.** Kashmir appeared in 2016 as a general question, in 2025 as "unfinished business", in 2026 as a test of the UN Security Council. Civil–military relations appeared in 2026 through youth and digital activism. Prepare topics, not fixed answers.
4. **The newest constitutional change gets a question.** The 27th Amendment passed on 13 Nov 2025 and was asked in Feb 2026. Expect the same for whatever passes in 2027.
5. **Repeats with a gap.** Sirhindi 2020 → 2024. Congress ministries 2016 → 2019. HEC 2017 → 2021. CPEC 2017 → 2021 → 2025. Energy four times in five years, then silence since 2021.

These are judgements, not promises. The paper will be set in late 2027, and events in 2027 will matter.

## 3. What I could not verify

### Sources I could not open

- **fpsc.gov.pk.** My tools could not connect to the site (certificate error). So:
  - The **syllabus** is confirmed from two independent secondary copies that agree with each other and with my own knowledge of the FPSC text, not from the official PDF.
  - The **past-paper questions** come from cssprepforum.com (2016–2025) and cssprep.com.pk (2026), which reproduce the FPSC papers. I paraphrased from their lists. I did not see the official PDFs. Spot-check a few years.
  - The **paper format** (20 MCQs for 20 marks in 30 minutes; four of seven questions, 20 marks each) is confirmed from the 2026 paper's instructions as reported, and is long-standing. **Negative marking** and the **Urdu option** are unconfirmed.
  - **Examiners' reports:** I found only press quotations of older reports (2013, 2014, 2016, 2018) and a 2026 government statement. I did not find Pakistan Affairs comments from reports after 2018. The "how answers are marked" section is therefore guidance, not an official scheme.
- **cssforum.com.pk** did not appear in my searches; I list it as a place to look, without relying on it.

### The pass-mark rule

**Resolved (24 Nov 2026):** FPSC CE Rules 2019, Appendix-I para 5, confirm the three General Knowledge papers are passed together at 120/300; Essay, Précis and Islamic Studies need 40 each; aggregate 50%. See `../../01-Plan/Exam-Rules.md`.

### Historical details where sources differ

These are stated in the notes with the commonly accepted figure and a caution:

- Muslim League seats in 1937 (104–110 of about 482) and 1946 (425–440 of 492); Congress's total in 1937.
- Dates of Shah Waliullah's stay in the Hijaz (1730–32 or 1731–33); founding dates of the Scientific Society (1863 or 1864) and of the Faraizi movement (1818–1820).
- Border lengths, the length of the Indus, and the number of Hijrat migrants.
- Casualties in 1971 (disputed; the notes say so and give the range).
- The attribution of "post-dated cheque" (Gandhi), "jugular vein" (Jinnah), "parting of the ways" (Jinnah) — all marked as reported or attributed.
- The words attributed to Indira Gandhi after 1971 — marked [verify]; do not quote.

### Figures given from memory

Energy (capacity payments of about Rs 2 trillion, fuel import bill, installed hydro, wind and Thar capacity), agriculture and industry shares, health indicators (stunting about 40%), HEC's university count, defence-import shares from China, and US aid totals for 2002–18 are approximate and from my general knowledge. They are of the right order. Replace them with Economic Survey figures when you update.

## 4. What is missing

1. **Official past-paper PDFs and MCQs from real papers.** The MCQ bank is original and based on the notes. It does not reproduce FPSC's own MCQs. Add the real ones from the past papers; they repeat.
2. **Maps and diagrams.** None are included. Practise four by hand: the Indus river system with dams and barrages; Kashmir with the LoC; CPEC routes; Pakistan with provinces and neighbours.
3. **Model answers for:** the nuclear programme, Palestine, the 27th Amendment, CPEC, non-traditional security, Pakistan's regional role, and early problems. Outlines are given at the end of the relevant notes files; full answers are not written.
4. **Sector-by-sector Economic Survey detail** (fiscal, monetary, trade, social sector tables).
5. **The provincial picture in depth:** separate backgrounders on Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the merged districts, Sindh, southern Punjab, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir are folded into the integration file, not written separately.
6. **Local government history** has only a short section.
7. **Women, minorities and human rights** as topics of their own.
8. **Islamic provisions of the constitution and Islamisation** are covered inside other files, not as a separate note.
9. **Urdu sources.** Everything here is from English-language sources.
10. **Everything after 23 November 2026.** The exam is in February 2028. Fifteen months of events — including the June 2027 Economic Survey and budget, any 28th Amendment, the end of the IMF programme, and whatever happens with India, Afghanistan, Iran and Gaza — must be added by you. Section 5 of `00-How-to-Use-This-Pack.md` has a routine for this.

## 5. Suggested checks before you rely on the pack

1. Open fpsc.gov.pk: confirm the syllabus wording, the paper's instruction box, and the pass rule.
2. Compare two past papers (say 2019 and 2024) with my paraphrases.
3. Read the 27th Amendment's text on the National Assembly website and correct History 10 where needed.
4. In June 2027, redo the figures in Contemporary 05, 14 and 20 and in the Quick Revision cards.
5. Search for "[verify]" across the folder (about two dozen flags) and resolve each as you study that file.

For Part I of the written paper (20 MCQs, 20 marks, 30 minutes) and for the Pakistan Affairs share of the MPT screening test.

**How to use it**

- Do one section at a time. Cover the answer key, mark your answers, then check.
- Target: 80% or better. Re-do wrong answers after a week.
- Facts are drawn from the notes in this pack. Current-affairs items (Section G) are correct to 23 November 2026 and will date; refresh them before the exam.
- Where sources give slightly different figures (seat counts, lengths), the question uses the commonly accepted one.
- Real papers also draw MCQs from very recent news and from general knowledge of Pakistan. Add your own questions each month.

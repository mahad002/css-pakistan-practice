# PART 1 — HISTORY

## H1. Ideology of Pakistan

- **Core line:** Muslims of the subcontinent were a nation with their own faith, culture and history, entitled to a state shaped by their own values, with equal rights for minorities.
- **Two-Nation Theory chain:** Sirhindi → Shah Waliullah → Sir Syed (1867 Urdu–Hindi) → separate electorates (1909) → Iqbal (1930) → Rahmat Ali (1933) → Jinnah (1940).
- **Iqbal:** khudi; Islam as a social order; against territorial nationalism; ijtihad through an assembly; Allahabad, 29 Dec 1930 (north-west state); letters to Jinnah 1937 (adds Bengal).
- **Jinnah:** Lahore, 22 Mar 1940 (two nations; international problem); 11 Aug 1947 (equal citizenship); Feb 1948 (no theocracy); State Bank, 1 July 1948 (Islamic social justice).
- **Debate:** Islamic state or state for Muslims? Jalal (bargaining counter) v. I.H. Qureshi, Sharif al Mujahid.
- **After 1947:** Objectives Resolution (12 Mar 1949) → Art. 2A (1985).

## H2. Muslim rule and decline

- **Arrival:** traders (Malabar), conquest (712), Sufis (Hujwiri, Chishti, Zakariya).
- **Dates:** 1192 Tarain; 1206 Sultanate; 1526 Panipat; Akbar 1556–1605; Aurangzeb d. 1707.
- **Legacy:** administration, Urdu, architecture, law, a distinct Indo-Muslim culture.
- **Decline:** weak successors, wars of succession, jagirdari crisis, Deccan wars, no navy, regional powers, Nadir Shah 1739, Abdali, the Company (Plassey 1757, Buxar 1764, Tipu 1799, Delhi 1803).

## H3. Reform movements

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sirhindi** (1564–1624) | Din-i-Ilahi; wahdat-ul-wujud | Letters to nobles; Naqshbandi network | *Maktubat* | Jailed at Gwalior 1619 |

- **Common thread:** each defended a separate Muslim identity. **Critical note:** Friedmann on Sirhindi's overstated political role.

## H4. Educational movements

- **Aligarh:** Sir Syed (1817–98); three aims — reconcile with the British, modern education, no agitational politics. *Asbab-i-Baghawat* 1859; Scientific Society 1863–64; MAO School 1875, College 1877; Educational Conference 1886; opposed Congress 1887. **Effect:** produced the League's founders. **Criticism:** loyalism, elitism, women ignored.
- **Deoband 1866** (Nanautavi, Gangohi): traditional, anti-British, public donations; majority with Congress (Madani), minority with the League (Thanvi, Usmani).
- **Nadwa 1894/1898** (Mungeri, Shibli): middle path.
- **Anjuman Himayat-i-Islam 1884**, Lahore; Islamia College 1892.
- **Sindh Madrassah 1885** (Hasan Ali Effendi); Jinnah's school.
- **Islamia College Peshawar 1913** (Sahibzada Abdul Qayyum).

## H5. 1857

- **Causes:** Doctrine of Lapse; Awadh 1856; land and economic ruin; missionaries and reforms; army grievances; cartridges.
- **Course:** Meerut 10 May; Delhi; Bahadur Shah; failed by 1858.
- **Results:** Crown rule (1858 Act); Queen's Proclamation; Muslims blamed → Aligarh and Deoband.

## H6. Freedom movement 1905–1929

- 1905 Bengal partitioned (Curzon); 1911 annulled.
- 1 Oct 1906 Simla Deputation (Aga Khan → Minto): separate electorates, weightage.
- 30 Dec 1906 Muslim League, Dhaka (Salimullah; Viqar-ul-Mulk in the chair).
- 1909 Minto–Morley: separate electorates.
- 1916 Lucknow Pact: Congress accepts separate electorates; one-third at centre; Muslims lose majorities in Punjab and Bengal.
- 1919 Montford: dyarchy. Rowlatt; Jallianwala Bagh.
- 1919–24 Khilafat: Ali brothers; non-cooperation; Hijrat; Chauri Chaura 1922; Caliphate abolished 3 Mar 1924.
- 1927 Delhi Proposals; Simon Commission. 1928 Nehru Report (joint electorates; one-quarter). 1929 Fourteen Points.

## H7. Freedom movement 1930–1940

- RTCs 1930–32; Communal Award 1932; *Now or Never* 1933.
- **1935 Act:** provincial autonomy; federation never formed; Sindh, Orissa; interim constitution of Pakistan to 1956.
- **1937:** Congress eight ministries; League about 109 of 482 Muslim seats; no coalition in UP.
- **Congress rule:** Bande Mataram, Wardha, Vidya Mandir, Hindi, mass contact; Pirpur and Sharif Reports; Day of Deliverance 22 Dec 1939.
- **Lahore Resolution, 23 Mar 1940:** Fazlul Haq moved; "independent states" in north-west and east; changed to one state at Delhi, April 1946.
- **Thesis:** Congress rule was the catalyst, not the origin.

## H8. Freedom movement 1940–1947

- 1940 August Offer → 1942 Cripps (opt-out clause) → Quit India → 1944 Gandhi–Jinnah → 1945 Wavell/Simla (who nominates Muslims?) → 1945–46 elections (30/30; about 428/492).
- **Cabinet Mission, 16 May 1946:** three-subject Union; groups A, B, C; League accepted 6 June; Nehru's statement 10 July; Direct Action 16 Aug.
- 20 Feb 1947 Attlee; **3 June Plan**: assemblies vote in Punjab and Bengal; referendums in NWFP and Sylhet; Boundary Commission; states choose.
- Independence Act 18 July; Radcliffe Award 17 Aug (Gurdaspur, Ferozepur, Calcutta).
- **Who caused partition?** Qureshi (nationhood); Jalal and Seervai (Congress chose it); Wolpert (British haste).

## H9. 1947–1958

- **Early problems:** refugees; assets (Rs 750 m; Rs 50 m unpaid); no ordnance factories; canal water (1 Apr 1948); states (Junagadh, Hyderabad, Kashmir); administration; language; deaths of Jinnah (1948) and Liaquat (1951).
- **Constitution delayed:** Islam's role; representation; federalism; language. Objectives Resolution 1949 → BPC 1950, 1952 → Bogra Formula 1953 → Assembly dissolved 24 Oct 1954 (Tamizuddin; necessity) → One Unit 1955 → 23 Mar 1956.
- **Factional politics:** seven PMs; League's decline; United Front 1954; bureaucrats in power; 7 Oct 1958.
- **Pacts:** 1954 MDAA and SEATO; 1955 Baghdad Pact. Why: India, aid, arms. Cost: USSR, Arab world, no help in 1965 and 1971.

## H10. Constitutions and amendments

## H11. Eras in one line each

- **Ayub (1958–69):** growth, IWT, 1962 Constitution, 1965 war, inequality.
- **Yahya (1969–71):** fair election, failed transfer, 1971.
- **Bhutto (1971–77):** Simla, 1973 Constitution, nationalisation, nuclear start, 1977 crisis.
- **Zia (1977–88):** Islamisation, Afghan jihad, 8th Amendment.
- **1988–99:** four governments dismissed; nuclear tests; Kargil.
- **Musharraf (1999–2008):** war on terror, growth, media, 2007 judicial crisis.
- **2008–18:** 18th Amendment, NFC, first transfers, APS and NAP, CPEC, Panama.
- **2018–22:** PTI; IMF; no-confidence April 2022.
- **2022–26:** floods; 9 May 2023; 2024 election; 26th and 27th Amendments; May 2025 conflict; KP governor's rule Oct 2026.

## H12. 1971

- **Causes:** language (1952); 1954 dismissal; parity; military rule; economic disparity; Six Points (1966); Agartala.
- **Trigger:** election 7 Dec 1970 (AL 160, PPP 81); postponement 1 Mar; action 25 Mar; India; 16 Dec.
- **Actors:** India (Mukti Bahini; treaty Aug 1971), USSR (vetoes), USA (tilt, no help), China (words).
- **Lessons:** honour mandates; real federalism; fair shares; no force for political problems.
- **Cite:** Hamoodur Rahman Commission; Rounaq Jahan.

## H13. Land and people

- 796,096 sq km; 241.49 m (2023); growth 2.55%; 64% under 30; Punjab about 53%.
- K2 8,611 m; Indus; Tarbela (Indus), Mangla (Jhelum).
- Resources: Thar coal, Sui gas, Reko Diq copper-gold, Khewra salt.
- Agriculture about 23% of GDP, 37% of jobs; industry about 19–20%; services about 58%.
- Literacy about 61–63%; 25 m children out of school; education 0.8% of GDP.

# PART 2 — CONTEMPORARY

## C1. Regional apparatus and Pakistan's role

- **Changes:** US exit from Afghanistan; US–China rivalry; May 2025 conflict; Afghan rupture; Iran war (28 Feb 2026); Saudi pact (17 Sep 2025); SCO expansion.
- **Roles:** mediator (Islamabad talks 11–12 Apr 2026); SCO chair 2026–27; UNSC member 2025–26; Board of Peace.
- **Limits:** economy, terrorism, two hostile borders.
- **Line:** influence abroad rests on stability at home.

## C2. Nuclear programme

- 1972 Multan; 1974 India's test; Kahuta 1976; tests 28 and 30 May 1998; NCA 2000; SPD; PNRA 2001; A.Q. Khan network 2004.
- **Doctrine:** credible minimum → full-spectrum deterrence; no NFU pledge.
- **Security:** NCA, SPD force, personnel reliability, export controls, IAEA safeguards on civil plants.
- **Concerns and replies:** proliferation (closed in 2004); terrorism (no breach); tactical weapons (centralised control); FMCT (stocks); NSG discrimination.
- **Now:** about 170 warheads (SIPRI 2026); 27th Amendment's National Strategic Command; lower threshold after May 2025.

## C3. SAARC, ECO, SCO

- **SAARC** 1985, Kathmandu, 8 members; last summit 2014; blocked by India–Pakistan; intra-regional trade about 5%.
- **ECO** 1985 (RCD 1964), Tehran, 10 members; ECOTA; blocked by Afghanistan and Iran sanctions.
- **SCO** 2001; Pakistan 2017; 10 members; security, energy, connectivity; **Pakistan chairs 2026–27**.

## C4. Civil–military relations

- **Causes:** security threat, weak parties, alliances, judicial validation, Afghan wars.
- **Terms:** overdeveloped state (Alavi); Milbus (Siddiqa); hybrid regime.
- **Now:** SIFC; five-year tenures; Field Marshal; CDF (27th Amendment); KP governor's rule.
- **Youth and digital space (2026):** wider debate, PECA 2025, polarisation.
- **Way forward:** performance, institutions (NSC), party reform, credible elections, gradual rebalancing.

## C5. Economy

- **FY26:** growth 3.7%; $452 bn; $1,901 per head; inflation about 6%; reserves $17 bn; remittances about $41 bn; poverty 28.9%; unemployment 7.1%.
- **Budget FY27:** Rs 18.77 tr; interest about Rs 8 tr; defence about Rs 3 tr; FBR Rs 15.26 tr.
- **IMF:** EFF $7 bn (Sep 2024, 37 months); third review May 2026.
- **Structural:** tax 10–11% of GDP; exports about 10% of GDP; investment 13–14%; circular debt; SOEs; elite capture (UNDP: about 6% of GDP); population.
- **Fix:** tax base, exports, energy, SOEs, NFC, human capital, charter of economy.

## C6. Non-traditional security; non-state actors

- **NTS:** terrorism, climate, water, food, health, cyber, population. Human security (UNDP 1994; Mahbub ul Haq). Securitisation (Buzan).
- **NSAs:** TTP, IS-K, BLA/BLF, sectarian groups.
- **2025:** deadliest year in a decade; GTI 2026 rank 1.
- **Response:** Zarb-i-Azb, NAP (2015), NACTA, Azm-i-Istehkam; gaps in the non-kinetic points.

## C7. Palestine

- 1917 Balfour → 1947 Res. 181 → 1948 Nakba → 1967 (Res. 242) → Oslo 1993 → 7 Oct 2023 → ceasefire 10 Oct 2025 → UNSC 2803 → Board of Peace.
- **Pakistan:** no recognition of Israel; two states on 1967 lines, Al-Quds capital; UNSC member; Board of Peace; no troops committed; conditions — peacekeeping only, Parliament decides.
- **Parallel:** Kashmir; UN's weakness.

## C8. National security

- Two fronts; lower threshold after May 2025; water as leverage; terrorism; economy as the core (NSP 2022).
- **Line:** the centre of gravity is internal.

## C9. Democracy and political system

- **Why weak:** late start; military rule; weak, dynastic parties; feudal and biradari ties; judicial validation; disputed elections; no local government.
- **Resilience:** 1973 Constitution; 18th Amendment; three completed parliaments.
- **Now:** coalition with two-thirds; Imran Khan in prison; KP emergency (11 Oct 2026); 28th Amendment talk.
- **Reforms:** Election Commission, local government, party democracy, charter of democracy.

## C10. War on terror; foreign policy

- **Phases with USA:** 2001–07 alliance → 2008–10 "do more" → 2011 breakdown → 2018 aid cut → Doha 2020 → 2021–24 neglect → 2025–26 reset.
- **Cost:** 80,000+ lives; $150 bn+. **Gain:** about $33 bn; debt relief.
- **Ideology v. interest:** the real flaw was a narrow, security-only definition of interest.

## C11. National integration

- **Causes:** centralisation; Punjab's weight; resources; language; force.
- **Tools:** Senate, CCI, NFC (57.5%; 82/10.3/5/2.7), 18th Amendment.
- **Now:** Balochistan (Jaffar Express Mar 2025; 31 Jan 2026); KP; canals (CCI Apr 2025); new provinces.
- **Fix:** local government, new NFC, dialogue, fair shares.

## C12. Water

- Under 1,000 cubic metres per head; 30 days' storage; 90%+ to agriculture.
- **IWT** 19 Sep 1960: east to India, west to Pakistan; no exit clause; **in abeyance since 23 Apr 2025**; Court of Arbitration 2025.
- **1991 Accord:** Punjab 55.94, Sindh 48.76, KP 5.78, Balochistan 3.87 MAF.
- Kalabagh; Bhasha, Dasu, Mohmand. Kabul River: no treaty.
- **Fix:** legal case abroad; storage, efficiency, telemetry, pricing at home.

## C13. National interest and sovereignty

- Interest: survival, security, economy, cohesion (Morgenthau).
- **Challenges to sovereignty:** strikes and raids (drones, Abbottabad, Salala, May 2025); IMF conditionality; water; proxies; loss of writ.
- **Line:** sovereignty rests on capacity and legitimacy.

## C14. Energy

- Shortage → surplus at unaffordable cost. Capacity 49,651 MW; circular debt about Rs 1.7–1.8 tr; capacity payments about Rs 2 tr a year; solar boom.
- **Causes:** take-or-pay contracts, losses, imported fuel, weak grid, governance.
- **Fix:** renegotiate, privatise distribution, raise demand, grid and storage, local and clean sources.

## C15. Neighbours

- **China:** 1963 border; KKH; CPEC 2015; largest arms supplier; irritants — security of Chinese, arrears.
- **Iran:** first to recognise Pakistan; pipeline stalled; Jan 2024 strikes; Pakistan mediating in 2026.
- **Afghanistan:** Durand Line; TTP; open conflict 2025–26.
- **Saudi Arabia:** remittances; defence agreement 2025.

## C16. India and Kashmir

- **Kashmir:** 1846 Amritsar; 26–27 Oct 1947; Res. 47 (21 Apr 1948); ceasefire 1 Jan 1949; Simla 1972 (LoC); 1989 uprising; four points 2004–07; **5 Aug 2019**.
- **2025:** Pahalgam 22 Apr; Sindoor 7 May; Bunyan-um-Marsoos 10 May; ceasefire 10 May.
- **UNSC weakness:** Chapter VI; veto; no enforcement.
- **Fix:** crisis mechanisms; Kashmiri-centred, legal, peaceful policy; economic strength.

## C17. Afghanistan

- 1979 invasion → Geneva 1988 → Taliban 1996 → 2001 → Doha 2020 → 15 Aug 2021.
- **Impact:** refugees, weapons, drugs, TTP, 80,000 dead.
- **2025–26:** Oct 2025 fighting; Doha ceasefire; "open war" 27 Feb 2026; Urumqi 1–7 Apr; clashes again Nov 2026.
- **Fix:** verifiable TTP deal via China, Qatar, Turkey; trade incentives; fix the merged districts.

## C18. Proxy wars

- Definition; why Pakistan is exposed; arenas — Afghan jihad, Saudi–Iran, India–Pakistan (Jadhav 2016), Balochistan.
- **Caveat:** external hands work on internal grievances.

## C19. Constitutional debates

- **Cases:** Tamizuddin 1955; Dosso 1958; Asma Jilani 1972; Nusrat Bhutto 1977; Zafar Ali Shah 2000; 2009 PCO judgment; Panama 2017; reserved seats 2024–25.
- **Debates:** judicial independence after the 26th and 27th; Arts. 232–234 in KP; Art. 239(4) and new provinces; military courts; PECA.

## C20. Social problems

- Poverty 28.9%; BISP. Education 0.8% of GDP; 25 m out of school. Health under 1% of GDP; 40% stunting; polio. Sanitation: untreated sewage.
- **Climate:** under 1% of emissions; floods 2010, 2022, 2025; Loss and Damage Fund; Art. 9A.
- **Media:** asset and liability; PECA 2025.

## C21. Federalism and the 18th Amendment

- **Gains:** autonomy, consensus, provincial innovation.
- **Problems:** federal fiscal squeeze, weak provincial taxes, no local government, standards.
- **Health and education:** laws and budgets up; outcomes poor; spending share down.
- **Position:** do not roll back; complete it — new NFC, conditional grants, CCI, local government.

## C22. CPEC

- 2015; Kashgar–Gwadar; about $25 bn invested; energy and roads done.
- Phase II: zones, agriculture, industry — slow. ML-1 moved towards ADB financing (2026).
- **Challenges:** security, arrears, trade imbalance, provincial grievances.
- **Line:** it changed infrastructure; reform decides whether it changes the economy.

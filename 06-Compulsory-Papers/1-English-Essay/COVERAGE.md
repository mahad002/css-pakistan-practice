## 4. What I could not verify

1. **FPSC primary documents.** I did not read the official syllabus PDF, any FPSC annual report, or any official past paper directly.
2. **Examiners' reports after CE-2019.** I found none for CE-2020 to CE-2025. The only later official signals are a 2023 newspaper column quoting the FPSC Annual Report 2020 and a 2025 briefing to a Senate committee.
3. **How the Essay is marked.** FPSC publishes no marking scheme. The rubric in `07-Practice-Plan.md` is my own construction from the official wording. The suggested link between a self-score of 60 and a real pass is a judgement.
4. **The 2023 papers.** Sources disagree on which set of ten topics was the February paper and which was the October Special paper. I followed a February 2023 news report. Confirm from fpsc.gov.pk.
5. **Exact wording of past topics.** The pack paraphrases. Small differences of wording between sites exist (for example "name of woman" and "name of a woman" in 2025).
6. **Essay pass rates for 2017, 2018 and 2022–2026.** Not found.
7. **What high scorers say.** I found coaching-site advice and one guide by a serving police officer, but no verifiable interview with a candidate who scored above 50 in the Essay. The CSS Forum page I tried returned no content. Advice about outline length, whether the outline counts in the word limit, and whether body headings are acceptable is therefore convention, not rule.
8. **Statistics marked [verify] or K in the Evidence Bank**, including: tax-to-GDP and debt-to-GDP ratios; per-person water availability, storage days and agriculture's share of water; circular debt; female labour-force participation; emigration numbers; learning poverty; the latest fertility rate; ND-GAIN rank; Global Terrorism Index 2026; WJP Rule of Law Index 2026; whether a Human Development Report 2026 exists; world poverty and internet figures; the outcome of COP31.
9. **Some facts inside the model essays** come from the K category (for example the 2023 reserve and inflation figures, the 2022 flood assessment, historical examples). I believe them to be accurate, but they were not re-checked online in this session. Before reusing any figure from an essay, find it in the Evidence Bank and check its status.
10. **Local government status in 2026**, province by province. The essay on local government is written so as not to depend on it.
11. **Books.** Titles and authors are from general knowledge. I did not check current editions or availability in Pakistan.

## 6. Limits you should keep in mind

- **The likely-themes list is a judgement.** It is based on frequency and current events. FPSC has surprised candidates before (February 2023).
- **The model essays are one writer's answers.** They show a method. They are not the only good way to answer, and memorising them would invite exactly the criticism examiners make.
- **Tone on sensitive subjects.** The pack mentions constitutional amendments, civil–military relations, accountability and conflicts in neutral terms. In the exam, keep the same calm, evidence-based tone.
- **Nothing here replaces writing.** The plan asks for about 45 full essays and 65 outlines. The pack is useful only in proportion to how much of that you do.

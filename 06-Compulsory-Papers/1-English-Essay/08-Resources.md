# Resources

A short list. The essay is a skill paper: after the first month, an hour of writing is worth more than an hour of reading about writing. Buy two or three books at most.

I have not been able to inspect current Pakistani editions or prices. Check availability before ordering.

## 1. Official sources (free)

| Source | How to use it |
|---|---|
| [FPSC past papers](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk) | Download every English Essay paper from 2016. Copy the exact wording of each topic into your practice folder. Read the instruction lines and the answer-book rules |
| FPSC annual reports and examiners' reports (same site) | Read the paragraph on English Essay in each available year. It is the only statement of what examiners dislike. Note which complaints repeat |
| [FPSC syllabus, CE-2016 onwards](https://fpsc.gov.pk/assets/media/2024-10-22-01-45-26-Syllabus_for_CE-2016_and_onwards.pdf) | Read the few lines on the Essay once. The reading list under English (Précis and Composition) is also useful for grammar |
| [Pakistan Economic Survey](https://www.finance.gov.pk) (each June) | Read the overview chapter and the highlights. Take ten figures a year into your evidence notes |

## 2. Books on writing and argument

| Book | How to use it |
|---|---|
| *They Say / I Say* by Gerald Graff and Cathy Birkenstein | The best short book on the moves of argument: stating a view, conceding, replying. Read in February 2027; use its sentence templates in your counter-argument paragraphs |
| *On Writing Well* by William Zinsser | Read the first seven chapters for clarity and plainness. It cures the inflated style that examiners dislike |
| *The Elements of Style* by Strunk and White | Eighty pages. Read once, then keep the list of rules beside you while editing |
| *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace* by Joseph M. Williams | Optional and harder. Use it if your sentences are correct but heavy |
| George Orwell, "Politics and the English Language" (essay, free online) | Twenty minutes. Read it before your first essay and again in December 2027 |

## 3. Books on grammar and usage

| Book | How to use it |
|---|---|
| *Practical English Usage* by Michael Swan | A reference, not a course. Look up each error you put in your error log |
| *English Grammar in Use* by Raymond Murphy | Do only the units on tenses, articles, prepositions and conditionals. Serves the Précis paper as well |
| *Exploring the World of English* by Saadat Ali Shah | The grammar book most CSS candidates use. Good for the Urdu-speaker's typical errors and for the Précis and Composition paper |
| *High School English Grammar and Composition* by Wren and Martin | Alternative to the above. Use the exercises on agreement and sentence correction |

## 4. Books for content and ideas

Read two or three, slowly, with a pen. Take arguments, not sentences.

| Book | How to use it |
|---|---|
| *Governing the Ungovernable* by Ishrat Husain | Institutional decline and reform in Pakistan. Feeds every governance and economy essay |
| *Pakistan: A Hard Country* by Anatol Lieven | How power works in Pakistani society. Good for depth and for a fair, non-partisan tone |
| *Issues in Pakistan's Economy* by S. Akbar Zaidi | The standard text. Read the chapters on taxation, agriculture and the IMF |
| *Why Nations Fail* by Daron Acemoglu and James Robinson | The institutions argument, with historical cases you can cite |
| *Development as Freedom* by Amartya Sen | Human development as the aim of growth. Useful for education, gender and poverty topics |
| *Pakistan: The Search for Stability*, edited by Maleeha Lodhi | Recent essays by specialists on economy, governance, foreign policy. Read as models of argument as well as for content |

## 5. Daily and weekly reading (free)

| Source | How to use it |
|---|---|
| [Dawn, editorials and opinion](https://www.dawn.com/opinion) | One editorial a day. Write its claim in one sentence and note one fact. This is your main source of Pakistan-specific argument and of formal English |
| [The Express Tribune, opinion](https://tribune.com.pk/opinion) | Second view on the same issues, twice a week |
| [Project Syndicate](https://www.project-syndicate.org) or the opinion pages of an international paper | One piece a week on the world economy or geopolitics, to see how a 900-word argument is built |
| [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org) | Reliable global figures with sources: poverty, population, energy, education. Use it to check any world statistic |
| [UNDP Human Development Reports](https://hdr.undp.org); [World Bank Pakistan](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/pakistan) | Country figures and short overviews. Read the Pakistan overview page twice a year |
| [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan) | Annual digital statistics for Pakistan |

## 6. CSS-specific sites

Use these for past papers and examiner feedback. Be careful with their model essays and "guess papers": examiners have complained in writing about memorised, ready-made material.

| Source | How to use it |
|---|---|
| [CSS Exam Desk: examiners' and experts' feedback](https://cssexamdesk.substack.com/p/css-english-essay-feedback-from-subject) | The most useful single page: extracts from examiners' reports for 2014–2018. Read it every three months |
| [CSS Exam Desk: essay guide by a serving officer](https://cssexamdesk.substack.com/p/english-essay-paper-for-the-css-exams) | Sensible, modest advice on topic choice and outline |
| [CSS Prep Forum: past essay papers](https://cssprepforum.com/css-pms-essays-topics/) | Topic lists by year. Ignore the course advertising |
| [CSS Forum](https://www.cssforum.com.pk), essay section | Long-running candidates' forum. Useful for past papers and for seeing other candidates' outlines; quality varies widely |
| [Purdue Online Writing Lab](https://owl.purdue.edu) | Free, reliable pages on thesis statements, paragraphing and grammar. Not CSS-specific, but sounder than most coaching notes |

## 7. What to skip

- Collections of "solved essays" to be memorised.
- Lists of "expected essays" sold before each exam.
- Books of "golden quotations". Use the short verified list in `05-Evidence-Bank.md`.
- Vocabulary lists of rare words. The GRE list you are already learning is more than enough; in the essay, use plain words.

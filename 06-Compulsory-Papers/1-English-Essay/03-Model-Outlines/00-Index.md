# Model Outlines: Index

31 outlines in seven files. Each has: the topic (written for this pack in exam style), a short decode, a thesis, a sectioned outline of 20–30 points, the evidence to carry into the hall, and a note on how the wording could change.

Facts are taken from `05-Evidence-Bank.md`, where each has its year and source. Check the bank before you rely on a number, and update both in October 2027.

## How to use an outline

1. Read the topic only. Cover the rest.
2. Decode it and write your own thesis and outline in 20 minutes.
3. Compare with the model. Note what you missed and what you did better.
4. A week later, write three body paragraphs from the model outline, by hand.
5. Do not memorise outlines word for word. In the exam the topic will be worded differently, and an outline that ignores the new wording fails on relevance.

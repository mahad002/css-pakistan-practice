# Evidence Bank

Compiled 23 November 2026. Recheck everything in October 2027 and again in January 2028 (see section 14).

## 1. How to use this file

- **Learn a few facts per theme, not all of them.** Eight to ten per theme is enough. One figure, correctly used and explained, is worth more than five dropped in a row.
- **Always attach the year and the source name** in the essay: "according to the Pakistan Economic Survey 2025-26…".
- **Round sensibly.** "About 26 million" is safer than "26.2 million" if you might misremember.
- **Never invent.** If you forget a number, write "roughly a third" or leave it out.

**Status codes**

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **V** | Checked in November 2026 against the linked page (usually a newspaper report of the official source, not the original document) |
| **K** | Well-established figure from the named source, written from general knowledge and not re-opened in this research. Likely right, but confirm once |
| **[verify]** | Commonly quoted, but I could not confirm the exact number or the latest value. Do not use until you have checked it |

Main links used for V items:

- Pakistan Economic Survey 2025-26 (released 11 June 2026): reports by [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/11/govt-unveils-economic-survey-2025-26/), [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/06/12/pakistan-economic-survey-2025-26-gdp-at-four-year-high-poverty-climbs-to-289percent) and [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2006877). The survey itself is at [finance.gov.pk](https://www.finance.gov.pk).

## 12. Original framing lines

These are written for this pack. Use them, adapt them, or better, write your own in the same manner. A framing line belongs at the end of a paragraph or in the conclusion.

**Governance and democracy**
1. Pakistan has held many elections and built few institutions.
2. A state that cannot collect its taxes will spend its life collecting loans.
3. Accountability that changes its target with every government is not accountability.
4. The law in Pakistan is not weak; it is selective, which is worse.
5. Democracy that stops at the provincial capital is a house without a ground floor.

**Economy**
6. Stability is not growth, and growth is not development.
7. The IMF is the fever, not the infection.
8. A country that buys what it does not earn will borrow what it cannot repay.
9. Remittances hide the weakness of the economy without curing it.

**People**
10. A school that does not teach is only a building.
11. Numbers are destiny only if nothing is done.
12. A nation that leaves its women at home plays with half a team.
13. The young are a dividend when they are employed and a danger when they are not.

**Climate and water**
14. Pakistan did not light the fire, but it lives in the house that is burning.
15. Rain becomes a disaster when it meets bad governance.
16. The river has not failed the country; the country has failed the river.

**Technology**
17. A machine can produce; only a person can mean.
18. Social media gave everyone a voice and no one an editor.
19. In the digital age a border also runs through a server room.

**World**
20. A state is as independent abroad as it is solvent at home.
21. Many poles need a roof; without one, the weather decides.
22. A ceasefire that leaves the grievance intact is a timetable for the next war.

**Abstract**
23. Fear can push people and need can pull them, but only hope makes them build.
24. Power does not change a person so much as remove the reasons to hide.
25. A full calendar is not a full life.

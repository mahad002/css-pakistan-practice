## Ten figures to know by heart

1. Population 241.49 million, growing 2.55% (Census 2023).
2. GDP growth 3.7%, per capita income $1,901 (FY26).
3. Public debt Rs86.7 trillion, 68% of GDP; interest takes over 42% of the budget (FY27).
4. Remittances $41.6bn; reserves about $21.5bn (late 2026).
5. Literacy 63%; 20–26 million children out of school.
6. HDI rank 168; gender gap 143rd of 145.
7. Climate Risk Index: 1st for 2022; 2022 floods cost over $30bn.
8. Global Terrorism Index 2026: 1st; 1,139 deaths in 2025.
9. Warheads: Pakistan 170, India 190 (SIPRI 2026).
10. Per capita water below 1,000 m³; agriculture uses over 90%.

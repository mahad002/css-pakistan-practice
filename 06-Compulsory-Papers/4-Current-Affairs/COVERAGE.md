## 5. Known internal inconsistencies to be aware of

1. **Islamabad Talks dates:** sources give 10–11 April and 11–12 April 2026. The pack uses 11–12 April.
2. **Collapse of the 2026 ceasefire:** 7 July or 8 July. The pack says "7–8 July" or "early July".
3. **Gaza first-phase date:** signed 9 October 2025; ceasefire in effect 10 October.
4. **Budget outlay:** Rs18.77 trillion (some reports say Rs18.8 trillion).
5. **Model answers** average about 1,130 words, a little under the 1,200–1,500 asked for. In the exam, add one fact per heading.

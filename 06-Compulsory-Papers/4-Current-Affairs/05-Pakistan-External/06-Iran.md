## 1. Background

- **Geography.** A 909 km border between Balochistan and Iran's Sistan-Baluchestan. Baloch people live on both sides.
- **History.** Iran was the first state to recognise Pakistan (1947). Both were in CENTO and the RCD (later ECO). Iran supported Pakistan in 1965 and 1971.
- **After 1979:** the Islamic Revolution, Saudi–Iranian rivalry and sectarian violence inside Pakistan; opposite sides in Afghanistan in the 1990s; US sanctions on Iran limiting trade.
- **Irritants:** cross-border militancy (Jaish al-Adl attacks in Iran; Baloch separatists Pakistan says shelter in Iran); smuggling of fuel; India's role at Chabahar port; the unbuilt gas pipeline.
- **January 2024:** Iran struck alleged Jaish al-Adl sites in Panjgur; Pakistan struck alleged BLA and BLF hideouts near Saravan two days later; ties were restored within a week. President Raisi visited Pakistan in April 2024.

## 3. Why Pakistan could mediate, and its limits (analysis)

**Assets:** trust with Tehran (a neighbour that condemned the strikes), access to Trump's circle, a defence pact with Saudi Arabia, no recognition of Israel, China's backing, and a direct interest in ending a war on its border.

**Limits:** no leverage over either side's core demands (enrichment, sanctions, control of Hormuz, Lebanon); Israel's distrust; the tension between mediator and Saudi treaty ally; a Defence Minister's social-media post on Israel that complicated the role; dependence on personalities.

**Costs of the war for Pakistan:** fuel at record prices, inflation back near 10–11%, missed growth target, risk to Gulf remittances, sectarian tension, refugee and smuggling pressure on Balochistan.

## 5. Way forward

1. Stay a mediator, not a party: keep the Islamabad process alive alongside Qatar and Oman.
2. Keep Saudi commitments strictly defensive and transparent to Tehran.
3. Border: joint patrols, intelligence-sharing, legal trade to replace smuggling.
4. Economy: barter and local-currency trade within sanctions limits; more border markets; electricity imports.
5. Pipeline: negotiate a deferral or an LNG/swap alternative to avoid penalties.
6. Protect sectarian harmony at home.
7. Prepare for outcomes: refugee contingency, strategic fuel reserves, diversified supply.

## 1. Background: a transactional cycle

| Period | Pattern |
|---|---|
| 1954–65 | Alliance (SEATO, CENTO), arms and aid; ended with the 1965 embargo |
| 1979–89 | Frontline state against the USSR; ended with the Pressler sanctions (1990) |
| 2001–11 | "War on terror" partner; Major Non-NATO Ally (2004); over $30bn in aid and reimbursements; ended in mistrust after Abbottabad and Salala (2011) |
| 2017–21 | Trump's first term: aid suspended (January 2018); then cooperation on the Doha deal |
| 2021–24 | Biden era: minimal contact; no leader-level call; Pakistan seen through Afghanistan and China; sanctions on missile-programme suppliers (2023–24) |

**The lesson scholars draw** (Dennis Kux, *Disenchanted Allies*; Husain Haqqani, *Magnificent Delusions*; Daniel Markey, *No Exit from Pakistan*): the relationship rises when the US needs Pakistan for a security task and falls when the task ends.

## 3. Current situation (November 2026)

- **Substance is thinner than the optics.** As of October 2026 the trade agreement was still unsigned and the minerals and crypto memoranda were "largely on paper" ([The Diplomat, Oct 2026](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/pakistans-trump-bet-meets-the-us-midterms); [CSIS, Apr 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan)).
- **Tariff law changed.** The US Supreme Court struck down the emergency-powers tariffs in February 2026. They were replaced by a 10% global surcharge under Section 122, itself under legal challenge. The exact rate now applied to Pakistani goods is **[verify]**.
- **Personal diplomacy.** The relationship rests on ties between Trump, Sharif and Munir. A Democratic Congress is expected to raise democracy, human rights and Imran Khan's imprisonment ([Foreign Affairs Forum, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.faf.ae/home/2026/11/10/the-gilded-bargain-trump-remade-americas-pakistan-ties-and-a-democratic-congress-is-about-to-test-them)).
- **Iran channel.** Pakistan's value as a go-between continues, though Qatar and Oman now carry much of the mediation.
- **The US is Pakistan's largest single export market** (about $5–6bn a year, mostly textiles) **[verify FY26]**.

## 4. Convergences and divergences

| Convergence | Divergence |
|---|---|
| Counter-terrorism against ISKP | China: CPEC, Gwadar, Chinese arms and technology |
| De-escalation with India and in the Gulf | India: US defence partnership with New Delhi continues despite trade friction |
| Critical minerals and energy | Iran: Pakistan condemned the US–Israel strikes and will not join military action |
| Stability of a nuclear state | Missile programme: US sanctions on entities linked to long-range missiles (December 2024) |
| IMF programme (US voting weight) | Democracy and rights concerns in Congress |
| Gaza stabilisation | Israel: Pakistan does not recognise it; public opinion is strongly pro-Palestinian |

## 5. Assessment (analysis)

1. The reset is real but **personalised and transactional**; it has few institutional roots (no strategic dialogue, modest trade, little aid).
2. Pakistan gained status, tariff advantage over India and a hearing in Washington at low cost.
3. Risks: over-dependence on one presidency; domestic backlash when US actions anger the public (the Iran war); being pressed to choose against China or Iran; a Congress hostile to the current political order in Pakistan.

## 6. Way forward

1. Institutionalise: sign the trade agreement; revive the Trade and Investment Framework Agreement and a strategic dialogue with working groups (energy, climate, IT, education).
2. Build ties with Congress, both parties, think tanks and the diaspora.
3. Make minerals real with bankable, transparent projects that also benefit Balochistan and KP.
4. Keep clear lines: no participation in wars against neighbours; no bases; CPEC protected.
5. Diversify exports to the US beyond textiles (IT services, surgical goods, sports goods).
6. Use the channel for regional peace: crisis management with India, Gulf de-escalation.
7. Address rights and democracy concerns on their merits at home, which removes the lever.

## 4. What weakens strategic stability (analysis)

1. **Conventional asymmetry:** India's defence budget is about eight to nine times Pakistan's, pushing Pakistan to rely on nuclear deterrence.
2. **Limited-war doctrines:** India's "Cold Start" and the post-2025 "new normal"; Pakistan's tactical weapons in reply. The **stability–instability paradox** in action.
3. **New technology:** ballistic missile defence (S-400), MIRVs, hypersonic and cruise missiles (BrahMos), drones, cyber, space-based surveillance and AI in targeting cut warning time and blur conventional and nuclear systems ("entanglement").
4. **Sea-based weapons** stretch command and control.
5. **No arms control:** only confidence-building measures exist.
6. **A three-sided chain:** China builds against the US, India against China, Pakistan against India.
7. **Discrimination in the regime:** the NSG waiver and US technology for India.
8. **Terrorism as a trigger:** an attack by a non-state group can start the ladder.
9. **Accidents:** India's accidental BrahMos launch into Pakistan (March 2022).

## 5. Existing confidence-building measures

- 1988 Agreement not to attack each other's nuclear installations (lists exchanged every 1 January).
- 1991 agreements on advance notice of military exercises and on airspace violations.
- 1999 Lahore Memorandum of Understanding.
- 2005 agreement on pre-notification of ballistic missile tests.
- 2007 agreement on reducing the risk from nuclear accidents.
- Military hotlines (DGMO).

## 6. Way forward

1. **A strategic restraint regime** (Pakistan's proposal since 1998): nuclear restraint, conventional balance, dispute settlement.
2. Extend test pre-notification to cruise and hypersonic missiles; add drones.
3. Nuclear risk-reduction centres and a political-level hotline.
4. A bilateral or regional test-ban commitment.
5. No attacks on command, control and early-warning assets; a no-cyber-attack pledge on nuclear systems.
6. Keep the arsenal safe and secure: continue IAEA cooperation, export controls and the Nuclear Regulatory Authority's work.
7. Criteria-based NSG membership for non-NPT states.
8. Avoid an open-ended arms race Pakistan cannot afford: keep deterrence "minimum".
9. Peaceful uses: more nuclear power (Chashma-5), medicine and agriculture.

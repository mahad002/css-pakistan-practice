**Assessment (analysis).** Pakistan used the seat to raise Kashmir by implication (peaceful settlement), to stay central on Palestine and to show itself as a responsible actor. The Council's paralysis on Gaza, Ukraine and Iran limited what any elected member could achieve.

## 2. OIC

- **Role:** founding member; second-largest Muslim state by population; the only one with nuclear weapons. Leads on Kashmir (Contact Group) and Islamophobia (15 March day, 2022; a UN special envoy post created in 2024–25).
- **2025–26:** OIC foreign ministers met in Istanbul (June 2025) during the Israel–Iran war; an Arab–Islamic summit in Doha (15 September 2025) followed Israel's strike on Qatar; emergency meetings in Riyadh in March 2026 during the Iran war, where Ishaq Dar's shuttle diplomacy was credited with easing Saudi–Iran tension ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_in_the_2026_Iran_war)).
- **Weaknesses:** rivalry among Saudi Arabia, Iran and Türkiye; members' differing ties with Israel (Abraham Accords) and the West; no collective security or trade mechanism; decisions are not binding. The Makkah defence alliance (2026) is a small-group answer to this gap.
- **Pakistan's agenda:** an OIC conflict-resolution mechanism, intra-OIC trade (now about 18–20% **[verify]**), science cooperation through COMSTECH, a unified voice on Palestine and Islamophobia.

## 3. SCO

- **Why it matters to Pakistan:** a platform with China, Russia, Iran and Central Asia; counter-terrorism cooperation (RATS); connectivity and energy; the one regional body where India and Pakistan both sit.
- **2024:** Islamabad hosted the heads-of-government meeting (October).
- **2025:** Tianjin summit; after the May conflict, India declined to sign a defence ministers' statement that omitted Pahalgam **[verify]**.
- **2026:** Bishkek summit (31 August–1 September): Bishkek Declaration; 28 documents; a security coordination centre in Bishkek; a statement criticising the war on Iran. India and Pakistan traded charges on terrorism. **Pakistan took over the chair and will host the Council of Heads of State in 2027** ([Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2626913/1); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/1/sco-slams-attacks-sanctions-on-iran-key-takeaways-from-bishkek-summit); [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/sco-leaders-adopt-bishkek-declaration-expand-cooperation-on-security-trade-technology/4043993)).
- **Chairmanship agenda (suggested):** an Afghanistan contact group; an SCO development bank; transit and digital-trade frameworks; climate and disaster cooperation; managing India's attendance in Islamabad.
- **Limits:** consensus rule; no economic integration; China–Russia weight; bilateral disputes are excluded.

## 4. SAARC

- Frozen since 2016 (file `03-Foundations/04`). South Asia is among the least integrated regions (intra-regional trade about 5%).
- **Causes:** India–Pakistan hostility; India's size (about 80% of regional GDP) and fears of dominance; preference for BIMSTEC; security-first borders.
- **Openings in 2026:** Bangladesh's new government and Nepal's and Sri Lanka's interest in revival; Pakistan's readiness to host the 19th summit. China has floated a new regional grouping with Pakistan and Bangladesh **[verify]**.
- **Pakistan's options:** push for functional cooperation without summits (health, disasters, climate, the SAARC Development Fund); sub-regional links with Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal and the Maldives.

## 5. ECO

- **Pakistan's stake:** routes to Türkiye, Iran and Central Asia; the Islamabad–Tehran–Istanbul rail corridor; energy trade.
- **Record:** the ECO Trade Agreement (2003) is still not fully applied; intra-regional trade is under 10%; "ECO Vision 2025" targets were missed.
- **2026 setbacks:** the Iran war and sanctions; the Pakistan–Afghanistan war.
- **Way forward:** revive ECOTA with tariff cuts, a payments and barter mechanism for sanctioned members, customs harmonisation, an ECO electricity market, tourism visas.

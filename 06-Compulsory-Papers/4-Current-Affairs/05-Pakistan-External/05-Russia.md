## 1. Background

| Period | Relationship |
|---|---|
| 1947–60s | Cold: Pakistan chose the US bloc; the U-2 incident (1960); Soviet vetoes for India on Kashmir |
| 1965–66 | Brief warmth: the USSR mediated the Tashkent Declaration |
| 1970s | The USSR backed India in 1971 (Indo-Soviet Treaty) but built Pakistan Steel Mills in Karachi (1973–85) |
| 1979–89 | Open hostility: Pakistan was the base of the Afghan resistance |
| 1990s | Drift |
| 2011– | Thaw: Russia lifted its arms embargo (2014), sold Mi-35 helicopters, began "Druzhba" joint exercises (2016), supported Pakistan's SCO membership (2017) |
| 2022 | Prime Minister Imran Khan was in Moscow on 24 February, the day of the invasion. Pakistan abstained on UN General Assembly votes condemning Russia. |
| 2023 | First cargo of discounted Russian crude reached Karachi (June) |

**Why the thaw:** India moved towards the US; Russia wanted partners after Western sanctions and a say in Afghanistan; Pakistan wanted energy, arms options and a second great-power friend besides China.

## 3. Limits (analysis)

1. **India first:** Russia's defence and energy ties with India are many times larger. Moscow will not risk them.
2. **Sanctions and payments:** banking channels, shipping insurance and US secondary sanctions make oil deals hard. Pakistan's refineries are not designed for Urals crude in volume.
3. **Thin economic base:** small trade, few complementarities beyond energy and grain.
4. **Pakistan's ties with the US** restrict arms purchases.
5. **Afghanistan:** Moscow engages the Taliban on its own terms.

## 4. Way forward

1. Energy: a government-to-government oil framework with a workable payment mechanism (yuan, barter); LNG; revive the gas pipeline on a build-own-operate model.
2. Connectivity: North–South corridor through Iran and Central Asia; rail freight.
3. Trade: a preferential arrangement with the Eurasian Economic Union; exports of textiles, rice, citrus, sports and surgical goods; banking links.
4. Industry: deliver the steel-mill project.
5. Security: counter-terrorism exercises; coordination on ISKP and Afghanistan through the Moscow Format.
6. Diplomacy: keep neutrality on Ukraine; use the SCO chair; seek BRICS partner status as a first step.
7. Education, science and space cooperation.

## 3. Patterns worth knowing (analysis)

1. **The paper follows the news with a lag of 3 to 14 months.** Papers are set in the autumn before the exam. The CSS-2026 paper (February 2026) asked about COP30 and the Gaza resolution, both from November 2025. So events up to about **November–December 2027** can appear in CSS 2028.
2. **Since 2025 the paper has turned inward and thematic.** The 2025 and 2026 papers carried three and four Pakistan-focused questions and broad "big idea" questions (ethnicity, brain drain, natural resources, civic space). Expect questions that need an argument built from several of this pack's files.
3. **Question style has changed from "discuss X" to "critically evaluate and recommend".** Nearly every 2025–26 question asks for causes plus remedies or a policy role for Pakistan.
4. **Compound questions.** The 2026 Q2 joined five problems in one question. Practise answers that connect topics instead of treating them one by one.
5. **Short notes return every second year** (2022, 2024, 2026). On that pattern 2028 would have them too. This is a pattern, not a rule.
6. **Evergreen topics rotate on a 2–3 year cycle.** New provinces (2021, 2024); CPEC (2016, 2018, 2023); Kashmir (2020, 2021, 2025); non-proliferation (2022, 2025); economy (2016, 2023, 2025).
7. **The MCQs are mostly static general knowledge with a few recent events**: organisations, headquarters, treaties, authors of well-known books, leaders, capitals, straits, and firsts.

# Keeping the Pack Current Until February 2028

> **As of:** 23 November 2026. This pack is a snapshot. By exam day it will be about 15 months old. The paper is usually set in the autumn before the exam, so events up to roughly **November–December 2027** can appear.
> **Goal of this file:** a routine you can keep while working full-time: **20 minutes a day, 1 hour a week, 2 hours a month.**

---

## 1. The daily 20 minutes

| Minutes | Task | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | **Dawn front page and national pages** (dawn.com or the e-paper) | Read headlines; open at most three stories that touch a backgrounder topic. Skip crime, sport, showbiz and routine political statements. |
| 8–15 | **One Dawn editorial and one op-ed** | Editorials give a balanced position in 400 words. Good regular op-ed writers for this paper: Maleeha Lodhi (foreign policy), Zahid Hussain (security, politics), Khurram Husain and Shahid Kardar (economy), Aizaz Ahmad Chaudhry and Touqir Hussain (diplomacy), Ali Tauqeer Sheikh (climate), Muhammad Amir Rana (terrorism), Huma Yusuf, Arifa Noor (politics). Note the argument, not the wording. |
| 15–20 | **Log it** | Add one or two entries to your update log (template below). If nothing matters today, write nothing. |

**Rules**
- Read at a fixed time (breakfast or commute). Use the Dawn app's "Editorial" and "Opinion" tabs.
- Do not take long notes. One event = one line with date, fact, source and the file it belongs to.
- If a day is missed, do not catch up. The weekly hour covers it.

## 2. The weekly hour (pick one fixed slot, e.g. Sunday morning)

| Minutes | Task |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | **World week in review.** Read one weekly summary: the BBC or Al Jazeera "week in pictures/news" pages, or The Economist's "The world this week" (two pages: politics and business). |
| 15–30 | **One long explainer** on the week's biggest event: Al Jazeera explainers, Reuters explainers, Dawn Prism, Arab News Pakistan or The Diplomat (South Asia section). |
| 30–45 | **Move log entries into the backgrounders.** Open the relevant files and add dated bullets under "Current situation" (see section 5). Fix any **[verify]** tag you can now settle. |
| 45–55 | **Numbers.** Check the State Bank's weekly reserves figure (every Thursday), and note anything new for `08-Facts-and-Figures-Sheet.md`. |
| 55–60 | **Five MCQs.** Write five MCQs from the week's news into the "My additions" section of `11-MCQ-Bank.md`. |

## 5. Templates

### 5.1 Update log (keep one file: `My-Update-Log.md` in this folder)

```
| Date | What happened (one line, with number if any) | Source | File to update | Done? |
|------|-----------------------------------------------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 2027-03-14 | IMF Board completed 5th review; $1.0bn released | Dawn | 04-Domestic/07 | ☐ |
```

### 5.2 Adding an update to a backgrounder

At the end of the "Current situation" section add a block like this. Never delete the old text; strike it through if it is wrong, so you can see how the story moved.

```
### Updates since November 2026
- **[2027-03-14]** IMF Board completed the fifth review; $1.0bn released (Dawn).
  *So what:* programme on track; next review is the last before it ends.
- **[2027-06-10]** Economic Survey 2026-27: growth X%, inflation Y% (MoF).
  *So what:* replaces the FY26 figures in section 2 and in file 08.
```

The "*So what*" line is the important part. It forces you to turn news into analysis, which is what earns marks.

## 8. Keeping it honest

1. **Date everything.** Write the date and source beside every new fact.
2. **Two sources for anything surprising.** Much of 2026's news was fast-moving and disputed (casualty counts, who struck what, who mediated).
3. **Separate fact from opinion** in your notes: use "F:" and "A:" (analysis) prefixes if it helps.
4. **Present both sides** on domestic politics, India and the Middle East. Note each side's claim and its source.
5. **Clear the [verify] tags.** `COVERAGE.md` lists them. Aim to settle five a month.
6. **Do not chase everything.** If an event does not fit one of the pack's files, it is unlikely to be a 20-mark question. It may be an MCQ: add it there.

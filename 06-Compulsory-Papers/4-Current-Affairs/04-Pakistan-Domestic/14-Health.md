## 1. Background

- **System.** Health is provincial since the 18th Amendment. Three tiers: primary (basic health units, rural health centres, Lady Health Workers), secondary (tehsil and district hospitals), tertiary (teaching hospitals). The private sector provides most out-patient care.
- **Financing.** Low public spending; households pay more than half of all health costs out of pocket **[verify latest National Health Accounts]**.
- **Programmes.** Lady Health Workers (since 1994); Expanded Programme on Immunisation; Sehat Sahulat (health insurance cards, widest in KP and Punjab); the polio programme.
- **Covid-19.** Pakistan's "smart lockdowns" and the NCOC, a civil–military coordination centre, were praised internationally. Recorded deaths were about 30,000.

## 3. Challenges

1. **Under-funding** and spending skewed to big-city hospitals over primary care.
2. **Double burden of disease:** infections and child malnutrition together with diabetes, heart disease and cancer.
3. **Population growth** outpaces services (file `12`).
4. **Workforce:** doctors concentrate in cities and emigrate; too few nurses and midwives (the ratio of nurses to doctors is inverted).
5. **Quality and regulation:** unregulated private clinics, quackery, counterfeit medicines, overuse of antibiotics, unsafe injections.
6. **Water, sanitation and nutrition:** the causes of much disease lie outside hospitals.
7. **Governance:** fragmented vertical programmes; weak data; politicised postings.
8. **Insecurity and mistrust** hindering polio eradication; cross-border virus movement with Afghanistan, made harder by mass returns and conflict.
9. **Climate:** heat, floods and smog raise disease and damage facilities.
10. **Mental health** is neglected.

## 4. Way forward

1. Raise public health spending towards 3% of GDP; protect primary-care budgets.
2. Universal health coverage through an essential package at primary level, with insurance for hospital care.
3. Expand and pay Lady Health Workers and community midwives; invest in nursing.
4. Integrate family planning, nutrition and immunisation at the same door.
5. Finish polio eradication: synchronised campaigns with Afghanistan, community trust, security.
6. Regulate: provincial healthcare commissions, drug quality (DRAP), infection control.
7. Clean water and sanitation as health policy.
8. Disease surveillance and pandemic preparedness (keep the NCOC model on standby; a national public health institute).
9. Retain workers: rural incentives, career paths.

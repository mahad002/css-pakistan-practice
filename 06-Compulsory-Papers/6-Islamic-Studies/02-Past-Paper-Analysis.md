### Single most repeated question types

1. The Prophet (PBUH) as a messenger of peace (in several wordings).
2. Extremism or terrorism in Pakistan: causes and Islamic remedies.
3. Human dignity and human rights (Farewell Sermon, UDHR).
4. Accountability of rulers and officials; duties of civil servants.
5. Ijtihad (four times) and Ijma (twice).
6. Self-purification (three times, plus Sufism once).
7. Status and rights of women (inheritance, daughters, feminism).
8. Military strategy of the Prophet (PBUH).
9. Islamic economic system, zakat and poverty.
10. One belief per year in rotation: Tawhid, Prophethood, Akhirah.

## Trends

1. **From description to application.** 2016–2018 asked "describe" and "write a note". 2023–2026 ask how a teaching applies to Pakistan's accountability system, extremism, sectarianism, the family, or the West.
2. **Pakistan-specific wording** has become common (2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026).
3. **Overlap with other papers.** The 2026 Two-Nation Theory question and the UDHR question need Pakistan Affairs and Current Affairs knowledge.
4. **Comparison questions** are rising: Islam and the UDHR, Islamic and Western civilization, Islam and other religions on rights.
5. **Short-note questions** (two 10-mark notes) appeared in 2021, 2023 and 2024. They reward wide coverage.
6. **Unusual questions** appear about once a year (Sufism 2021; fard kifayah and class-based lifestyle 2025; Two-Nation Theory 2026). Wide reading of the notes protects against these.
7. **Worship questions have faded** since 2019 except Hajj in 2023 Special. Fasting has not been asked at all in this period.

## Predictions for CSS 2028

These are judgments from the pattern, not certainties. Prepare the whole syllabus; use this list to decide what to write full practice answers on.

| Likelihood | Topic | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Very high | A Seerah question: peace-making and treaties, or the Prophet (PBUH) as educator or diplomat | Seerah appeared in 11 of 12 papers and was missing in 2026. Educator asked once; diplomat never by name. |
| Very high | Ijtihad or Ijma (possibly "collective ijtihad", "parliament and ijma", or ijtihad on modern issues) | Asked in 6 papers but absent in 2025 and 2026 |
| High | Status of women (inheritance, women and public life, feminism) | Last direct question 2022–2023 |
| High | Extremism, sectarianism, Islamophobia or interfaith harmony | Asked nearly every year in some form |
| High | Governance: civil servants' duties, letters of Hazrat Umar (RA) and Hazrat Ali (RA), Quranic guidance on good governance | Accountability asked 2021–2026; the neighbouring items are due |
| High | Economic system: riba and interest-free banking, zakat, poverty | Pakistan's constitutional target for ending riba falls at the start of 2028 (see notes file 7). Check the latest position before the exam. |
| Medium | A worship question: fasting, salah or zakat with "spiritual, moral and social impact" | None since 2023 Special; fasting never asked |
| Medium | Prophethood or finality of Prophethood; Akhirah | Belief rotation: Tawhid was 2026 |
| Medium | Islam and the West, or the role of Islam in the modern world | Asked 2025 and 2026; "role of Islam in the modern world" is a syllabus item never asked directly |
| Medium | Islamic political system: shura and democracy, sovereignty | Last asked 2019 |
| Medium | Judicial system of Islam | Syllabus item never asked directly |
| Medium | Social system and family; social justice; equality | 2022, 2025, 2026 show a new interest |
| Lower | Deen and religion; distinctive aspects of Islam | Asked 2016 and 2022 |
| Wild card | A current issue framed through Islam: Palestine and the Ummah's unity, artificial intelligence and ethics, climate and stewardship, social media and morality | One unusual question per year since 2021 |

**Minimum safe preparation:** ten fully prepared topics from at least five sections guarantee four comfortable choices in any paper set in this period. The fifteen model answers in `05-Model-Answers.md` were chosen on this basis.

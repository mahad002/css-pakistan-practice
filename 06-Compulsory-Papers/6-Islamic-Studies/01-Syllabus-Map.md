# Syllabus Map — Islamic Studies (100 marks)

Source: FPSC, *Revised Scheme and Syllabus for CSS Competitive Examination*, paper "Islamic Studies (100 marks)", pages 15–19. Read from a copy of the FPSC syllabus PDF hosted at csspastpapers.pk (link in `10-Resources.md`). The list below follows the official wording, with FPSC's spellings tidied.

**Frequency key** (12 papers checked: 2016–2026 plus the 2023 Special exam):

- ●●● asked in 6 or more papers
- ●● asked in 3–5 papers
- ● asked in 1–2 papers
- ○ not asked directly in 2016–2026

Counts are of 20-mark questions (a half-question "short note" counts). The 2025 paper is only partly confirmed; see `02-Past-Paper-Analysis.md`.

Tick the box when you have (a) read the notes, (b) memorised 5 references, (c) written or outlined one answer.

## Priority list (study in this order)

| Rank | Topic | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seerah: peace, treaties, military strategy, educator, diplomat | Asked almost every year; absent in 2026 |
| 2 | Human rights, dignity, status of women | 9 of 12 papers |
| 3 | Extremism, terrorism, sectarianism, Islamophobia, jihad | 8 of 12 papers |
| 4 | Governance: accountability, civil servants, Rightly Guided Caliphs, letters | 8 of 12 papers |
| 5 | Ijtihad and Ijma | 6 of 12 papers; absent in 2025 and 2026 |
| 6 | Beliefs: Tawhid, Prophethood, Akhirah | 6 of 12 papers |
| 7 | Civilization: self-purification, moral values, tolerance, characteristics | 8 of 12 papers, spread over many sub-topics |
| 8 | Economic system: zakat, riba, poverty, wealth concentration | 5 of 12 papers |
| 9 | Islam and the West | 3 papers, all recent (2023 Special, 2025, 2026) |
| 10 | Worship: salah, zakat, hajj, fasting | 5 papers, none since 2023 Special |
| 11 | Deen and religion; political, social and judicial systems | Lower frequency, but simple to prepare |

Official reading list: the syllabus gives 17 "required" and 24 "recommended" books. The ones worth a self-studying candidate's time are in `10-Resources.md`.

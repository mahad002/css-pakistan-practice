# Key Terms Glossary

Arabic and Islamic terms used in the paper, with plain definitions. Terms are grouped by theme and listed alphabetically inside each group. Spellings are simple English transliterations; the Urdu-style spelling common in Pakistan is added in brackets where it differs.

Use: (1) to define the key term in the first line of an answer; (2) for MCQs; (3) to write the term correctly.

---

## 1. Belief (Aqidah)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Akhirah** (Akhirat) | The Hereafter: life after death, resurrection, judgment, Paradise and Hell |
| **Allah** | The proper name of the one God |
| **Aqidah** | Creed; the set of beliefs a Muslim holds |
| **Asma al-Husna** | The most beautiful names of Allah |
| **Barzakh** | The interval between death and resurrection |
| **Fitrah** | The sound natural disposition on which every human is created, inclined to recognise Allah |
| **Hashr** | The gathering of all people on the Day of Judgment |
| **Iman** | Faith: belief in Allah, His angels, books, messengers, the Last Day and the divine decree |
| **Islam** | Submission to the will of Allah; the religion revealed through the prophets and completed through Muhammad (PBUH) |
| **Kufr** | Disbelief; rejection of the truth after it is known |
| **Malak** (pl. Mala'ikah) | Angel |
| **Mizan** | The scale on which deeds are weighed; also balance and justice |
| **Mu'jizah** | A miracle given to a prophet as proof |
| **Nabi** | A prophet: one who receives revelation |
| **Nifaq** | Hypocrisy: showing faith while hiding disbelief |
| **Qadr / Taqdir** | Divine decree: Allah's knowledge and measure of all things |
| **Qiyamah** (Qayamat) | The Resurrection; the Day of Judgment |
| **Rasul** | A messenger: a prophet sent with a message, often a new law or scripture |
| **Risalah** (Risalat) | Messengership; prophethood |
| **Ruh** | The soul or spirit |
| **Shirk** | Associating partners with Allah in His being, attributes or worship; the gravest sin |
| **Tawhid** (Tauheed) | The oneness of Allah |
| **Tawhid al-Rububiyyah** | Oneness of Allah as Creator, Sustainer and Ruler |
| **Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah** | Oneness of Allah as the only one worthy of worship |
| **Wahy** | Revelation from Allah to a prophet |
| **Khatm al-Nubuwwah** | The finality of prophethood in Muhammad (PBUH) |

## 2. Deen, sources and scripture

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ayah** | A verse of the Quran; literally "sign" |
| **Deen** (Din) | The complete way of life revealed by Allah: belief, worship, morals and law |
| **Hadith** (pl. Ahadith) | A report of what the Prophet (PBUH) said, did or approved |
| **Hadith Qudsi** | A hadith in which the Prophet (PBUH) reports words from Allah; the meaning is Allah's, the wording the Prophet's |
| **Juz / Para** | One of the thirty equal parts of the Quran |
| **Madhhab** (Mazhab) | A school of law; in Urdu also used for "religion" |
| **Makki / Madani** | Surahs revealed before / after the Hijrah |
| **Millah** | A religious community or its way |
| **Mushaf** | The written copy of the Quran |
| **Quran** | The word of Allah revealed to Muhammad (PBUH); literally "recitation" |
| **Seerah** (Sirah) | The biography of the Prophet (PBUH) |
| **Shariah** | The law of Islam; literally "the path to water" |
| **Sunnah** | The way of the Prophet (PBUH): his sayings, actions and approvals; the second source of law |
| **Surah** | A chapter of the Quran |
| **Tafsir** | Explanation and commentary of the Quran |
| **Ummah** | The community of believers worldwide |

## 3. Hadith sciences

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Asma al-Rijal** | The science of the biographies and reliability of narrators |
| **Da'if** | Weak hadith |
| **Hasan** | Fair or good hadith, slightly below sahih |
| **Isnad / Sanad** | The chain of narrators |
| **Marfu'** | A report attributed to the Prophet (PBUH) |
| **Matn** | The text of a hadith |
| **Mawdu'** | Fabricated report |
| **Mawquf** | A report that stops at a Companion |
| **Muhaddith** | A scholar of hadith |
| **Mutawatir** | A report narrated by so many people in each generation that it is certain |
| **Rawi** | A narrator |
| **Sahih** | Sound, authentic hadith |
| **Sihah Sittah** | The six major Sunni collections: Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah |

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|

| **Du'a** | Supplication |

| **Hajj** | The pilgrimage to Makkah in Dhul Hijjah, obligatory once on those able |
| **Ibadah** (Ibadat) | Worship; in the wide sense, every act done in obedience to Allah |
| **Ihram** | The state of consecration for pilgrimage, and the two sheets worn by men |
| **I'tikaf** | Retreat in a mosque for worship, especially in the last ten days of Ramadan |
| **Jama'ah** | Congregation |
| **Khushu'** | Humility and attentiveness in prayer |
| **Miqat** | The boundary point where pilgrims enter ihram |
| **Nafl** | A voluntary act of worship |
| **Nisab** | The minimum amount of wealth on which zakat is due |
| **Qiblah** | The direction of prayer, toward the Ka'bah |
| **Sadaqah** | Voluntary charity |
| **Sadaqat al-Fitr** | The charity due before the Eid prayer at the end of Ramadan |
| **Salah** (Salat, Namaz) | The prescribed prayer |
| **Sawm** (Roza) | Fasting |
| **Sa'i** | Walking seven times between Safa and Marwah |
| **Shahadah** | The testimony of faith |
| **Talbiyah** | The pilgrim's chant: "Here I am, O Allah, here I am" |
| **Taqwa** | God-consciousness; guarding oneself against what displeases Allah |
| **Tawaf** | Walking seven times around the Ka'bah |
| **Tayammum** | Purification with clean earth when water is unavailable |
| **Umrah** | The lesser pilgrimage, at any time of year |
| **Ushr** | A tenth: the zakat on farm produce |
| **Wudu** (Wuzu) | Ablution before prayer |
| **Wuquf** | The standing at Arafat on 9 Dhul Hijjah; the central rite of Hajj |
| **Zakat** (Zakah) | The obligatory annual share of wealth due to the eight categories named in the Quran |

## 5. Law and jurisprudence (Fiqh and Usul)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ahl al-Hall wa'l-Aqd** | "Those who loose and bind": the qualified representatives who choose and advise the ruler |
| **Diyat** | Blood-money; compensation for killing or injury |
| **Faqih** | A jurist |
| **Fatwa** | A legal opinion given by a qualified scholar |
| **Fiqh** | Islamic jurisprudence; the human understanding of the Shariah |
| **Halal / Haram** | Lawful / unlawful |
| **Hirabah** | Armed attack on public safety; highway robbery; the fiqh category closest to terrorism |
| **Hudud** (sing. Hadd) | Punishments fixed by the Quran or Sunnah |
| **Ijma** | Consensus of the qualified jurists of an age on a legal ruling; third source of law |
| **Ijtihad** | The utmost effort of a qualified jurist to derive a ruling from the sources |
| **Ikhtilaf** | Difference of scholarly opinion |
| **Illah** | The effective cause of a ruling, on which analogy is built |
| **Istihsan** | Juristic preference: leaving a strict analogy for a stronger reason; chiefly Hanafi |
| **Istishab** | Presumption that an existing state continues until the contrary is proved |
| **Makruh / Mandub / Mubah** | Disliked / recommended / permitted |
| **Maqasid al-Shariah** | The objectives of the law: protection of religion, life, intellect, family and property |
| **Maslahah** | Public interest or benefit |
| **Maslahah Mursalah** | A public interest neither affirmed nor rejected by a text; chiefly Maliki |
| **Mufti** | A scholar qualified to give fatwa |
| **Mujtahid** | A scholar qualified to exercise ijtihad |
| **Qadi** (Qazi) | A judge |
| **Qisas** | Just retaliation for killing or injury |
| **Qiyas** | Analogy: extending a ruling to a new case with the same cause; fourth source of law |
| **Sadd al-Dhara'i** | Blocking the means that lead to harm |
| **Sulh** | Peaceful settlement; reconciliation |
| **Tahkim** | Arbitration |
| **Takfir** | Declaring a Muslim to be an unbeliever |
| **Taqlid** | Following the ruling of a jurist without examining his evidence |
| **Ta'zir** | Discretionary punishment set by the judge or state |
| **Urf** | Custom recognised by the law where the texts are silent |
| **Usul al-Fiqh** | The principles and methods by which rulings are derived |

|---|---|

| **Bay'ah** (Bai'at) | The pledge of allegiance |
| **Bayt al-Mal** | The public treasury |
| **Dhimmi** (Zimmi) | A non-Muslim citizen under the protection of the Islamic state |
| **Diwan** | A register or department of state; first the stipend register of Umar (RA) |
| **Fay'** | Revenue gained without fighting; held for public purposes |
| **Hakimiyyah** | Sovereignty; in Islam it belongs to Allah |

| **Jizyah** | A tax on non-Muslim citizens in return for protection and exemption from military service |

| **Kharaj** | Land tax |
| **Khilafah** (Khilafat) | Vicegerency of man on earth; also the Caliphate |
| **Khulafa-e-Rashideen** | The Rightly Guided Caliphs: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali (may Allah be pleased with them) |
| **Khums** | One-fifth: the share of spoils set aside for public and welfare purposes |
| **Majlis al-Shura** | The consultative council |
| **Mazalim** | The court of grievances against officials and the powerful |
| **Muhasabah / Ihtisab** | Accountability |
| **Muhtasib** | The officer of hisbah; in Pakistan, the ombudsman |
| **Shura** | Consultation |
| **Siyasah Shar'iyyah** | Governance according to the Shariah |
| **Ulu'l-Amr** | Those in authority (An-Nisa 4:59) |
| **Wali** | Governor; guardian |
| **Wazir** | Minister |

## 7. Economy

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gharar** | Excessive uncertainty in a contract; prohibited |
| **Ihtikar** | Hoarding goods to raise prices |
| **Ijarah** | Leasing or hire |
| **Infaq** | Spending in the way of Allah |
| **Israf / Tabdhir** | Extravagance / squandering |
| **Istisna** | A contract to manufacture or build to order |
| **Maysir / Qimar** | Gambling |
| **Mudarabah** | Profit-sharing: one party gives capital, the other labour |
| **Murabahah** | Sale at cost plus an agreed, disclosed profit |
| **Musharakah** | Partnership in which all contribute capital and share profit and loss |
| **Qard Hasan** | An interest-free loan |
| **Riba** | Usury or interest: a fixed increase demanded on a loan, or unequal exchange of the same commodity |
| **Rizq** | Provision from Allah |
| **Salam** | A sale with full advance payment for goods delivered later |
| **Sukuk** | Certificates of ownership in an asset or project; the Islamic alternative to bonds |
| **Takaful** | Cooperative, mutual insurance |
| **Waqf** | A permanent charitable endowment |

| **Sulh al-Hudaybiyyah** | The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah |

## 11. Jihad, peace and contemporary terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dar al-Islam / Dar al-Harb** | Classical juristic terms for lands under Muslim rule and lands at war with them; many modern scholars add other categories such as lands of treaty |
| **Da'wah** | Inviting people to Islam |
| **Fasad fi'l-Ard** | Spreading corruption and disorder on earth |
| **Fitnah** | Trial; persecution; civil strife |
| **Ghuluw** | Excess or extremism in religion |
| **Islamophobia** | Fear of, prejudice against and hostility toward Islam and Muslims |
| **Jihad** | Striving in the way of Allah with self, speech, wealth or arms |
| **Mujaddid** | A renewer of the religion |
| **Mu'ahid / Musta'min** | A non-Muslim under treaty / under a grant of safe-conduct |
| **Qital** | Fighting; armed combat |
| **Tajdid** | Renewal of the religion |
| **Tasamuh** | Tolerance |
| **Wasatiyyah** | Moderation; the middle way (from Al-Baqarah 2:143) |

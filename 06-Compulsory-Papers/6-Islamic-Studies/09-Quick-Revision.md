# Quick Revision

One card per topic: the skeleton of a 20-mark answer, the five best references to quote, one or two names, and a contemporary link. Use in the last month and on the last day.

**Before the exam, check three things:** (1) you can write each card's outline from memory; (2) you can quote its five references with the surah name or collection; (3) you have one Pakistan or world example ready.

**Arabic:** the short phrases you may write in Arabic are in `04-Quran-and-Hadith-Reference-Bank.md`, Part 3. Write Arabic only if you can do it without error.

**Universal skeleton:** Introduction (define, one reference, line of answer) → 10–14 headed points with references → Seerah and Caliphate examples → scholars → contemporary relevance and recommendations → conclusion.

## Section I — Introduction to Islam

### 1. Concept of Islam and Deen; Deen versus religion

**Outline:** meaning of Islam (peace, submission) → meaning of Deen (judgment, obedience, law, way of life) → religion in the narrow sense → 7 differences (scope, sphere, law, state, world and Hereafter, priesthood, aim) → Islam as complete code → importance of Deen: individual (purpose, nature, peace, standard, character, balance, hope), society (equality, justice, family, economy, accountable rule, brotherhood), civilization (direction for knowledge and power).

**Five references:** Al Imran 3:19 · Al-Ma'idah 5:3 · Al-Baqarah 2:208 · Al-An'am 6:162 · Hadith of Jibril (Muslim)

**Names:** Maududi (*Four Basic Quranic Terms*); Muhammad Asad. **Today:** ritual without conduct; Article 31.

### 2. Distinctive aspects of Islam

**Outline:** pure monotheism → universality → finality and completeness → preserved Book → complete way of life → harmony with nature and reason → balance and moderation → ease → unity of spiritual and worldly → equality → individual responsibility → no compulsion → permanence with flexibility (ijtihad) → no priesthood.

**Five references:** Al-Ikhlas 112:1–4 · Saba 34:28 · Al-Hijr 15:9 · Al-Baqarah 2:143 · Al-Baqarah 2:185

**Names:** Dr Hamidullah; Khurshid Ahmad.

### 3. Tawhid; existence of Allah

**Outline:** meaning → kinds (lordship, worship, names and attributes) → shirk → **rational arguments:** causation, design, human nature, the self, unity of order, moral law, contingency, prophets and the Quran, Ibrahim's (AS) reasoning → **effects on the individual:** dignity, no fear, humility, trust, peace, responsibility, hope, single purpose → **effects on society:** equality, no lordship of man over man, rule of law, unity, accountability, science.

**Five references:** Al-Ikhlas 112:1–4 · At-Tur 52:35–36 · Al-Anbiya 21:22 · Al-Hujurat 49:13 · Ar-Ra'd 13:28

**Names:** al-Ghazali; Maududi (*Towards Understanding Islam*); Iqbal. **Today:** idols of wealth, nation, self-opinion.

### 4. Prophethood (Risalat) and its finality

**Outline:** meaning (nabi, rasul) → need (limits of reason, justice, example) → qualities of prophets → four tasks of the Prophet (PBUH) → special features (universal, complete, preserved, mercy) → **finality:** verse, "last brick" hadith, "no prophet after me", consensus → why it ended → effects: individual (certainty, model, love, purification), society (one law, unity, justice, respect for all prophets, no false claimants).

**Five references:** Al-Ahzab 33:40 · Al-Ahzab 33:21 · Al-Hadid 57:25 · Al-Jumu'ah 62:2 · "last brick" hadith (Bukhari, Muslim)

**Names:** Iqbal (Lecture 5). **Today:** Article 260; honour of the Prophet (PBUH) defended through character.

### 5. Akhirah

**Outline:** meaning and stages → **arguments:** first creation, greater creation, revived earth, stages of growth, purpose, justice, power, conscience → **individual effects:** accountability, self-restraint, purpose, patience, no greed, sincerity → **collective effects:** honest trade, just rulers, fair courts, care of the weak, low crime, sacrifice, peace → contrast with materialism.

**Five references:** Az-Zalzalah 99:7–8 · Ya-Sin 36:78–79 · Al-Mu'minun 23:115 · Al-Jathiyah 45:21–22 · "feet will not move" hadith (Tirmidhi)

**Today:** corruption persists despite laws because inner accountability is weak.

### 6. Salah

**Outline:** meaning → obligation (Mi'raj; fixed times) → kinds → **spiritual:** link with Allah, peace, sins removed, humility, strength → **moral:** restraint from wrong, discipline, cleanliness, sincerity → **social:** equality, unity, mosque as centre, leadership and order, community awareness → why effects are missing.

**Five references:** Al-Ankabut 29:45 · Ta-Ha 20:14 · Al-Mu'minun 23:1–2 · Al-Hajj 22:41 · river hadith (Bukhari, Muslim)

### 7. Zakat

**Outline:** meaning (purify, grow) → obligation → nisab and rates → eight heads → **spiritual:** purifies, gratitude, reward → **moral:** generosity, compassion, humility, dignity of receiver → **social and economic:** circulation, social security, anti-hoarding, demand, lower class gap, brotherhood → poverty reduction → zakat versus tax → Pakistan (1980 Ordinance; reforms).

**Five references:** At-Tawbah 9:60 · At-Tawbah 9:103 · Al-Hashr 59:7 · Adh-Dhariyat 51:19 · Mu'adh hadith (Bukhari)

**Names:** Abu Bakr's (RA) stand; Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz.

### 8. Sawm

**Outline:** meaning → obligation (2 AH) → **spiritual:** taqwa, sincerity, forgiveness, Quran, Laylat al-Qadr → **moral:** self-control, truthfulness, patience, gratitude → **social:** empathy, generosity, unity, equality, sadaqat al-fitr → abuses today (profiteering, waste).

**Five references:** Al-Baqarah 2:183 · Al-Baqarah 2:185 · Al-Qadr 97:3 · "fasting is a shield" (Bukhari) · "false speech" hadith (Bukhari)

### 9. Hajj

**Outline:** meaning → obligation → main rites → **spiritual:** Tawhid renewed, forgiveness, reminder of death, link with Ibrahim (AS), piety → **moral:** restraint, patience, humility, sacrifice → **social, economic, political:** equality, unity, universality, exchange of ideas, trade, forum of the Ummah, peace.

**Five references:** Al Imran 3:97 · Al-Hajj 22:27–28 · Al-Hajj 22:37 · Al-Baqarah 2:197 · "returns like the day his mother bore him" (Bukhari, Muslim)

**Today:** unity of Hajj not carried into politics.

### 10. Fard kifayah

**Outline:** definition; contrast with fard ayn → evidence → religious role (scholars, judges, funeral) → social role (al-Ghazali: medicine, arithmetic) → modern scope: health, engineering, defence technology, economics, law, media, AI, research, relief → responsibility of state and able individuals.

**References:** At-Tawbah 9:122 · Al Imran 3:104 · Al-Anfal 8:60 · Al-Ma'idah 5:2 · Muslim 2664 (strong believer)

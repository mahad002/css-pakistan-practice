# MCQ Bank — 300 Questions with Answers

Part I of the paper has 20 MCQs for 20 marks, to be done in at most 30 minutes, with no negative marking. The screening test (MPT) also carries Islamic Studies MCQs.

**How to use**

1. Cover the answer key. Do one section at a time and mark yourself.
2. Put a dot beside every question you got wrong. Redo only the dotted ones a week later.
3. Recent papers use "None of these" as option D in most questions. In the exam, choose it only when you are sure the first three are wrong.
4. Where sources differ, the question says "commonly" or "usually", and the key gives the answer FPSC-style papers expect.

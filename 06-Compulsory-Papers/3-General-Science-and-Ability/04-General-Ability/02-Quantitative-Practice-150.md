## Set A: Number skills (1–20)

**1.** Simplify: 48 ÷ 6 × 2 + 5² − (9 − 4).
Solution: bracket = 5; 5² = 25; 48 ÷ 6 = 8; 8 × 2 = 16; 16 + 25 − 5.
**Answer: 36**

**2.** Evaluate 3/4 + 5/6 − 7/12.
Solution: LCM 12 → 9/12 + 10/12 − 7/12 = 12/12.
**Answer: 1**

**3.** Arrange in descending order: 5/8, 7/12, 2/3, 11/18.
Solution: LCM 72 → 45/72, 42/72, 48/72, 44/72.
**Answer: 2/3, 5/8, 11/18, 7/12**

**4.** Round 47.3856 to (i) 2 decimal places, (ii) 3 significant figures, (iii) the nearest ten.
Solution: (i) third decimal is 5 → round up. (ii) digits 4, 7, 3 then 8 → round up. (iii) units digit 7 → round up.
**Answer: (i) 47.39 (ii) 47.4 (iii) 50**

**5.** How many prime numbers lie between 40 and 70?
Solution: 41, 43, 47, 53, 59, 61, 67.
**Answer: 7**

**6.** Find the HCF and LCM of 84 and 126.
Solution: 84 = 2² × 3 × 7; 126 = 2 × 3² × 7. HCF = 2 × 3 × 7; LCM = 2² × 3² × 7.
**Answer: HCF 42, LCM 252** (check: 42 × 252 = 84 × 126 = 10,584)

**7.** Three bells ring every 6, 8 and 15 minutes. They ring together at 9:00 am. When do they next ring together?
Solution: LCM(6, 8, 15) = 2³ × 3 × 5 = 120 minutes = 2 hours.
**Answer: 11:00 am**

**8.** A floor is 4.2 m by 3.6 m. What is the largest square tile that fits exactly, and how many are needed?
Solution: HCF(420 cm, 360 cm) = 60 cm. Tiles = (420 ÷ 60) × (360 ÷ 60) = 7 × 6.
**Answer: 60 cm tile; 42 tiles**

**9.** Find the remainder when 2¹⁰ is divided by 7.
Solution: 2³ = 8 leaves remainder 1. 2¹⁰ = (2³)³ × 2 → remainder 1 × 2. (Or 1024 = 7 × 146 + 2.)
**Answer: 2**

**10.** Find the smallest number that leaves remainder 5 when divided by 6, 8 or 12.
Solution: LCM(6, 8, 12) = 24. Number = 24 + 5.
**Answer: 29**

**11.** N leaves remainder 3 when divided by 5 and remainder 2 when divided by 7. Find the smallest N, and the remainder when N is divided by 6.
Solution: 5k + 3: 3, 8, 13, 18, 23. Remainders mod 7: 3, 1, 6, 4, 2 → N = 23. 23 = 6 × 3 + 5.
**Answer: N = 23; remainder 5**

**12.** What is the units digit of 7³⁵?
Solution: units digits of powers of 7 cycle 7, 9, 3, 1. 35 = 4 × 8 + 3 → third in the cycle.
**Answer: 3**

**13.** Write 0.375 as a fraction in lowest terms and as a percentage.
Solution: 375/1000 = 3/8; × 100 = 37.5%.
**Answer: 3/8; 37.5%**

**14.** Find √1764 by prime factorisation.
Solution: 1764 = 2² × 3² × 7². Root = 2 × 3 × 7.
**Answer: 42**

**15.** Five readings of a length are 8.246, 8.251, 8.239, 8.257 and 8.244 m. Find the absolute error between the exact mean and the mean of the readings rounded to 2 decimal places.
Solution: exact sum = 41.237; exact mean = 8.2474. Rounded readings: 8.25, 8.25, 8.24, 8.26, 8.24; sum 41.24; mean 8.248. Error = |8.248 − 8.2474|.
**Answer: 0.0006 m**

**16.** Find (i) the sum of the first 50 natural numbers and (ii) the sum of the first 20 odd numbers.
Solution: (i) n(n + 1)/2 = 50 × 51/2. (ii) n² = 20².
**Answer: (i) 1,275 (ii) 400**

**17.** How many numbers from 1 to 200 are divisible by both 4 and 6?
Solution: divisible by LCM(4, 6) = 12. 200 ÷ 12 = 16 remainder 8.
**Answer: 16**

**18.** A number divided by 13 gives quotient 17 and remainder 9. Find the number.
Solution: 13 × 17 + 9 = 221 + 9.
**Answer: 230**

**19.** (i) Write 0.0000456 in scientific notation. (ii) Evaluate (3.2 × 10⁴) × (5 × 10⁻²).
Solution: (i) move the point 5 places. (ii) 3.2 × 5 = 16; 10⁴ × 10⁻² = 10²; 16 × 10² = 1.6 × 10³.
**Answer: (i) 4.56 × 10⁻⁵ (ii) 1.6 × 10³ = 1,600**

**20.** (i) Is 221 prime? (ii) Is 4,356 divisible by 9 and by 11?
Solution: (i) 221 = 13 × 17. (ii) digit sum 18 → divisible by 9; (4 + 5) − (3 + 6) = 0 → divisible by 11.
**Answer: (i) No (ii) Yes to both** (4,356 = 66²)

## Set B: Percentages (21–40)

**21.** Find 35% of 840.
Solution: 840 × 35/100 = 8.4 × 35.
**Answer: 294**

**22.** 48 is what percentage of 320?
Solution: 48/320 × 100 = 3/20 × 100.
**Answer: 15%**

**23.** A price rises from Rs 250 to Rs 290. Find the percentage increase.
Solution: 40/250 × 100.
**Answer: 16%**

**24.** After a 20% rise a salary is Rs 54,000. Find the old salary.
Solution: 54,000 ÷ 1.2.
**Answer: Rs 45,000**

**25.** A firm's sales fall 20% one year and 25% the next. Find the overall percentage fall.
Solution: 0.80 × 0.75 = 0.60 of the original.
**Answer: 40%**

**26.** A price is raised 25% and then cut 20%. Find the net change.
Solution: 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00.
**Answer: No change (0%)**

**27.** In a 15%-off sale, a TV and an oven together cost Rs 30,600. The oven's normal price is Rs 12,000. Find the TV's normal price.
Solution: normal total = 30,600 ÷ 0.85 = 36,000. TV = 36,000 − 12,000.
**Answer: Rs 24,000**

**28.** A bill of Rs 20,880 includes 16% sales tax. Find the tax.
Solution: basic = 20,880 ÷ 1.16 = 18,000. Tax = 20,880 − 18,000.
**Answer: Rs 2,880**

**29.** A sofa sells for Rs 12,300 after an 18% discount. (i) Find the marked price. (ii) What would it sell for after successive discounts of 10% and 8%?
Solution: (i) 12,300 ÷ 0.82 = 15,000. (ii) 15,000 × 0.90 × 0.92 = 15,000 × 0.828.
**Answer: (i) Rs 15,000 (ii) Rs 12,420** (Rs 120 more than with a single 18% discount)

**30.** 4 litres of paint cover 30 m². By what percentage must the paint be increased to cover 48 m²?
Solution: 30/4 = 7.5 m² per litre. Needed = 48 ÷ 7.5 = 6.4 litres. Increase = 2.4/4 × 100.
**Answer: 60%**

**31.** In a two-candidate election the winner gets 58% of the votes and wins by 2,400. Find the total votes and the winner's votes.
Solution: margin = 58% − 42% = 16% of total. Total = 2,400 ÷ 0.16 = 15,000. Winner = 58% of 15,000.
**Answer: 15,000 votes; winner 8,700**

**32.** A town of 80,000 grows 5% a year. Find its population after 2 years.
Solution: 80,000 × 1.05² = 80,000 × 1.1025.
**Answer: 88,200**

**33.** A price is cut by 20%. By what percentage must the new price rise to restore the original?
Solution: new = 0.8P. Needed rise = 0.2P/0.8P = 1/4.
**Answer: 25%**

**34.** The price of sugar rises 25%. By what percentage must a family cut consumption to keep its spending the same?
Solution: consumption must become 1/1.25 = 0.8 of before.
**Answer: 20%**

**35.** A student scores 432 out of 600. (i) Find the percentage. (ii) How many more marks were needed for 80%?
Solution: (i) 432/600 × 100. (ii) 80% of 600 = 480; 480 − 432.
**Answer: (i) 72% (ii) 48 marks**

**36.** A restaurant bill is Rs 8,000. A 10% discount is given. Then a 5% service charge and 15% GST are both added on the discounted amount. Find the amount payable.
Solution: 8,000 × 0.9 = 7,200. Charges = 20% of 7,200 = 1,440.
**Answer: Rs 8,640**

**37.** A shopkeeper raised a price by 20% by mistake instead of reducing it by 20%. The wrong tag shows Rs 144. Find the original price and the correct tag price.
Solution: 1.2P = 144 → P = 120. Correct = 0.8 × 120.
**Answer: Original Rs 120; correct tag Rs 96**

**38.** A buyer pays a deposit of Rs 25,000 and 24 equal monthly instalments. The total paid is 125% of the cash price of Rs 500,000. Find each instalment.
Solution: total = 1.25 × 500,000 = 625,000. Instalments total = 600,000. Each = 600,000 ÷ 24.
**Answer: Rs 25,000**

**39.** A 40 kg alloy is 25% copper. How much copper must be added to make it 40% copper?
Solution: copper = 10 kg. (10 + x)/(40 + x) = 0.4 → 10 + x = 16 + 0.4x → 0.6x = 6.
**Answer: 10 kg** (check: 20/50 = 40%)

**40.** A length of 12.5 m is measured as 12.2 m. Find the percentage error.
Solution: error 0.3 m; 0.3/12.5 × 100.
**Answer: 2.4%**

## Set C: Profit, loss and discount (41–50)

**41.** A machine bought for Rs 24,000 is sold at a gain of 12.5%. Find the selling price.
Solution: gain = 1/8 of 24,000 = 3,000.
**Answer: Rs 27,000**

**42.** A motorbike sells for Rs 630,000, which includes a profit equal to one-sixth of its cost. Find the cost and the profit.
Solution: CP × 7/6 = 630,000 → CP = 540,000. Profit = 90,000.
**Answer: Cost Rs 540,000; profit Rs 90,000**

**43.** An article sold for Rs 1,020 gives a loss of 15%. (i) Find the cost. (ii) At what price would it give a 10% gain?
Solution: (i) 1,020 ÷ 0.85 = 1,200. (ii) 1,200 × 1.1.
**Answer: (i) Rs 1,200 (ii) Rs 1,320**

**44.** A man buys pencils at 12 for Rs 100 and sells them at 10 for Rs 100. Find his gain percent.
Solution: CP each = 100/12 = 8⅓; SP each = 10. Gain = 1⅔ on 8⅓ = 1/5.
**Answer: 20%**

**45.** A trader marks goods 40% above cost and gives a 25% discount. Find the profit or loss percent.
Solution: SP = 1.40 × 0.75 = 1.05 of cost.
**Answer: 5% profit**

**46.** Two phones are sold for Rs 9,900 each, one at a 10% gain and one at a 10% loss. Find the overall result.
Solution: costs = 9,900 ÷ 1.1 = 9,000 and 9,900 ÷ 0.9 = 11,000; total cost 20,000; total sales 19,800.
**Answer: Loss of Rs 200 = 1%**

**47.** The cost of 15 articles equals the selling price of 12. Find the gain percent.
Solution: 12 SP = 15 CP → SP = 1.25 CP.
**Answer: 25%**

**48.** A dishonest dealer sells at cost price but uses a 900 g weight for 1 kg. Find his gain percent.
Solution: he gains 100 g on every 900 g given: 100/900 × 100.
**Answer: 11 1/9% (about 11.1%)**

**49.** A shopkeeper sells an item at a 6% loss. Had he sold it for Rs 450 more he would have gained 9%. Find the cost.
Solution: 15% of cost = 450.
**Answer: Rs 3,000**

**50.** A trader wants a 20% profit on an article costing Rs 600 after allowing a 10% discount. Find the marked price.
Solution: SP = 600 × 1.2 = 720. MP = 720 ÷ 0.9.
**Answer: Rs 800**

## Set D: Ratio, proportion, sharing, mixtures (51–68)

**51.** Divide Rs 7,200 in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5.
Solution: 12 parts; one part = 600.
**Answer: Rs 1,800, Rs 2,400, Rs 3,000**

**52.** If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, find A : B : C.
Solution: make B = 12: A : B = 8 : 12; B : C = 12 : 15.
**Answer: 8 : 12 : 15**

**53.** Milk and water are in the ratio 7 : 2. When 9 litres of water are added the ratio becomes 7 : 3. Find the original amounts.
Solution: milk 7k, water 2k. 7k/(2k + 9) = 7/3 → 21k = 14k + 63 → k = 9.
**Answer: Milk 63 L, water 18 L (total 81 L)**

**54.** The ages of two brothers are in the ratio 4 : 7. In 6 years the ratio will be 2 : 3. Find their ages.
Solution: (4k + 6)/(7k + 6) = 2/3 → 12k + 18 = 14k + 12 → k = 3.
**Answer: 12 and 21 years**

**55.** Divide Rs 700 among A, B and C so that half of A's share, one-third of B's share plus 10, and one-quarter of C's share plus 20 are all equal.
Solution: let each = k. A = 2k, B = 3(k − 10), C = 4(k − 20). Sum: 9k − 110 = 700 → k = 90.
**Answer: A Rs 180, B Rs 240, C Rs 280** (check: 90 = 80 + 10 = 70 + 20)

**56.** A man leaves Rs 1,950,000. A debt of Rs 150,000 is paid first. The rest goes to 2 sons and 2 daughters, each son getting twice a daughter's share. Find each share.
Solution: 1,800,000 in 2 : 2 : 1 : 1 = 6 parts of 300,000.
**Answer: Each son Rs 600,000; each daughter Rs 300,000**

**57.** An estate is worth Rs 985,000. Debts are Rs 20,000 and funeral costs Rs 5,000. The widow gets one-eighth of the remainder; the rest is shared by 2 sons and 3 daughters with a son getting twice a daughter's share. Find each share.
Solution: net = 960,000. Widow = 120,000. Rest = 840,000 in 2 : 2 : 1 : 1 : 1 = 7 parts of 120,000.
**Answer: Widow Rs 120,000; each son Rs 240,000; each daughter Rs 120,000**

**58.** 8 pens cost Rs 360. Find the cost of 14 pens.
Solution: one pen = 45; 14 × 45.
**Answer: Rs 630**

**59.** 50 men can build a wall in 40 days. How long will 80 men take?
Solution: 50 × 40 = 2,000 man-days; 2,000 ÷ 80.
**Answer: 25 days**

**60.** A map has a scale of 1 : 50,000. What real distance does 7 cm on the map represent?
Solution: 7 × 50,000 = 350,000 cm = 3,500 m.
**Answer: 3.5 km**

**61.** y is directly proportional to x². When x = 3, y = 18. Find y when x = 5. What happens to y if x is doubled?
Solution: k = 18/9 = 2. y = 2 × 25 = 50. Doubling x multiplies y by 2² = 4.
**Answer: y = 50; y becomes 4 times**

**62.** Four friends share Rs 2,800. A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 3 : 4 and C : D = 4 : 5. Find each share.
Solution: A : B : C : D = 2 : 3 : 4 : 5; 14 parts of 200.
**Answer: Rs 400, Rs 600, Rs 800, Rs 1,000**

**63.** 50 g of an alloy has gold and copper in the ratio 7 : 3. How much gold must be added to make the ratio 4 : 1?
Solution: gold 35 g, copper 15 g. (35 + x)/15 = 4 → x = 25.
**Answer: 25 g**

**64.** Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 8 and their sum is 143. Find them.
Solution: 13 parts; one part = 11.
**Answer: 55 and 88**

**65.** The incomes of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 4 and their expenses in the ratio 3 : 2. Each saves Rs 8,000. Find their incomes.
Solution: 5k − 3m = 8,000 and 4k − 2m = 8,000. From the second, m = 2k − 4,000. Then 5k − 6k + 12,000 = 8,000 → k = 4,000.
**Answer: Rs 20,000 and Rs 16,000** (expenses 12,000 and 8,000)

**66.** 20 litres of 30% acid are mixed with 30 litres of 50% acid. Find the strength of the mixture.
Solution: acid = 6 + 15 = 21 litres in 50 litres.
**Answer: 42%**

**67.** A invests Rs 300,000 for 12 months, B Rs 400,000 for 9 months and C Rs 600,000 for 4 months. The profit is Rs 400,000. Find each share.
Solution: capital × time = 3,600 : 3,600 : 2,400 = 3 : 3 : 2; 8 parts of 50,000.
**Answer: A Rs 150,000; B Rs 150,000; C Rs 100,000**

**68.** A car uses 9 litres of petrol for 126 km. How much does it need for 350 km?
Solution: 126 ÷ 9 = 14 km per litre; 350 ÷ 14.
**Answer: 25 litres**

## Set E: Averages (69–78)

**69.** The average mark of 15 students is 42. A new student joins and the average becomes 43. Find the new student's mark.
Solution: 16 × 43 − 15 × 42 = 688 − 630.
**Answer: 58**

**70.** The average of 9 numbers is 50. The average of the first five is 46 and of the last five is 55. Find the fifth number.
Solution: 5 × 46 + 5 × 55 − 9 × 50 = 230 + 275 − 450.
**Answer: 55**

**71.** A batsman averages 40 runs over 9 innings. How many runs must he score in the 10th innings to raise his average to 42?
Solution: 10 × 42 − 9 × 40 = 420 − 360.
**Answer: 60**

**72.** A car covers 60 km at 30 km/h and the next 60 km at 60 km/h. Find the average speed.
Solution: time = 2 h + 1 h = 3 h; distance 120 km.
**Answer: 40 km/h** (not 45)

**73.** In a class, 30 students average 60 marks and 20 students average 70. Find the class average.
Solution: (1,800 + 1,400)/50.
**Answer: 64**

**74.** Find the average of the first 10 multiples of 7.
Solution: 7 × (1 + 2 + … + 10)/10 = 7 × 5.5.
**Answer: 38.5**

**75.** The average of five consecutive odd numbers is 31. Find the largest.
Solution: the middle number is 31; numbers are 27, 29, 31, 33, 35.
**Answer: 35**

**76.** The average of 20 numbers was worked out as 35. Later it was found that 54 had been read as 45. Find the correct average.
Solution: the sum was 9 too small. Correct average = 35 + 9/20.
**Answer: 35.45**

**77.** The average age of 25 students is 14 years. When the teacher is included the average becomes 15. Find the teacher's age.
Solution: 26 × 15 − 25 × 14 = 390 − 350.
**Answer: 40 years**

**78.** A train covers 0.3, 0.5, 0.6, 0.6 and 0.5 km in its first five minutes. For the next five minutes it runs at a steady 36 km/h. Find the total distance and the average speed for the ten minutes.
Solution: first stage = 2.5 km. Second stage = 36 × 5/60 = 3 km. Total 5.5 km in 10 min = 1/6 h. Speed = 5.5 × 6.
**Answer: 5.5 km; 33 km/h**

## Set F: Interest (79–86)

**79.** Find the simple interest on Rs 40,000 at 7.5% a year for 4 years, and the amount.
Solution: 40,000 × 7.5 × 4/100 = 12,000.
**Answer: Interest Rs 12,000; amount Rs 52,000**

**80.** Rs 50,000 becomes Rs 62,000 in 3 years at simple interest. Find the rate.
Solution: I = 12,000. R = 100 × 12,000/(50,000 × 3).
**Answer: 8% a year**

**81.** In how many years will a sum double at 12.5% simple interest?
Solution: interest must equal the principal: 12.5 × T = 100.
**Answer: 8 years**

**82.** Find the compound interest on Rs 20,000 at 10% a year for 2 years. How much more is it than simple interest?
Solution: 20,000 × 1.1² = 24,200; CI = 4,200. SI = 4,000.
**Answer: Rs 4,200; Rs 200 more**

**83.** Find the amount of Rs 16,000 after 3 years at 5% compound interest.
Solution: 16,000 × 1.05³ = 16,000 × 1.157625.
**Answer: Rs 18,522**

**84.** What sum amounts to Rs 13,310 in 3 years at 10% compound interest?
Solution: P × 1.331 = 13,310.
**Answer: Rs 10,000**

**85.** A sum at simple interest amounts to Rs 9,440 in 3 years and Rs 10,400 in 5 years. Find the sum and the rate.
Solution: interest for 2 years = 960 → 480 a year. Principal = 9,440 − 3 × 480 = 8,000. Rate = 480/8,000 × 100.
**Answer: Rs 8,000; 6% a year**

**86.** A machine worth Rs 500,000 loses 10% of its value each year. Find its value after 3 years.
Solution: 500,000 × 0.9³ = 500,000 × 0.729.
**Answer: Rs 364,500**

## Set G: Work, time and rates (87–98)

**87.** A can do a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. How long do they take together?
Solution: 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 per day → 36/5 days.
**Answer: 7.2 days (7 1/5 days)**

**88.** A and B together finish a job in 6 days. A alone takes 10 days. How long does B take alone?
Solution: 1/6 − 1/10 = 2/30 = 1/15.
**Answer: 15 days**

**89.** 15 men can finish a job in 20 days. After 5 days, 5 men leave. How many more days are needed?
Solution: total 300 man-days; done 75; left 225; with 10 men: 22.5.
**Answer: 22.5 days**

**90.** Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours and pipe B in 8 hours. Pipe C empties it in 12 hours. With all three open, how long to fill the tank?
Solution: 1/6 + 1/8 − 1/12 = (4 + 3 − 2)/24 = 5/24 per hour.
**Answer: 4.8 hours (4 h 48 min)**

**91.** One pipe fills a pool 1.5 times as fast as another. Together they take 6 hours. How long does each take alone?
Solution: slower rate r, faster 1.5r. 2.5r × 6 = 1 → r = 1/15.
**Answer: Slower 15 hours; faster 10 hours** (check: 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6)

**92.** A fort has food for 600 men for 40 days. After 10 days, 150 more men arrive. How long will the remaining food last?
Solution: food left = 600 × 30 = 18,000 man-days; 18,000 ÷ 750.
**Answer: 24 days**

**93.** Tailors A, B and C can finish an order alone in 10, 15 and 30 hours. A and B start together. After 2 hours B leaves and C takes his place. Find the total time taken.
Solution: in 2 hours A and B do 2(1/10 + 1/15) = 1/3. Left: 2/3. A and C together do 1/10 + 1/30 = 2/15 per hour. Time = (2/3) ÷ (2/15) = 5 hours.
**Answer: 7 hours**

**94.** 6 men or 9 women can do a job in 12 days. How long will 4 men and 6 women take?
Solution: 6 men = 9 women → 1 man = 1.5 women; 6 women = 4 men. Team = 8 men. Job = 6 × 12 = 72 man-days.
**Answer: 9 days**

**95.** A is twice as fast as B. Together they finish in 14 days. How long does A take alone?
Solution: B's rate b, A's 2b. 3b × 14 = 1 → b = 1/42; A's rate = 1/21.
**Answer: 21 days**

**96.** A printer prints 240 pages in 8 minutes. How many pages does it print in 1 hour 15 minutes?
Solution: 30 pages a minute × 75 minutes.
**Answer: 2,250 pages**

**97.** Two guards walk round a building, one taking 18 minutes a round and the other 24. They start together at 8:00. When are they next together at the start?
Solution: LCM(18, 24) = 72 minutes.
**Answer: 9:12**

**98.** A, B and C can do a job alone in 6, 8 and 12 days. They do it together for Rs 9,000. How should the money be shared?
Solution: shares in the ratio of rates 1/6 : 1/8 : 1/12 = 4 : 3 : 2.
**Answer: Rs 4,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 2,000**

## Set H: Speed, distance, time (99–110)

**99.** A car travels 150 km in 2½ hours. Find its speed in km/h and m/s.
Solution: 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 km/h; × 5/18 = 16⅔.
**Answer: 60 km/h; about 16.7 m/s**

**100.** Convert 72 km/h to m/s. How long does a car at this speed take to cover 500 m?
Solution: 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s; 500 ÷ 20.
**Answer: 20 m/s; 25 s**

**101.** A man walks for 45 minutes at 4 km/h and then runs for 30 minutes. He covers 9 km in all. Find his running speed.
Solution: walked 3 km; ran 6 km in 0.5 h.
**Answer: 12 km/h**

**102.** Two trains 300 km apart travel towards each other at 60 km/h and 90 km/h. When do they meet?
Solution: closing speed 150 km/h; 300 ÷ 150.
**Answer: After 2 hours**

**103.** A train 180 m long runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to pass (i) a pole, (ii) a platform 270 m long?
Solution: 54 km/h = 15 m/s. (i) 180 ÷ 15. (ii) (180 + 270) ÷ 15.
**Answer: (i) 12 s (ii) 30 s**

**104.** From the same point, car A goes east at 36 km/h, car B west at 64 km/h and car C north at 48 km/h. Find the distances between each pair after 30 minutes.
Solution: A 18 km, B 32 km, C 24 km. AB = 18 + 32. AC = √(18² + 24²) = √900. BC = √(32² + 24²) = √1600.
**Answer: AB 50 km; AC 30 km; BC 40 km**

**105.** A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/h and the current is 3 km/h. How long does it take to go 45 km downstream and return?
Solution: downstream 15 km/h → 3 h; upstream 9 km/h → 5 h.
**Answer: 8 hours**

**106.** A thief drives off at 40 km/h. A police car follows 30 minutes later at 60 km/h. When and where does it catch him?
Solution: head start 20 km; closing speed 20 km/h → 1 hour; police distance 60 km.
**Answer: 1 hour after the police start; 60 km from the start**

**107.** A man drives to a town at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find his average speed.
Solution: 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60).
**Answer: 48 km/h**

**108.** Walking at 4 km/h a boy reaches school 10 minutes late. Walking at 5 km/h he is 5 minutes early. Find the distance.
Solution: d/4 − d/5 = 15/60 → d/20 = 1/4.
**Answer: 5 km**

**109.** Two runners go round a 400 m track in the same direction at 5 m/s and 3 m/s. After how long does the faster first lap the slower?
Solution: he gains 2 m each second; 400 ÷ 2.
**Answer: 200 s**

**110.** Thunder is heard 6 seconds after the lightning is seen. Sound travels at 340 m/s. How far away was the strike?
Solution: 340 × 6.
**Answer: 2,040 m (about 2 km)**

## Set I: Algebra, equations, sequences (111–125)

**111.** Solve 3(2x − 5) = 4x + 7.
Solution: 6x − 15 = 4x + 7 → 2x = 22.
**Answer: x = 11**

**112.** Solve 2x + 3y = 31 and 3x − y = 8.
Solution: y = 3x − 8. 2x + 9x − 24 = 31 → x = 5; y = 7.
**Answer: x = 5, y = 7**

**113.** Three adult and two child tickets cost Rs 1,900. Two adult and three child tickets cost Rs 1,600. Find each fare.
Solution: add: a + c = 700. Subtract: a − c = 300.
**Answer: Adult Rs 500; child Rs 200**

**114.** Three consecutive even numbers add up to 198. Find them.
Solution: middle = 198 ÷ 3 = 66.
**Answer: 64, 66, 68**

**115.** A father is four times as old as his son. In 6 years their ages will total 62. How old was the father when the son was born?
Solution: s + 4s + 12 = 62 → s = 10; father 40; difference 30.
**Answer: 30 years**

**116.** The digits of a two-digit number add up to 11. Reversing the digits makes the number 27 larger. Find the number.
Solution: t + u = 11. (10u + t) − (10t + u) = 27 → u − t = 3. So t = 4, u = 7.
**Answer: 47** (74 − 47 = 27)

**117.** A farmer has hens and rabbits: 50 heads and 140 legs. How many of each?
Solution: all hens → 100 legs; extra 40 legs → 20 rabbits.
**Answer: 30 hens, 20 rabbits**

**118.** A phone call costs a fixed Rs 5 plus Rs 2.50 a minute. (i) Write the cost C of an N-minute call. (ii) Find the cost of 12 minutes. (iii) How long can one talk for Rs 50?
Solution: (ii) 5 + 30. (iii) (50 − 5) ÷ 2.5.
**Answer: (i) C = 5 + 2.5N (ii) Rs 35 (iii) 18 minutes**

**119.** Solve x² − 5x − 14 = 0.
Solution: (x − 7)(x + 2) = 0.
**Answer: x = 7 or x = −2**

**120.** A rectangle has sides 2x cm and (2x + 1) cm and a diagonal of (3x − 1) cm. Find its area and perimeter.
Solution: 4x² + 4x² + 4x + 1 = 9x² − 6x + 1 → x² − 10x = 0 → x = 10 (x = 0 rejected). Sides 20 and 21; diagonal 29 (400 + 441 = 841 ✓).
**Answer: Area 420 cm²; perimeter 82 cm**

**121.** A rectangle has sides (3x + 2) cm and (2x + 5) cm and a perimeter of 114 cm. Find its area.
Solution: 2(5x + 7) = 114 → x = 10. Sides 32 and 25.
**Answer: 800 cm²**

**122.** An arithmetic sequence starts 7, 11, 15, … Find the 20th term and the sum of the first 20 terms.
Solution: a + 19d = 7 + 76 = 83. Sum = 20/2 × (7 + 83).
**Answer: 83; 900**

**123.** A geometric sequence starts 3, 6, 12, … Find the 10th term and the sum of the first 10 terms.
Solution: 3 × 2⁹ = 3 × 512. Sum = 3(2¹⁰ − 1) = 3 × 1,023.
**Answer: 1,536; 3,069**

**124.** Find the equation of the line through (1, 3) and (4, 9), its slope and its intercepts.
Solution: m = (9 − 3)/(4 − 1) = 2. y − 3 = 2(x − 1) → y = 2x + 1. x-intercept: 0 = 2x + 1.
**Answer: y = 2x + 1; slope 2; y-intercept 1; x-intercept −½**

**125.** A shop gives one free chocolate for every 5 wrappers returned. A boy buys 125 chocolates. How many free chocolates can he get in all?
Solution: 125 wrappers → 25 free; 25 wrappers → 5 free; 5 wrappers → 1 free.
**Answer: 31 free chocolates** (156 eaten in total)

## Set J: Geometry and mensuration (126–140)

**126.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.
Solution: 9 parts = 180°; one part = 20°.
**Answer: 40°, 60°, 80°**

**127.** Find (i) each interior angle of a regular octagon, (ii) the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon, (iii) each exterior angle of a regular decagon.
Solution: (i) 6 × 180/8. (ii) 4 × 180. (iii) 360/10.
**Answer: (i) 135° (ii) 720° (iii) 36°**

**128.** A 13 m ladder leans on a wall with its foot 5 m from the wall. How high does it reach?
Solution: √(13² − 5²) = √144.
**Answer: 12 m**

**129.** A man walks 3 km north, 4 km east, 3 km north and 4 km east. How far is he from the start?
Solution: 6 km north, 8 km east; √(36 + 64).
**Answer: 10 km**

**130.** A 1.8 m man casts a 2.4 m shadow. At the same time a tree casts a 16 m shadow. Find the height of the tree.
Solution: similar triangles: h/16 = 1.8/2.4 = 3/4.
**Answer: 12 m**

**131.** A man 1.6 m tall stands 6 m from a lamp post 4 m high. Find the length of his shadow.
Solution: 1.6/4 = s/(s + 6) → 4s = 1.6s + 9.6 → 2.4s = 9.6.
**Answer: 4 m**

**132.** Find the circumference and area of a circle of radius 14 cm.
Solution: 2 × 22/7 × 14; 22/7 × 196.
**Answer: 88 cm; 616 cm²**

**133.** A path 2 m wide runs round the outside of a lawn 30 m by 20 m. Find the area of the path. How many 30 cm square tiles will pave it?
Solution: outer 34 × 24 = 816; lawn 600; path 216 m². Tile = 0.09 m²; 216 ÷ 0.09.
**Answer: 216 m²; 2,400 tiles**

**134.** Right-angled triangular tiles with legs 20 cm and 10 cm cost Rs 25 each. Find the cost of tiling a floor 5 m by 4 m.
Solution: tile area = 100 cm². Floor = 500 × 400 = 200,000 cm². Tiles = 2,000.
**Answer: Rs 50,000**

**135.** A solid cuboid has a base 12 cm by 5 cm and a total surface area of 392 cm². Find its height and volume.
Solution: 2(60 + 12h + 5h) = 392 → 17h = 136 → h = 8. Volume = 12 × 5 × 8.
**Answer: 8 cm; 480 cm³**

**136.** Find the surface area and volume of a sphere of radius 21 cm.
Solution: 4 × 22/7 × 441 = 5,544. 4/3 × 22/7 × 9,261 = 38,808.
**Answer: 5,544 cm²; 38,808 cm³**

**137.** A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume, curved surface and total surface.
Solution: 22/7 × 49 × 10; 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10; 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 17.
**Answer: 1,540 cm³; 440 cm²; 748 cm²**

**138.** A pyramid has a square base of side 6 m and a height of 4 m. Find its volume and the total area of its four sloping faces.
Solution: volume = ⅓ × 36 × 4. Slant height = √(4² + 3²) = 5. Faces = 4 × ½ × 6 × 5.
**Answer: 48 m³; 60 m²**

**139.** A square plot has an area of 625 m². Walls 2 m high are built on three sides and a wall 4 m high on the fourth. Find the total wall area.
Solution: side 25 m. 3 × 25 × 2 + 25 × 4 = 150 + 100.
**Answer: 250 m²**

**140.** Find the area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm.
Solution: s = 21. Area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7,056.
**Answer: 84 cm²**

## Set K: Statistics, sets, probability (141–150)

**141.** Find the mean, median, mode and range of 12, 15, 11, 15, 18, 13, 15, 21.
Solution: sum 120; mean 15. Ordered: 11, 12, 13, 15, 15, 15, 18, 21; median = (15 + 15)/2. Mode 15. Range 21 − 11.
**Answer: Mean 15; median 15; mode 15; range 10**

**142.** Find the standard deviation of 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.
Solution: mean 5. Squared deviations 9, 1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 4, 16 → sum 32. Variance 32/8 = 4.
**Answer: 2**

**143.** Marks of 20 students: 5 (2 students), 6 (5), 7 (8), 8 (4), 9 (1). Find the mean, median and mode.
Solution: Σfx = 10 + 30 + 56 + 32 + 9 = 137; mean = 137/20. The 10th and 11th values are both 7. Highest frequency is at 7.
**Answer: Mean 6.85; median 7; mode 7**

**144.** U = {1, 2, …, 20}; A = multiples of 3; B = even numbers. Find n(A ∩ B) and n(A ∪ B).
Solution: A ∩ B = {6, 12, 18}. n(A) = 6, n(B) = 10. n(A ∪ B) = 6 + 10 − 3.
**Answer: 3; 13**

**145.** Of 100 students, 60 like cricket, 45 like hockey and 20 like both. How many like neither? How many like only cricket?
Solution: at least one = 60 + 45 − 20 = 85. Neither = 15. Only cricket = 60 − 20.
**Answer: 15; 40**

**146.** In a survey of 100 people, 45 drink tea, 40 coffee and 30 juice; 15 drink tea and coffee, 12 coffee and juice, 10 tea and juice; 5 drink all three. How many drink none?
Solution: at least one = 45 + 40 + 30 − 15 − 12 − 10 + 5 = 83.
**Answer: 17**

**147.** A card is drawn from cards numbered 1 to 20. Find the probability of (i) a prime, (ii) a multiple of 3 or 5, (iii) a perfect square.
Solution: (i) primes 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19 → 8. (ii) multiples of 3: 6; of 5: 4; of 15: 1 → 9. (iii) 1, 4, 9, 16 → 4.
**Answer: (i) 2/5 (ii) 9/20 (iii) 1/5**

**148.** Two dice are thrown. Find the probability of (i) a total of 8, (ii) a double, (iii) a total of 10 or more.
Solution: 36 outcomes. (i) (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2). (ii) 6 doubles. (iii) totals 10, 11, 12: 3 + 2 + 1.
**Answer: (i) 5/36 (ii) 1/6 (iii) 1/6**

**149.** A bag has 5 red, 4 blue and 3 green balls. Two are drawn without replacement. Find the probability that (i) both are red, (ii) one is red and one is blue.
Solution: (i) 5/12 × 4/11 = 20/132. (ii) 2 × 5/12 × 4/11 = 40/132.
**Answer: (i) 5/33 (ii) 10/33**

**150.** A team of 10 has 4 engineers and 6 others. A random sample of 3 is chosen. Find the probability of (i) exactly 2 engineers, (ii) no engineers.
Solution: C(10, 3) = 120. (i) C(4, 2) × C(6, 1) = 6 × 6 = 36. (ii) C(6, 3) = 20.
**Answer: (i) 3/10 (ii) 1/6**

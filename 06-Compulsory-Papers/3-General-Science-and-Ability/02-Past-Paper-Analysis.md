## Trends

1. **2026 was harder and more analytical.** Science parts used "evaluate" and joined two ideas in one part (for example lipids plus membrane fluidity). Ability had argument analysis, a decision tree, a hypergeometric probability and a remainder problem. Expect this style to continue.
2. **Syllabus words are now quoted directly.** 2026 used "Decision Support Systems", "social media", "disaster risk management", "avalanche", "rounding", "remainders", "random sample", "mechanical". Rare syllabus items are now live.
3. **IT is steady at about three parts a year** and is the candidate's home ground.
4. **Off-syllabus items appear almost every year** (mirage, AC/DC, ear, TB, Kepler, fossils, biotechnology, forests, radioactivity). See `03-Notes/08-Beyond-Syllabus-Extras.md`.
5. **Some ability questions are flawed or ambiguous.** Examples: 2026 Q7(a) puts someone "opposite" in a circle of five; 2026 Q7(b) only works if the ratio is read as chemical to water. State your assumption and solve.
6. **Pakistan angle.** Several parts ask about Pakistan (waterlogging 2017, climate effects 2022, solid waste 2022, energy prospects 2021).

# Resources: The Few Worth Using

This pack is meant to be enough on its own for 70+. Use outside sources for three jobs only:
1. Getting the **official past papers** to practise on.
2. **Filling a gap** when a topic in the notes does not make sense to you.
3. **Updating facts** that change (disease numbers, climate data, Pakistan's energy and population figures).

Do not buy more than two books for this paper.

I have not checked current editions or prices of the books below. Check the latest edition before buying.

---

## 1. Official sources (free, essential)

| Source | What to take | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| FPSC syllabus: [Revised Scheme and Syllabus, CE-2016 onwards](https://fpsc.gov.pk/assets/media/2024-10-22-01-45-26-Syllabus_for_CE-2016_and_onwards.pdf) | Pages 7–9: General Science & Ability | Print the two pages. Keep them with `01-Syllabus-Map.md`. Check each year whether FPSC has revised it. |
| FPSC past papers: [fpsc.gov.pk](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk) → CSS → Past Papers | Papers 2016–2026, including the MCQ sheets | Download 2022–2026 for timed practice in January 2028. I could read only the 2026 MCQ sheet; collect the others and look for repeats. |
| FPSC examiners' reports (same site, under CSS) | The paragraph on GK-I (General Science & Ability) for each year | Read all available years once. They say what loses marks. |
| FPSC rules for the current year | Any change in paper format or calculator rule | Check in the month you apply. |

If the FPSC site will not open, the same papers are mirrored on [cssforum.com.pk](https://www.cssforum.com.pk) and [cssprep.com.pk](https://cssprep.com.pk/css-general-science-ability-past-papers-desc/).

## 2. Books

### Pick one CSS guide (optional)

| Book | Good for | Weakness | How to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| A recent one-volume CSS guide to General Science & Ability from a mainstream Pakistani publisher (for example the guides by Mian Shafiq, by Akram Kashmiri, or the Jahangir's World Times "To the Point" volume) | Solved past papers in one place; local examples; MCQ lists | Uneven accuracy; long, memorised-style answers; some out-of-date facts | Use for the solved papers and extra MCQs only. Do not read cover to cover. Where it disagrees with this pack or with a textbook, check a primary source. |

### Pick one reasoning book (optional, for extra practice)

| Book | Good for | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| R. S. Aggarwal, *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning* | Thousands of graded items on series, coding, blood relations, direction, seating, syllogism | Do 20 items a week from the chapters where you scored lowest in `04-General-Ability/03`. Skip chapters that never appear (cubes, paper folding in depth). |
| R. S. Aggarwal, *Quantitative Aptitude* | Extra arithmetic drill | Only if you fall below 90% on the 150 problems here. You probably will not need it. |
| Edgar Thorpe, *Test of Reasoning* (on the FPSC reading list) | Same ground as Aggarwal | An alternative, not an addition. |

### School textbooks (free, and the best gap-fillers)

| Book | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Punjab or Federal Textbook Board **Biology, Class 9–10** | Cell, biomolecules, enzymes, human physiology, classification. The exam's biology is at this level. |
| **Chemistry, Class 9–10** | Atomic structure, bonding, environmental chemistry (air and water) |
| **Physics, Class 9–10** | Waves, EM spectrum, electricity, basic electronics, information technology chapter |
| **General Science, Class 8** | A fast refresher if a whole area feels unfamiliar |

They are free on the textbook boards' websites and e-learning portals. Read a chapter only when a section of the notes does not click. One chapter takes about an hour.

### The FPSC "suggested readings"

The official list (Asimov's *New Guide to Science*, Strahler's *Physical Geography*, Potter's *Food Science*, Cunningham's *Environmental Science*, Freeman's *Fundamentals of Telecommunications* and others) is far too large for a 100-mark paper. Nobody reads them for CSS. Use one only as a reference if a library has it. Cunningham's *Environmental Science: A Global Concern* is the most useful of them, because environment is the most repeated section.

## 3. Free online sources

### For understanding

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org) | Short videos on the cell, digestion, circulation, bonding, EM waves. Search the exact topic; watch at 1.5× speed. |
| [OpenStax](https://openstax.org) free textbooks: *Concepts of Biology*, *Astronomy*, *Chemistry* | A clear chapter with diagrams when the school book is not enough. The diagrams are good models to copy. |
| [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov) | Solar system, Sun, black holes, eclipses, climate indicators |
| [USGS Earthquake Hazards](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards) and [NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov) education pages | Earthquakes, tsunamis, hurricanes, tornadoes |
| [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com) | A reliable short article on any single topic |

### For current, checkable facts

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [WHO fact sheets](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets) | Polio, malaria, dengue, hepatitis, diarrhoeal disease, TB, air pollution, drinking water. Update the case numbers in your notes once, in January 2028. |
| [Global Polio Eradication Initiative](https://polioeradication.org) and Pakistan's polio programme site | Latest Pakistan case count |
| [UNEP Ozone Secretariat](https://ozone.unep.org) | Montreal Protocol, Kigali Amendment, recovery of the ozone layer |
| [UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int) and [IPCC](https://www.ipcc.ch) summaries for policymakers | Kyoto, Paris, latest warming figures |
| [NDMA Pakistan](https://www.ndma.gov.pk) | Disaster management structure, flood reports |
| Pakistan Economic Survey ([finance.gov.pk](https://www.finance.gov.pk)), chapters on Energy, Environment/Climate Change, Population, Health | Pakistan's energy mix, forest cover, population and health figures. One evening each June when the new survey comes out. |
| Pakistan Bureau of Statistics | Census figures |
| [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com) science, climate and health reporting | Examples for answers. You already read the editorial page daily; clip anything on smog, floods, polio, dengue, energy or AI into a one-page "GSA examples" note. |

### For past-paper solutions

| Source | Caution |
|---|---|
| [cssprepforum.com solved papers](https://cssprepforum.com/css-solved-general-science-and-ability-past-paper-2026/) (2016–2026) | Useful to see every question in one place. Answers are long and sometimes wrong in the maths. Solve first, then compare. |
| [cssforum.com.pk](https://www.cssforum.com.pk) General Science & Ability threads | Good for discussion of ambiguous questions. Quality varies. |

## 4. What to skip

- Thick "everyday science" MCQ books written for the pre-2016 syllabus. Much of their content (inventions, scientific instruments lists, units trivia) is no longer on the syllabus.
- Coaching-academy notes with memorised essays. A 5-mark part needs 150 words and a diagram.
- University-level texts for any topic.
- YouTube "guess papers".

---

## 5. A minimal kit

1. This folder.
2. The official past papers 2016–2026, printed.
3. A pencil, ruler and eraser for diagrams; practise with them from the start.
4. One school biology textbook (PDF).
5. One reasoning book, only if your score on the 100 reasoning problems is under 85.

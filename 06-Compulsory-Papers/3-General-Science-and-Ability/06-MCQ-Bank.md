Paper format reminder: 20 MCQs, 1 mark each, 30 minutes, OMR sheet, no negative marking. In 2026 all 20 came from Part I (science and IT). A block of ability MCQs is included here for practice and for the screening test (MPT).

How to use:
- Do one section at a sitting, timed at 45 seconds a question.
- Cover the answer key at the end of each section. Mark yourself, then reread the notes for every miss.
- Do the whole bank twice: once after the first pass of the notes, once in the last month.
- These questions are original. They follow the style and level of the 2026 paper. Every fact was checked against the notes in `03-Notes/`.

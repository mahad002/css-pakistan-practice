# Syllabus Map: General Science & Ability

Source: FPSC "Revised Scheme and Syllabus for CSS Competitive Examination" (CE-2016 onwards), pages 7–9. Topic names below follow the official list. Read from a copy of the FPSC pages hosted at csspastpapers.pk; the full official PDF is at [fpsc.gov.pk](https://fpsc.gov.pk/assets/media/2024-10-22-01-45-26-Syllabus_for_CE-2016_and_onwards.pdf).

Frequency = how often the topic appeared in the written (Part II) questions of the 11 papers from 2016 to 2026.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Asked in 5 or more of 11 papers |
| **Medium** | Asked in 3–4 papers |
| **Low** | Asked in 1–2 papers |
| **None** | Not asked in a written question 2016–2026 (may still appear as an MCQ) |

Notes files are in `03-Notes/`. Short names used below:
PS-A = `01-Physical-Sciences-A-Universe-and-Earth.md`, PS-B = `02-Physical-Sciences-B-Hazards-and-Disasters.md`, PS-C = `03-Physical-Sciences-C-Energy-Atoms-Materials.md`, BIO = `04-Biological-Sciences.md`, ENV = `05-Environmental-Science.md`, FOOD = `06-Food-Science.md`, IT = `07-Information-Technology.md`, EXTRA = `08-Beyond-Syllabus-Extras.md`.

Tick the box when you can write the 5-mark answer from memory.

## Priority order (study the High rows first)

1. Fibre optics; GPS; AI; computer basics.
2. Energy sources (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, non-renewable).
3. Cyclones, tornadoes, earthquakes; then the other hazards and DRM.
4. Water pollution, air pollution and smog, greenhouse effect, ozone, acid rain, atmosphere layers, water cycle.
5. Balanced diet, vitamins, food deterioration and preservation, preservatives and antioxidants.
6. Heart and circulation, digestion and liver, cell, carbohydrates and lipids, hepatitis, dengue, polio.
7. EM spectrum, bonding, radioactivity, fertilizers, pesticides, vaccines, antibiotics.
8. Universe, solar system, eclipses, rotation and revolution.
9. All of Part II. Every topic there is worth learning because the questions are computational.

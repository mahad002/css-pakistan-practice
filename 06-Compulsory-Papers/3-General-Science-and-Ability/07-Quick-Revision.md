## Sheet 7: Formula sheet for Section B

**Percentages**
- x% of N = Nx/100. Change % = (new − old)/old × 100.
- After a rise of x%: × (1 + x/100). After a fall: × (1 − x/100).
- **Reverse:** original = final ÷ (1 ± x/100).
- Successive a% and b%: net = a + b + ab/100.

**Profit and loss**
- Profit % = profit/CP × 100. SP = CP × (1 + g/100). CP = SP ÷ (1 + g/100).
- Discount is on marked price: SP = MP × (1 − d/100).

**Ratio**
- Share = total × own part ÷ sum of parts.
- Partnership: capital × time.
- Inheritance: debts first; widow 1/8 (with children); son : daughter = 2 : 1.

**Averages**
- Sum = average × number. New member = new total − old total.
- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time; equal distances: 2ab/(a + b).

**Interest**
- SI = PRT/100. A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ.

**Work and rates**
- Rate = 1/time. Together = ab/(a + b). Men × days = constant.

**Speed**
- d = s × t. km/h × 5/18 = m/s. Opposite: add speeds. Same direction: subtract.
- Train: length ÷ speed (pole); (train + platform) ÷ speed.

**Algebra**
- x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)]/2a. (a ± b)² = a² ± 2ab + b². a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b).
- Line: y = mx + c; m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).
- AP: a + (n − 1)d; sum n/2 (first + last). GP: arⁿ⁻¹; sum a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1).
- 1 + 2 + … + n = n(n + 1)/2. First n odd numbers sum to n².

**Geometry**
- Triangle angles 180°. Polygon interior sum (n − 2) × 180°. Exterior sum 360°.
- Pythagoras: a² + b² = c². Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.
- Areas: rectangle lw; triangle ½bh; circle πr²; trapezium ½(a + b)h.
- Circle circumference 2πr.
- Cuboid: V = lwh; S = 2(lw + wh + lh). Cylinder: V = πr²h; curved 2πrh.
- Cone: V = ⅓πr²h. Sphere: V = 4/3 πr³; S = 4πr². Pyramid: V = ⅓ base × height.
- Regular n-gon has n lines of symmetry; circle has infinitely many.

**Statistics, sets, probability**
- Mean = Σx/n; median = middle; mode = most frequent; range = max − min; SD = √[Σ(x − mean)²/n].
- n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B).
- P = favourable/total; P(not A) = 1 − P(A); independent: multiply; either/or (exclusive): add.
- C(n, r) = n!/[r!(n − r)!].
- Systematic sampling interval k = N/n.

**Numbers**
- LCM for "together again"; HCF for "largest equal piece". HCF × LCM = product.
- N = divisor × quotient + remainder.
- Absolute error = |approximate − exact|.

**Reasoning reminders**
- Draw the compass. Facing north: right = east. Facing south: right = west.
- Round table facing centre: right = anticlockwise.
- Coding: try a letter shift; if it fails, reverse the word first.
- Position: total = from left + from right − 1.
- Gears: opposite directions; teeth × turns equal.
- Clock mirror image: subtract from 11:60. Angle = |30H − 5.5M|.
- Series: differences → ratios → squares or cubes → two alternating rules.
- Argument: premise, conclusion, hidden assumption; name the fallacy; give a counter-example.

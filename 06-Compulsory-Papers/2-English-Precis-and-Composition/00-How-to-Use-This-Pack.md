# How to Use This Pack

**Paper:** English (Précis and Composition), CSS Competitive Examination (FPSC)
**Marks:** 100. **Time:** 3 hours. **Pass mark:** 40.
**Target sitting:** CSS 2028 (written exam expected February 2028).
**Pack written:** 23 November 2026. Latest paper analysed: CSS 2026 (February 2026).

Failing this one paper fails the whole written exam, whatever your total. Treat it as a skills paper. You cannot cram it. You pass it by writing a small amount every week for a year.

## 2. Paper format (confirmed from the 2019–2026 papers)

The official syllabus and the real paper do not match exactly. The table below shows what the real paper has looked like. Details and sources are in `10-Past-Paper-Analysis.md`.

| Part | Q. no. | Task | Items given | Items to attempt | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I (MCQ, OMR sheet, first 30 minutes) | 1(a) | Synonyms: pick the word closest in meaning | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| I | 1(b) | Antonyms: pick the word most opposite in meaning | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| II (answer book, 2 hours 30 minutes) | 2 | Précis of a passage, with a title | 1 passage | 1 | 20 (syllabus split: 15 précis + 5 title) |
| II | 3 | Reading comprehension | 1 passage, 5 questions | 5 | 20 (4 each) |
| II | 4 | Sentence correction | 8 sentences | 5 | 10 (2 each) |
| II | 5(a) | Punctuation | 1 short passage (or 8 sentences) | all (or 5) | 5 |
| II | 5(b) | Fill in prepositions | 8 sentences | 5 | 5 (1 each) |
| II | 6 | Pairs of words: use both words of each pair in sentences. In 2019 and 2020 this slot was idioms instead. | 8 | 5 | 10 (2 each) |
| II | 7 | Translation, Urdu to English | 1 paragraph (once: 8 sentences, attempt 5) | 1 | 10 |
| | | **Total** | | | **100** |

Points to remember:

- Part I is taken away after 30 minutes. You cannot go back to it.
- Every MCQ is one mark. No negative marking is stated on the paper.
- In Q4, Q5(b) and Q6 you choose 5 of 8. Extra attempts are not marked. Pick the five you are sure of.
- Précis and comprehension together are 40 marks. That is the pass mark on its own. They are also where most marks are lost.
- Older papers (2016–2018) split Q4, Q5 and Q6 into 5-mark halves and added analogies, a dialogue to rewrite in indirect speech, and idioms. Those types may return. They are covered in `09-Other-Question-Types.md`.

### What the syllabus says (CE-2016 onwards)

| Syllabus section | Marks | Seen in real papers 2016–2026? |
|---|---|---|
| I. Précis writing (15 for the précis, 5 for the title) | 20 | Yes, every year |
| II. Reading comprehension (5 questions, 4 marks each) | 20 | Yes, every year (2016 used 3 questions of 5 marks plus a 5-mark vocabulary item) |
| III. Grammar and vocabulary (tense, articles, prepositions, conjunctions, punctuation, phrasal verbs, synonyms, antonyms) | 20 | Yes: the MCQ part is synonyms and antonyms; prepositions and punctuation appear in Q5 |
| IV. Sentence correction | 10 | Yes, every year |
| V. Grouping of words (20 words to sort into pairs of similar or opposite meaning) | 10 | **Not seen as a question in any paper I could check from 2016 to 2026** |
| VI. Pairs of words (10 pairs, explain and use any 5) | 10 | Yes, most years (8 pairs given, 5 attempted) |
| VII. Translation (ten short Urdu sentences) | 10 | Yes, every year, but almost always as one paragraph |

The syllabus text could only be read through secondary copies, because the FPSC site would not load (see `COVERAGE.md`).

## 3. Time plan for the 3 hours

| Time | Minutes | Task | Marks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | 30 | Part I: 20 MCQs | 20 | You will finish in 10–12 minutes. If Part II is printed on the same question paper (it usually is), use the spare minutes to read the précis and comprehension passages. Never leave an MCQ blank. |
| 0:30–0:35 | 5 | Read all of Part II. Tick the five items you will attempt in Q4, Q5(b), Q6 | – | Decide the order now |
| 0:35–1:15 | 40 | Q2 Précis | 20 | Read 8 min, plan 7 min, draft 12 min, count and fair copy 13 min |
| 1:15–1:45 | 30 | Q3 Comprehension | 20 | 6 minutes per answer |
| 1:45–2:07 | 22 | Q7 Translation | 10 | Read the whole paragraph before you write |
| 2:07–2:20 | 13 | Q4 Sentence correction | 10 | Rewrite the full sentence. Change only what is wrong |
| 2:20–2:35 | 15 | Q5 Punctuation and prepositions | 10 | Rewrite the passage neatly once |
| 2:35–2:50 | 15 | Q6 Pairs of words (or idioms) | 10 | Meaning in brackets, then one clear sentence each |
| 2:50–3:00 | 10 | Check | – | Précis word count, title, question numbers, spelling |

### Order of attempting

1. **Précis first**, while you are fresh. It needs the most thought and is the largest single block of marks.
2. **Comprehension second.** You are already in reading mode.
3. **Translation third.** It is also a thinking task. Do not leave it for the last ten minutes.
4. **Then the short questions** (Q4, Q5, Q6). These are quick and mechanical. If you run out of time here, you lose a mark or two, not ten.

You may answer in any order, but write the question number clearly and start each question on a new page. Keep all parts of one question together.

An alternative some candidates use: do the short questions first to "bank" 30 marks. Do not do this until you have tested it in mocks. In practice it eats into précis time, and the précis suffers most from hurry.

| The government told the National Assembly in May 2026 that a significant number of candidates fail the two English papers, and blamed weak academic preparation, poor analysis, low comprehension and reliance on coaching academies. Pass marks (FPSC CE Rules 2019): 40 in this paper, Essay and Islamic Studies; the three GK papers together 120/300; 33% in each optional; 50% aggregate. | Dawn, 17 May 2026: https://www.dawn.com/news/2000784 |

What this means in practice:

| Cause of lost marks | What to do about it | File |
|---|---|---|
| Précis copies sentences from the passage, is too long, has no title, or adds opinions | Learn the method. Write one précis a week for a year. Count words every time | `01` |
| Précis written in the first person or in mixed tenses | Third person, one tense. Checklist before the fair copy | `01` |
| Comprehension answers copied word for word from the passage | Answer in your own words, in full sentences, 3–5 sentences each | `02` |
| "Correcting" things that were right in Q4 | Change only the error. Learn the 12 error families | `03` |
| Capital letters and full stops missed in punctuation | Follow the fixed order of passes in `04` | `04` |
| Vocabulary MCQs with rare words (2021 and 2025 were very hard) | Do not chase rare words. Learn 500 well, use elimination, expect 10–14 out of 20 | `05` |
| Pairs of words: wrong spelling attached to the wrong meaning | Learn each pair with a memory hook and your own sentence | `07` |
| Translation done word by word | Translate sense by sense. Learn tense mapping and common idioms | `08` |
| Poor handwriting and no layout | Blue or black pen, margins, one question per page, legible fair copy | – |
| Running out of time | Five timed mocks before the exam | `11` |

## 5. A realistic score target

You need 40. Aim for 55–60 so that a bad précis does not sink you. Marking of the précis and comprehension is known to be strict.

| Question | Max | Safe target | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCQs | 20 | 11 | 500 words learned well, plus elimination |
| Précis | 20 | 9 | Correct length, own words, clear title, no errors |
| Comprehension | 20 | 10 | Own words, direct answers |
| Sentence correction | 10 | 7 | Choose the five you are sure of |
| Punctuation | 5 | 4 | Mechanical with practice |
| Prepositions | 5 | 4 | Choose five of eight |
| Pairs of words or idioms | 10 | 6 | Choose five of eight |
| Translation | 10 | 5 | Complete, accurate, plain English |
| **Total** | **100** | **56** | |

The short questions (Q4–Q6) are worth 30 marks and can be scored at 70–80% with drill. That is where a software engineer with good general English picks up easy marks. The précis and translation are the two skills you have not practised. Give them the most hours.

## 6. How to work through the pack

- **Daily (20 minutes):** vocabulary cards from `05`, using the spaced-repetition schedule there, then idioms or pairs on alternate days.
- **Weekly (about 3.5 hours of your 15, including the daily cards):** one précis, one translation paragraph, one block of grammar or punctuation. `11-Practice-Plan.md` gives the exact routine for each week.
- **Always write by hand and time yourself.** The exam is handwritten. Typing hides slow handwriting and spelling slips.
- **Mark your own work against the model.** Use the rubric in `01`. Keep everything in `02-Practice-and-Tracking/My-Precis/` in the main CSS folder, dated, so you can see progress.
- **Keep an error log.** One line per mistake: the wrong form, the right form, the rule. Re-read it every Sunday. Most candidates repeat the same ten mistakes.
- **Model answers are one good answer, not the only answer.** For précis and translation there are many acceptable versions. Compare ideas covered, length, and accuracy, not exact wording.

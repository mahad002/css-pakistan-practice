This file describes what each paper tested. It does not reproduce passages. Short items (idioms, word pairs, single words) are listed because they are the repeating stock you must learn.

### 2016

| Question | What it tested |
|---|---|
| MCQ words (as listed by cssprep.com.pk) | quiescent, nabob, sartorial, pall, matutinal, sacrosanct, impecunious, louche, sapient, stentorian, loquacious, chimera, temerity, propinquity, vociferous, germane, irascible, approbate, supercilious, fatuous |

## 4. Repeat patterns

### 4.1 Format

| Feature | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Overall structure | Fixed since 2019. Seven questions, same marks |
| Q6 | Idioms in 2019 and 2020; pairs of words in every paper from 2021 to 2026 (seven papers) |
| Punctuation | A passage in eight papers; separate sentences in 2016, 2018, 2023 Special, 2024 |
| Translation | A paragraph in eleven papers; separate sentences once (2023 Special) |
| Précis word limit | Stated in 2016 and 2018 (about 120), and reportedly in 2026 (100). Otherwise not stated |
| Analogies, dialogue to paragraph | 2016–2018 only |
| Grouping of words | Never |
| Choice | Always 5 of 8 in corrections, prepositions, and pairs or idioms |

### 4.2 Difficulty

| Level | Years |
|---|---|
| Easy to moderate | 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024, 2026 |
| Hard in parts | 2016, 2017, 2018, 2022 |
| Very hard | 2021 (MCQs, comprehension), 2025 (everything) |

There is no steady upward trend. Hard papers are followed by fair ones.

### 4.3 Items that repeated

| Kind | Repeats in 2016–2026 |
|---|---|
| Pairs | faint/feint (2018, 2023, 2024); dinghy/dingy (2017, 2018); veracity/voracity (2017, 2023 Special); allude/elude (2016, 2023 Special); moat/mote (2016, 2026) |
| Pairs repeated from before 2016 | fain/feign, hoard/horde, ingenious/ingenuous, luxuriant/luxurious (all in 2026); compliment/complement, eminent/imminent, immigrant/emigrant, momentary/momentous, antic/antique (all in 2023); eligible/illegible (2023 Special); adverse/averse (2016); born/borne, lose/loose (2018) |
| MCQ words | loquacious, temerity, propinquity, vociferous, germane, levitate, inception, assuage, rancour, panacea, anomaly/consistency |
| MCQ word also used in a pair | defenestration (MCQ 2021, pair 2025); wreak (MCQ 2022, pair 2024); venial (MCQ 2017; venal/venial is an old pair) |
| Preposition chunks | responsible for; bound for; apply to... for; put off; give in to; by heart; of one's own accord; similar to; familiar with |
| Correction types | In most papers: subject–verb agreement, tense and sequence of tenses, a wrong or unnecessary preposition. In about half: pronoun case, articles, dangling or misplaced modifiers. Several times: "unless / without / whether", indirect question word order, double negatives, "too... to" |
| Idioms | No idiom repeated within 2015–2020 |

### 4.4 Passages

| Question | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Précis | Usually 240–400 words. Reflective or argumentative prose on education, society, culture, work, cities, or a literary or historical figure. One personal narrative (2016). Several are taken from twentieth-century essayists or from textbooks of précis passages |
| Comprehension | Alternates between expository (2018, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2025) and narrative or literary extracts (2016, 2019, 2026). Political commentary in 2021 |
| Translation | National and Muslim-world themes (2018, 2019, 2021, 2024, 2025, 2026), moral reflection (2016, 2017, 2022), education (2020), fable (2023) |

## 5. Predictions for CSS 2028

These are estimates from eleven years of papers. Treat them as a guide for allocating effort, not as a forecast to rely on.

| Item | Estimate | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Format unchanged (seven questions, 20/20/20/10/10/10/10) | Very likely | Stable for eight consecutive regular papers |
| Q6 is pairs of words | Likely (about 4 in 5) | Seven papers in a row |
| Q6 is idioms, or split pairs and idioms | Possible (about 1 in 5) | Happened in 2016–2020 |
| At least two of the eight pairs come from earlier papers | Very likely | True in 2023, 2023 Special, 2024, 2026 |
| At least three pairs are plain homophones | Likely | True in 2022, 2024, 2026 |
| MCQs at standard advanced level, not the 2025 level | More likely than not | 2025-type years (2021, 2025) have each been followed by fair years |
| Précis passage of 250–350 words on a social, educational or cultural theme, no word limit or a limit near one-third | Likely | The norm outside 2025 |
| Comprehension is expository | Slightly more likely than narrative | 2026 was narrative; the type tends to alternate |
| Punctuation is a passage with some dialogue | Likely | Eight of twelve papers used a passage |
| Translation is a paragraph on a national, Muslim-world or moral theme | Very likely | Eleven of twelve |
| Sentence correction includes agreement, tense, a modifier error and a preposition error, with two-error sentences | Very likely | Every recent paper |
| Grouping of words appears | Unlikely, but prepare one hour for it | In the syllabus; never set |
| Analogies or dialogue-to-paragraph return | Unlikely | Not seen since 2018 |

## 6. What to do with this analysis

1. Learn every pair in Section A of `07-Pairs-of-Words.md` and every idiom in Section A of `06-Idioms-and-Phrasal-Verbs.md` first.
2. Learn the preposition chunks in section 1.1 of `09-Other-Question-Types.md`.
3. Practise précis on passages of 250–400 words, and once or twice on a 600-word passage in case 2025 repeats.
4. In comprehension, practise both expository and narrative passages. `02-Comprehension.md` has six of the first and two of the second.
5. In months 9–12, obtain the real papers for 2022–2026 and sit two of them under exam conditions in addition to the five mocks.

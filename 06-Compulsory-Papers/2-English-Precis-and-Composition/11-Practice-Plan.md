# Practice Plan: 12 Months

**Period:** Monday 1 February 2027 to Sunday 30 January 2028 (52 weeks). The written exam is expected in February 2028.
**Your total study time:** about 15 hours a week for all papers.
**Share for this paper:** about 3.5 hours a week in phases 1, 2 and 4; about 2.5 hours in phase 3.

If the FPSC schedule shifts, slide the phases. Keep the order.

---

## 1. The fixed weekly routine

| When | Minutes | What |
|---|---|---|
| Every day | 20 | Cards: 12 minutes of vocabulary (`05`, spaced repetition), then 8 minutes of idioms and phrasal verbs (`06`) or pairs of words (`07`), on alternate days |
| One weekday evening | 30 | The week's short drill (third column of the tables below) |
| Weekend | 75 | The week's main task (first column) for 40 minutes by hand, under the clock; then 15 minutes marking it against the model; then the second task |
| Sunday | 10 | Read your error log. Do the vocabulary self-test of 20 cards |

Rules:

1. Handwrite every précis, translation and comprehension answer. Time it.
2. Never read a model answer before writing your own.
3. Keep all attempts, dated, in one notebook or in `02-Practice-and-Tracking` in the parent folder.
4. A missed week is not made up by doing two précis in one sitting. Move on and keep the rhythm.
5. In a week with less time, keep the daily cards and the weekend précis or translation. Drop the drill.

## 3. The five mock papers

Each mock is a full paper in the exam's format, written for this pack, with an answer key and model answers after the paper. They are in `11-Mock-Papers/`.

| Mock | File | Q6 | Punctuation form | Translation form | Sit in week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `11-Mock-Papers/Mock-1.md` | Pairs of words | Passage | Paragraph | 26 |
| 2 | `11-Mock-Papers/Mock-2.md` | Idioms | Passage with dialogue | Paragraph | 40 |
| 3 | `11-Mock-Papers/Mock-3.md` | Pairs of words | Eight sentences, rewrite five | Paragraph | 43 |
| 4 | `11-Mock-Papers/Mock-4.md` | Idioms | Passage | Eight sentences, translate five | 46 |
| 5 | `11-Mock-Papers/Mock-5.md` | Pairs of words (harder) | Passage | Paragraph | 49 |

The mocks draw their MCQ words, pairs and idioms from the lists in this pack, weighted towards past-paper items. That is deliberate: it tests whether the lists have been learned. The real paper will contain some words that are in no list.

### How to sit a mock

1. Print the paper pages only (everything above the line "ANSWER KEY"). Do not look at the key.
2. Three hours, one sitting, by hand, on lined paper. Phone in another room.
3. First 30 minutes: Part I only. Then put it aside and do not return to it.
4. Follow the time plan in `00-How-to-Use-This-Pack.md`, section 3.
5. Stop at three hours even if unfinished. An unfinished mock is information.

### How to mark a mock

| Question | Marking |
|---|---|
| Q1 | 1 mark each from the key |
| Q2 | The rubric in `01-Precis-Writing.md`, section 6. Be strict about length and lifted phrases. Then take 3 marks off your total to allow for the severity of real marking |
| Q3 | 4 for a correct, complete answer in your own words; 2 for partly correct or partly copied; 0 for wrong |
| Q4 | 2 for a fully correct sentence; 1 if one of two errors is fixed; 0 if a new error is introduced |
| Q5(a) | Start from 5. Take off half a mark for each missing or wrong capital, sentence end, quotation mark or apostrophe, down to 0 |
| Q5(b) | 1 mark each |
| Q6 | 2 a pair or idiom: 1 for each correct, meaning-showing sentence (pairs), or 2 for a sentence that shows the idiom's meaning |
| Q7 | The four-part scale in `08-Translation-Urdu-to-English.md`, section 10 |

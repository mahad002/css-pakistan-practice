## 4. Accuracy checks that were done

- Every précis passage and model précis was counted by script. Worked examples are 32–36% of the passage; practice answers 34–37%; mock answers 31–35%.
- Vocabulary, idiom, phrasal verb and pair lists were checked by script for duplicates and for complete rows.
- The 150 grammar sentences and their answers come from one data file, so numbering cannot drift between questions and answers.
- The unpunctuated practice passages were generated by script from the punctuated answers, so the words match exactly.
- Meanings, synonyms and antonyms were written from standard dictionary senses. The third-party MCQ keys were not copied, because several are wrong.

What was not done: no second person has read the model answers. A few grammar corrections follow traditional exam usage where modern usage is looser (for example "taller than I", "one... one's", "as if he knew"); these are marked in the rules. Model translations are one acceptable version among several.

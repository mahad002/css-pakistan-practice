## I. Concepts, Approaches, Context
- **Definitions:** Wilson (1887) "government in action"; Goodnow (1900) will vs execution; White (1926) first textbook; Gulick POSDCORB (1937); Waldo (1948) administration is political.
- **Henry's five paradigms:** dichotomy → principles → PA as political science → PA as management → PA as PA.
- **Dichotomy:** Wilson, Goodnow → attacked by Appleby, Waldo, Simon → Svara's complementarity (mission, policy, administration, management).
- **Bureaucracy vs democracy:** hierarchy vs equality; expertise vs participation; reconcile through control, representation, transparency, participation. Pakistan: overdeveloped state (Alavi), politicisation since 1973.
- **Efficiency vs equity:** Okun's leaky bucket; BISP, NFC examples.
- **Core values:** rule of law, efficiency, effectiveness, equity, responsiveness, accountability, transparency, integrity, neutrality.
- **TPA → NPA (Minnowbrook 1968, social equity) → NPM (Hood's 7 doctrines 1991; Osborne–Gaebler's 10 principles 1992) → NPS (Denhardts' 7 principles 2000/03) → NPG (Osborne 2006/10), governance (Rhodes 1996; World Bank 1992).**
- **Schick (1998):** developing countries should not try NZ reforms first.
- **Market failure:** public goods, externalities, natural monopoly, information asymmetry.
- **Rawls:** veil of ignorance; difference principle.
- **Islamic PA:** amanah, shura, adl, muhasabah, merit; Umar's diwan, qadis, hisbah; Ali's letter to Malik al-Ashtar; al-Mawardi.
- **Roots in Pakistan:** Arthashastra; Mughal mansabdari, sarkar–pargana; Cornwallis 1793; ICS 1855; DC as ruler.

## II. Theories
- **Weber:** 3 authorities; 8 features; *Economy and Society* (1922); iron cage. Critics: Merton (goal displacement), Selznick, Gouldner, Crozier, Bennis, Riggs (formalism).
- **Taylor (1911):** 4 principles; time and motion; functional foremanship. Critique: man as machine.
- **Fayol (1916):** 5 functions, 14 principles; gangplank. **Gulick–Urwick (1937).** **Simon (1946):** "proverbs".
- **Hawthorne (1924–32):** illumination, relay room, interviews, bank wiring. Mayo, Roethlisberger, Dickson (1939). Barnard (1938): acceptance theory.
- **Motivation:** Maslow 1943; Herzberg 1959; McGregor 1960; McClelland; Vroom 1964; Adams 1963; Locke 1968; Ouchi 1981.
- **Leadership phases:** trait (Stogdill 1948) → behavioural (Ohio, Michigan, Blake–Mouton 1964) → contingency (Fiedler 1967; Hersey–Blanchard 1969; House 1971) → transformational (Burns 1978; Bass 1985), servant (Greenleaf 1970), distributed.
- **Network governance:** Rhodes; Kickert et al. 1997; Agranoff–McGuire; NCOC, polio examples.
- **Strategic management:** Bryson (1988) 10 steps; SWOT; Moore's strategic triangle (1995); Mintzberg emergent strategy.
- **Public choice:** Buchanan–Tullock (1962), Downs (1957, 1967), Niskanen (1971), Ostrom (1973), Dunleavy (1991).
- **Structures:** line/staff/auxiliary; mechanistic/organic; Mintzberg's 5 configurations.
- **Pakistan govt:** Arts. 90–99; Rules of Business 1973; Divisions; Secretary = PAO; rightsizing 2024–25 (82 bodies; 150,000 posts).
- **IGR:** CCI (Art. 153–154, 90 days); NEC (156); NFC (160; 7th award 57.5%; 82/10.3/5/2.7); Arts. 144, 158, 161; governor's rule KP Oct 2026.

## III. Policy
- **Dye:** what governments do or not do. **Lowi:** distributive, regulatory, redistributive, constituent.
- **Models:** rational; Simon bounded; Lindblom incremental (1959); Etzioni mixed scanning (1967); Dror optimal; garbage can (1972); Kingdon streams (1984); ACF (Sabatier).
- **Implementation:** Pressman–Wildavsky (1973); top-down vs Lipsky (1980); Hogwood–Gunn 10 conditions; Matland matrix; Elmore backward mapping.
- **Evaluation:** formative/summative, process/outcome/impact, CBA/CEA; Tyler, Scriven (goal-free), Patton, Pawson–Tilley (realist); RCTs; OECD-DAC 6 criteria.
- **Pakistan machinery:** Planning Commission (1953/1958); NEC; ECNEC; CDWP; DDWP; PC-I to PC-V; Uraan Pakistan.
- **Why implementation fails here:** vague goals, instability, capacity, coordination, funds, corruption, transfers, centralisation, vested interests, no LGs, stay orders, no M&E.
- **Donors:** IMF EFF (2024), World Bank; Paris Declaration 5 principles.

## IV. Budgeting
- **Key (1940)** question; **Lewis (1952)**; **Wildavsky** incrementalism; **Schick (1966)** control → management → planning.
- **Systems:** line-item (1921) → performance (Hoover 1949) → PPBS (McNamara 1961) → MBO (1973) → ZBB (Pyhrr; Carter 1977) → outcome-based (NZ 1989, GPRA 1993) → participatory (Porto Alegre 1989).
- **Principles:** annuality, unity, universality, non-assignment, specificity, balance, publicity, accuracy, prior authorisation.
- **Musgrave:** allocation, distribution, stabilisation.
- **Constitution:** 73 (money bill, Senate 14 days), 78, 80, 81 (charged), 82 (demands; cannot increase), 84 (supplementary), 86, 160, 168–171.
- **Process:** BCC → BSP → MTBF/OBB → PSDP via APCC/NEC → June budget → passage before 30 June → execution → AGP audit → PAC.
- **Problems:** low tax-to-GDP; interest dominates; PSDP cuts; pro-cyclical; weak scrutiny; supplementary grants.
- **Laws:** PFM Act 2019; FRDL Act 2005; Audit Ordinance 2001.

## V. HRM
- **Spoils:** Jackson 1829; Marcy 1832. **Merit:** Northcote–Trevelyan 1854; Pendleton 1883; Hatch 1939; Macaulay 1854/ICS 1855.
- **Closed vs open:** rank-in-person vs rank-in-position; France/Pakistan vs USA/NZ.
- **Personnel vs HRM:** compliance vs strategy.
- **Functions:** planning, job analysis, recruitment, selection, training, appraisal, pay, promotion, discipline, relations, separation.
- **Pakistan:** Art. 240, 242; Civil Servants Act 1973; APT Rules 1973; E&D Rules 2020; Conduct Rules 2026; quota 7.5% merit, Punjab 50, Sindh 19, KP 11.5, Bal. 6, GB/ex-FATA 4, AJK 2; women 10%; contributory pension from 1 July 2024 (10% + 12%).

- **Natural justice:** nemo judex; audi alteram partem; reasons (S. 24A GCA 1897).
- **Review grounds:** GCHQ 1984 — illegality, irrationality (Wednesbury 1948), procedural impropriety; proportionality.
- **Discretion:** Davis — confine, structure, check.
- **Delegated legislation:** controls — parliamentary, judicial (ultra vires), procedural.
- **Delegation of authority:** parity, absolute responsibility, unity of command, scalar; steps: tasks → authority → accountability → controls.
- **Pakistan:** Arts. 199, 184(3), 212; FST 1973; Wafaqi Mohtasib 1983; FOIRA 2013; Federal Constitutional Court (27th Am., 2025).
- **Marbury v. Madison (1803):** Marshall; judicial review.
- **Ethics:** Friedrich–Finer; Nolan's 7; Conduct Rules 2026.

## IX. Provincial and Local Government
- **Province:** Governor (101), CM (130), cabinet (132); Chief Secretary; departments; P&D Board; BoR; Rules of Business 2011 (Punjab).
- **Agencies:** attached departments, authorities, companies; overlap with LGs.
- **LG basis:** Art. 32, 140A (18th Am.), Elections Act s.219(4) (120 days).
- **History:** Basic Democracies 1959 → Zia 1979 → LGO 2001 (Nazim, DCO, 33% women) → lapse 2009 → 2013 Acts → KP 2019 amendments → Punjab 2019, 2022, **2025** (district councils abolished; party-based polls; delimitation done Nov 2026; no date yet).
- **5D:** devolution of political power; decentralisation of administrative authority; deconcentration of management functions; diffusion of power–authority nexus; distribution of resources.
- **Challenges:** irregular polls, weak protection, provincial resistance, fiscal dependence, parallel bodies, capacity, elite capture, urban governance.

# Resources — Public Administration

Few, well used. The notes in `03-Notes/` cover the syllabus; use these to deepen the high-frequency sections and to refresh Pakistan facts.

**Tags:** **[FREE/OFFICIAL]** = free and from a government, university or public body. **[FREE]** = free, other source. **[BUY]** = book to buy or borrow.

## 1. Core books (pick two)

| Book | Tag | Use it for | How to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nicholas Henry, *Public Administration and Public Affairs* (12th ed. or later) | [BUY] | Paradigms, theories, budgeting, HRM, IGR | Read chapters on paradigms, organisation theory, budgeting and HRM. On the FPSC reading list |
| Robert Denhardt, Janet Denhardt and Tara Blanc, *Public Administration: An Action Orientation* (7th ed. or later) | [BUY] | Concepts, NPS, management skills (section VII) | The management-skills chapters map almost one-to-one to syllabus section VII. On the FPSC list |
| Jay Shafritz and Albert Hyde (eds.), *Classics of Public Administration* (7th ed. or later) | [BUY] | Original texts: Wilson, Weber, Gulick, Simon, Lindblom, Wildavsky, Hood, Denhardt | Read the classic essays named in the notes once; quote the authors accurately |
| David Rosenbloom, *Public Administration: Understanding Management, Politics and Law in the Public Sector* (8th ed. or later) | [BUY] | Administrative law, accountability, three lenses | Sections VI and XI |
| Charles H. Kennedy, *Bureaucracy in Pakistan* (OUP, 1987) | [BUY] | Civil service history, 1973 reforms, culture | Section VIII; also Pakistan Affairs |
| Ishrat Husain, *Governing the Ungovernable: Institutional Reforms for Democratic Governance* (OUP, 2018) | [BUY] | Pakistan's institutions, NCGR, reform agenda | Sections VIII, X, XII. The author chaired NCGR and the 2018–21 Task Force |
| David Osborne and Ted Gaebler, *Reinventing Government* (1992) | [BUY] | NPM | Skim the ten principles and examples |

Local guidebooks (e.g. by Pakistani publishers) are useful for MCQs, but examiners complain of "guidebook answers". Use them only for revision.

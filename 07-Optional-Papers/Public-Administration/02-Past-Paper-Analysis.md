## Format confirmed from the papers

Every year 2016–2026: 3 hours; Part I = 20 MCQs (1 mark each, 30 minutes, OMR sheet taken back, **no negative marking** stated on 2024–2026 papers); Part II = Q2–Q8, **attempt any four**, 20 marks each. Q8 is nearly always "short notes on any two of three" (10 each). Since 2024 most questions are split into parts (a) and (b). No calculator (Public Administration is not on FPSC's calculator list).

## What this means

1. **Prepare I, II, IV, X and XI in depth** — about two-thirds of all questions. With these five you can almost always find four questions.
2. **Examiners repeat.** Six topics have come back word for word or nearly so. Prepare full answers for every repeat in the table.
3. **Pakistan application is expected.** About half the questions end with "in Pakistan" or "with reference to Pakistan". A theory-only answer loses marks.
4. **Recent papers split questions into parts.** Give each part a heading and roughly equal space.
5. **Short notes (Q8)** are often the safest 20 marks: definitions plus one Pakistan example plus one criticism, about 450 words each.

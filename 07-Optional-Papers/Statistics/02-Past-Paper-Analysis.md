## Observations

1. **Exact repeats happen.** The 2026 RCBD question used exactly the 2025 data. The "sampling distribution of x̄" question is set nearly every year with a new small population.
2. **Section B is the more predictable half:** one sampling question, one inference question, one ANOVA question, and (usually) one vital-statistics/index-number question. Choose the two you know best.
3. **Recent papers add theory and critique** (2026: exchangeability, the role of probability, p-value critique). Prepare short, well-structured essays (½–1 page) for these.
4. **Non-parametric methods are rare** but appear in Section A every few years as an easy alternative.
5. **Printed errors are common** (missing sample variance in 2018, a negative "density" in 2016, a letter "H" in the 2024 RCBD table). State your interpretation in one line and carry on — examiners reward sensible handling.

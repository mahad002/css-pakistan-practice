# Syllabus Checklist — Criminology (CSS, 100 marks)

Source: FPSC "Syllabus for CE-2016 and onwards", paper "Criminology (100 marks)". The syllabus has **four sections of 25 marks each** containing thirteen numbered items (I–XIII). The FPSC PDF would not open in my page reader (certificate error), so the wording below comes from a faithful copy of the FPSC text published by the National Officers Academy ([lms.noacss.pk](https://lms.noacss.pk/uploads/syllabus/Criminology/2022/2a63d5e2.pdf)). Check once against the official PDF: https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/uploads/syllabus/1767002683737_Syllabus-for-CE-2016-and-onwards.pdf

**Why sections matter:** the paper's Part II has two questions from each syllabus section and you must answer **one from each section**. You cannot skip a section.

**Notes files:** one file per numbered syllabus item; items I and II share a file, IX and X share a file, and the long item XIII is split across three files.

**Frequency** is from ten papers 2016–2026 (2019 could not be retrieved; 2021 partly reconstructed). High = 3+ times; Medium = 1–2 times; Low = not asked as a main question.

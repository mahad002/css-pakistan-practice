# Resources — Criminology

The notes in `03-Notes/` cover the syllabus. Use these to deepen high-frequency topics and refresh Pakistani facts.

**Tags:** **[FREE/OFFICIAL]** = free, from a government, university, UN or public body. **[FREE]** = free, other source. **[BUY]** = book to buy or borrow.

## 1. Core books (pick one main text)

| Book | Tag | Use it for | How to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Larry J. Siegel, *Criminology: Theories, Patterns and Typologies* (any recent edition) | [BUY] | Theories, typologies, criminal justice | The best single text for the syllabus. Read the theory chapters (classical, trait, social structure, social process, conflict, life-course) and the chapters on white-collar, organised and cyber crime |
| Larry J. Siegel and Brandon Welsh, *Juvenile Delinquency: Theory, Practice and Law* | [BUY] | Section II (juvenile delinquency and justice) | On the FPSC list. Read the chapters on definitions, statistics, juvenile court process, corrections |
| Vold, Bernard and Snipes, *Theoretical Criminology* | [BUY] | Deeper theory | Use for 20-mark theory answers (strain, labelling, conflict) |
| Charles Swanson et al., *Criminal Investigation* (or Osterburg and Ward) | [BUY] | Section III | Principles, crime scene, interviewing, forensics |
| Local CSS guides (e.g. by Pakistani publishers) | [BUY] | MCQs, Pakistan-specific items | Use for revision only; examiners dislike memorised guidebook answers |

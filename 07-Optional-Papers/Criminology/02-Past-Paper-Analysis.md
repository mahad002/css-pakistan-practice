## Format confirmed from the papers

Every year: 3 hours; Part I = 20 MCQs (1 mark each, 30 minutes, OMR, **no negative marking** stated 2024–2026). Part II = **eight questions (Q2–Q9) in four sections, two per section; attempt four, one from each section**; 20 marks each. Some questions are split into short notes (2 × 10 or 4 × 5 marks).

## Strategy that follows

1. **One question per section is compulsory.** Prepare at least **two strong topics per section** so you always have a choice.
2. **Section I:** scope of criminology in Pakistan + one theory applied to youth (strain, labelling, conflict, social learning). Also crime as a social problem.
3. **Section II:** juvenile delinquency causes and prevention + police challenges. Back-up: rehabilitation/recidivism, juvenile justice system.
4. **Section III:** principles of investigation + one international organisation (INTERPOL, UNODC, IPA). Back-up: forensics/crime scene, gender-based crime investigation.
5. **Section IV:** terrorism/radicalisation + money laundering or community policing. Back-up: FIA/ANF/NAB, cyber crime and AI.

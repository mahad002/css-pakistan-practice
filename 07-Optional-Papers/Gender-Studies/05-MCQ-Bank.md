# MCQ Bank — Gender Studies

185 MCQs written for this pack, in syllabus order. The real paper has 20 MCQs (20 marks, 30 minutes, no negative marking). Recent papers often use "D) None of these"; here all four options are real choices so you learn the facts. Pakistan figures are as of Nov 2026.

## I. Introduction to Gender Studies

**Q1.** The first formal Women's Studies programme was set up in 1970 at:
- A) San Diego State University
- B) Columbia University
- C) Harvard University
- D) University of Oxford
**Answer:** A. San Diego State University launched the first programme in 1970.

**Q2.** Who popularised the distinction between "sex" and "gender" in *Sex, Gender and Society* (1972)?
- A) Judith Butler
- B) Sylvia Walby
- C) Ann Oakley
- D) Kate Millett
**Answer:** C. Ann Oakley's 1972 book made the sex/gender distinction standard.

**Q3.** "Gender: A Useful Category of Historical Analysis" (1986) was written by:
- A) Nancy Chodorow
- B) Joan Scott
- C) Sherry Ortner
- D) Gayle Rubin
**Answer:** B. Joan Wallach Scott argued gender is a primary way of signifying power.

**Q4.** The autonomy versus integration debate is linked to *Theories of Women's Studies*, edited by:
- A) Moser and Kabeer
- B) Mumtaz and Shaheed
- C) Tong and Jaggar
- D) Bowles and Klein
**Answer:** D. Gloria Bowles and Renate Duelli Klein (1983).

**Q5.** In the autonomy/integration debate, "integration" means:
- A) Bringing gender perspectives into all existing disciplines
- B) Merging women's ministries with other ministries
- C) Women-only universities
- D) Separate Women's Studies departments
**Answer:** A. Integration means mainstreaming gender across the curriculum.

**Q6.** Gender Studies differs from Women's Studies mainly because it:
- A) Is limited to biology
- B) Rejects feminist theory
- C) Studies only women
- D) Studies gender relations, including men and masculinities
**Answer:** D. Gender Studies examines gender as a relational system.

**Q7.** Gayle Rubin's concept of the "sex/gender system" (1975) appeared in:
- A) *Gender Trouble*
- B) "The Traffic in Women"
- C) *The Second Sex*
- D) *Sexual Politics*
**Answer:** B. Rubin's essay "The Traffic in Women" introduced the term.

**Q8.** How many structures of patriarchy did Sylvia Walby identify in *Theorizing Patriarchy* (1990)?
- A) Five
- B) Four
- C) Eight
- D) Six
**Answer:** D. Household production, paid work, the state, male violence, sexuality, culture.

**Q9.** Women's Studies centres were set up in five Pakistani universities by the Ministry of Women Development in:
- A) 1979
- B) 1989
- C) 1999
- D) 2009
**Answer:** B. The centres date from 1989.

**Q10.** Gender mainstreaming was adopted as a global strategy at:
- A) Mexico City, 1975
- B) Nairobi, 1985
- C) Beijing, 1995
- D) New York, 2000
**Answer:** C. The Beijing Platform for Action (1995) adopted it.

**Q11.** "Androcentrism" means:
- A) Equal treatment of the sexes
- B) The study of androgyny
- C) Treating men's experience as the universal norm
- D) Treating women's experience as the norm
**Answer:** C. Male-centred knowledge presented as universal.

**Q12.** The best description of Gender Studies as a field is:
- A) Single-discipline
- B) Purely quantitative
- C) Interdisciplinary
- D) Purely biological
**Answer:** C. It draws on many disciplines and combines their methods.

**Q13.** Gender equity, as distinct from gender equality, refers to:
- A) Preference for men
- B) Identical treatment in every case
- C) Biological difference
- D) Fair treatment, which may need special measures to reach equality
**Answer:** D. Equity is fairness according to need, e.g. quotas.

**Q14.** "The State of the Discipline of Women's Studies in Pakistan" (on the FPSC reading list) is by:
- A) Asma Jahangir
- B) Rubina Saigol
- C) Fatima Mernissi
- D) Benazir Bhutto
**Answer:** B. Rubina Saigol wrote it.

**Q15.** Which UN body issued the 1997 definition of gender mainstreaming?
- A) ILO
- B) ECOSOC
- C) UNESCO
- D) Security Council
**Answer:** B. ECOSOC's agreed conclusions of 1997.

## II. Social Construction of Gender

**Q16.** "One is not born, but rather becomes, a woman" is from:
- A) *Sexual Politics*
- B) *The Feminine Mystique*
- C) *The Second Sex*
- D) *A Room of One's Own*
**Answer:** C. Simone de Beauvoir, 1949.

**Q17.** Margaret Mead's *Sex and Temperament* (1935) compared which three societies?
- A) Arapesh, Mundugumor, Tchambuli
- B) Trobriand, Samoa, Manus
- C) Hopi, Zuni, Navajo
- D) Nuer, Dinka, Azande
**Answer:** A. All three were in New Guinea.

**Q18.** "Doing Gender" (1987) was proposed by:
- A) Parsons and Bales
- B) Davis and Moore
- C) Berger and Luckmann
- D) West and Zimmerman
**Answer:** D. Candace West and Don Zimmerman.

**Q19.** Judith Butler's "performativity" holds that gender is:
- A) An inherited psychological archetype
- B) A biological destiny
- C) A costume freely chosen each day
- D) A stylised repetition of acts under social norms
**Answer:** D. Repetition under norms creates the appearance of an inner gender.

**Q20.** The concept of "hegemonic masculinity" was developed by:
- A) Andrea Dworkin
- B) R.W. Connell
- C) bell hooks
- D) Michael Kimmel
**Answer:** B. Raewyn Connell, *Gender and Power* (1987) and *Masculinities* (1995).

**Q21.** "Is Female to Male as Nature Is to Culture?" was written by:
- A) Sherry Ortner
- B) Judith Lorber
- C) Gayle Rubin
- D) Margaret Mead
**Answer:** A. Sherry Ortner (1972/1974).

**Q22.** The term "queer theory" was coined by:
- A) Judith Butler
- B) Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick
- C) Teresa de Lauretis
- D) Michel Foucault
**Answer:** C. De Lauretis used it in 1990.

**Q23.** Foucault's challenge to the "repressive hypothesis" argues that:
- A) The Victorians silenced all talk of sexuality
- B) Religion alone controls sexuality
- C) Sexuality is a purely biological drive
- D) Power produces discourse about sexuality rather than only repressing it
**Answer:** D. From *The History of Sexuality*, vol. 1 (1976).

**Q24.** "The Five Sexes" (1993), on intersex variation, was written by:
- A) Anne Fausto-Sterling
- B) Ann Oakley
- C) Sandra Bem
- D) Thomas Laqueur
**Answer:** A. A biologist's challenge to the strict binary.

**Q25.** Thomas Laqueur's *Making Sex* (1990) describes a historical shift from:
- A) Religion to science only
- B) A one-sex to a two-sex model
- C) Gender to sex
- D) A two-sex to a one-sex model
**Answer:** B. The "two-sex model" arose in the 18th century.

**Q26.** John Money coined the term "gender role" in:
- A) 1935
- B) 1955
- C) 1975
- D) 1990
**Answer:** B. 1955.

**Q27.** The Bem Sex-Role Inventory measures:
- A) Masculine, feminine and androgynous traits
- B) Fertility
- C) Violence
- D) Intelligence
**Answer:** A. Sandra Bem, 1974.

**Q28.** Connell's "emphasised femininity" refers to:
- A) Female masculinity
- B) Femininity that dominates men
- C) Feminist activism
- D) Femininity organised around accommodating men's interests
**Answer:** D. It complements hegemonic masculinity.

**Q29.** Nancy Chodorow's *The Reproduction of Mothering* explains gender through:
- A) Language only
- B) Early childhood relations with mothers
- C) Market forces
- D) Hormones
**Answer:** B. A psychoanalytic object-relations account.

**Q30.** *Female Masculinity* (1998) was written by:
- A) Raewyn Connell
- B) Judith Lorber
- C) Jack Halberstam
- D) Kate Millett
**Answer:** C. It separates masculinity from male bodies.

**Q31.** Which theory says children learn gender by imitating models and being rewarded or punished?
- A) Sociobiology
- B) Psychoanalytic
- C) Social learning
- D) Cognitive development
**Answer:** C. Bandura and Mischel.

**Q32.** Lawrence Kohlberg's account of gender is:
- A) Psychoanalytic
- B) Cognitive-developmental, with gender constancy
- C) Functionalist
- D) Social learning
**Answer:** B. Children form a gender concept first, then seek matching behaviour.

**Q33.** "Night to His Day: The Social Construction of Gender" was written by:
- A) Harriet Bradley
- B) Judith Lorber
- C) Rosemarie Tong
- D) Naila Kabeer
**Answer:** B. From *Paradoxes of Gender* (1994).

## III. Feminist Theories and Practice

**Q34.** *A Vindication of the Rights of Woman* (1792) was written by:
- A) Mary Wollstonecraft
- B) Harriet Taylor

- D) Elizabeth Cady Stanton

**Q35.** *The Subjection of Women* (1869) was written by:
- A) Karl Marx
- B) Friedrich Engels
- C) John Stuart Mill
- D) Jeremy Bentham

**Q36.** *The Feminine Mystique* (1963) was written by:
- A) Germaine Greer
- B) Kate Millett
- C) Betty Friedan
- D) Gloria Steinem
**Answer:** C. "The problem that has no name."

**Q37.** *Sexual Politics* (1970) was written by:
- A) Mary Daly
- B) Kate Millett
- C) Adrienne Rich
- D) Shulamith Firestone
**Answer:** B. A key radical feminist text on patriarchy.

**Q38.** Who argued in *The Dialectic of Sex* that liberation needs technology to free women from biological reproduction?
- A) Betty Friedan
- B) Shulamith Firestone

**Q39.** The essay "The Personal Is Political" is credited to:
- A) Simone de Beauvoir
- B) bell hooks
- C) Audre Lorde

**Q40.** Engels linked women's oppression to private property in:
- A) *The Origin of the Family, Private Property and the State*
- B) *Das Kapital*
- C) *The Communist Manifesto*
- D) *Woman and Socialism*

**Q41.** "The Unhappy Marriage of Marxism and Feminism" (1979), which set out dual-systems theory, was written by:
- A) Heidi Hartmann
- B) Iris Marion Young

**Q42.** Which school locates women's oppression in both capitalism and patriarchy?

**Q43.** The Wages for Housework campaign is associated with:

**Q44.** Karen Horney answered Freud's "penis envy" with the idea of:
- A) The Oedipus complex

**Q45.** *In a Different Voice* (1982), which proposed an "ethic of care", was written by:
- A) Juliet Mitchell
- B) Carol Gilligan
- C) Nancy Chodorow
- D) Dorothy Dinnerstein

**Q46.** "The Laugh of the Medusa" (1975), which introduced *écriture féminine*, was written by:
- A) Luce Irigaray
- B) Hélène Cixous
- C) Simone de Beauvoir

**Q47.** *This Sex Which Is Not One* (1977) was written by:
- A) Hélène Cixous
- B) Luce Irigaray
- C) Judith Butler
- D) Julia Kristeva

**Q48.** "A Cyborg Manifesto" (1985) was written by:
- A) Linda Nicholson
- B) Sandra Harding
- C) Donna Haraway

**Q49.** "Under Western Eyes", a critique of how Western feminists portray "Third World women", was written by:

**Q50.** "Strategic essentialism" is a concept of:
- A) Donna Haraway

**Q51.** UN Women launched the HeForShe campaign in:
- A) 2010

**Q52.** Who defined feminism as "a movement to end sexism, sexist exploitation, and oppression"?
- A) bell hooks

**Q53.** Which school is most associated with gradual legal reform within existing institutions?

**Q54.** Postmodern feminism rejects:
- A) Differences among women

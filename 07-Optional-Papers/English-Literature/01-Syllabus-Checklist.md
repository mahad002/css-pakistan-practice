# Syllabus Checklist — English Literature (CSS, Group V, 100 marks)

Source: FPSC "Revised Scheme and Syllabus for CSS Competitive Examination (CE-2016 and onwards)", English Literature pages. The FPSC site would not open from my tools (certificate error), so the list below was checked word for word against a faithful copy of the same FPSC pages hosted by NOA CSS ([lms.noacss.pk PDF](https://lms.noacss.pk/uploads/syllabus/English%20Literature/2022/a570bbe8.pdf)) and against [mkg.com.pk](https://mkg.com.pk/blog/css-english-literature-syllabus) and [cssprep.com.pk](https://cssprep.com.pk/css-syllabus-english-literature/). All three agree. The official text writes "Ode to Autumn" (Keats's title is "To Autumn") and "Literacy Theory" (a typo for "Literary Theory"). The marks in brackets are the syllabus's own weightings; in the actual paper every Part-II question carries 20 marks (see `02-Past-Paper-Analysis.md`).

Frequency key, from the 77 Part-II questions of 2016–2026: **High** = asked in 4+ years; **Medium** = 1–3 years; **Low** = never asked as a full question (MCQs only).

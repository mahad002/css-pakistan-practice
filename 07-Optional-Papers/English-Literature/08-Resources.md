## 2. Set texts: public-domain status and free copies

Copyright rule of thumb: Pakistan protects works for the author's life plus 50 years (Copyright Ordinance 1962); the US protects works published before 1931 no longer (as of 2026). Project Gutenberg (US) only hosts works that are public domain in the US. "Checked" means I confirmed the ebook number by search on 24 Nov 2026; "not re-checked" means the number is from my own knowledge and Gutenberg's site was rate-limiting me, so confirm it when you open it.

## 3. The few best books

| Book | Free? | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| Peter Barry, *Beginning Theory* (4th ed., 2017) | Buy | **Start here for theory.** Clear chapters on every school on the syllabus with "what X critics do" checklists. Read the six relevant chapters in Feb–Apr 2027 |
| Terry Eagleton, *Literary Theory: An Introduction* (anniversary ed., 2008) | Buy | On FPSC's suggested list. Read the chapters on structuralism, poststructuralism, psychoanalysis and the conclusion after Barry; quote Eagleton in answers |
| Hans Bertens, *Literary Theory: The Basics* (2nd ed., 2008) | Buy | On FPSC's list. Short and reliable on feminism and postcolonialism |
| Selden, Widdowson and Brooker, *A Reader's Guide to Contemporary Literary Theory* (5th ed., 2005) | Buy | On FPSC's list. Use as reference for names and dates |
| M.H. Abrams and G.G. Harpham, *A Glossary of Literary Terms* | Buy | The best single reference for MCQ terms |
| A.C. Bradley, *Shakespearean Tragedy* (1904) | **FREE** [Gutenberg 16966](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16966) (not re-checked) | Read the lectures on *Hamlet* and *King Lear*; Bradley is quoted in CSS questions |
| Martin Esslin, *The Theatre of the Absurd* | Buy/library | Chapters on Beckett and Pinter |
| Harold Bloom (ed.), *Modern Critical Views / Interpretations* series | Library | On FPSC's list: one volume per author with critical essays. Use for critics' names on Lawrence, Eliot, Joyce, O'Neill |
| Woolf, *A Room of One's Own* (1929) | **FREE** in Pakistan and Australia: [PGA](https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks02/0200791h.html) [verify link] | Read in full (short); the foundation of every feminist answer |

## 5. What not to rely on

- **Guidebooks of "solved papers"** that print plot summaries. The CE-2018 examiner's report criticised exactly this: candidates "merely gave a summary of the work". Use them for past-paper lists only.
- **AI-generated "analysis" pages** that invent quotations. Check every quotation against the text.

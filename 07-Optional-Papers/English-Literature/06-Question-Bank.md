# Question Bank — English Literature (CSS)

Every Part-II question from 2016 to 2026 (paraphrased; sources in `02-Past-Paper-Analysis.md`), plus predicted questions marked **[Likely]**. A two-text question is listed under the first text. Practise each under timed conditions: 5 minutes to plan, 30 minutes to write.

## Essays: Russell, *The Conquest of Happiness*

- **[CSS 2016]** Russell's book "gets straight to the point": give your view of it with examples (part b, 10 marks) (paraphrased)
- **[CSS 2018]** How does Russell connect abstract philosophical concerns with ordinary daily effort towards contentment? (paraphrased)
- **[CSS 2019]** Is happiness still possible once we work on the causes of unhappiness Russell identifies? (paraphrased)
- **[CSS 2022]** Russell's *Conquest of Happiness* and Emerson's "Self-Reliance" both hold that individuals must rely on their own moral judgement: compare (paraphrased)
- **[CSS 2023]** Is happiness possible in the modern world? Answer with reference to *The Conquest of Happiness* (paraphrased)
- **[Likely]** "The happy man is the man who lives objectively." Explain Russell's idea of outward-looking happiness.
- **[Likely]** Discuss Russell's analysis of competition, envy and the fear of public opinion as causes of unhappiness, with modern examples.
- **[Likely]** Evaluate *The Conquest of Happiness* as a work of practical philosophy: what are its strengths and limitations?

# Past-Paper Analysis — Applied Mathematics (CSS 2016–2026)

Checked 24 Nov 2026. Since 2016 Applied Mathematics is a **single 100-mark paper**: 8 questions, attempt any 5, 20 marks each (usually two 10-mark parts), 3 hours, calculator allowed, **no MCQs**. Before 2016 it was two papers (I: vectors, statics, dynamics; II: ODEs, numerical methods, tensors), so pre-2016 papers are still useful practice for single topics but not for the paper's shape.

## Observations

1. **The paper is very stable.** The same eight-question frame recurs; most years have one VC, one or two mechanics, two or three ODE, one Fourier/PDE and two numerical questions.
2. **Exact repeats happen.** The square-of-forces problem (2018 → 2023), the ax² + by² = 1 kinematics problem (2024 → 2026), ∫₀¹dx/(1 + x²) by trapezoidal/Simpson (2016 → 2018), Gauss–Seidel on a 3×3 dominant system (almost every year).
3. **Mixed questions are now common** (2023–2026): one question may pair, say, dynamics with a Bernoulli equation. You must be able to do both halves of the five questions you choose.
4. **Statics is optional in practice.** It vanished in 2020 and 2026. Prepare the three standard types, but plan your five questions without it.
5. **Recent drift:** 2024–2026 added more PDE theory (canonical forms, Lagrange's method, transformation of first-order PDEs, modelling the wave equation) and dropped interpolation. Expect at least one "derive or model" part each year.

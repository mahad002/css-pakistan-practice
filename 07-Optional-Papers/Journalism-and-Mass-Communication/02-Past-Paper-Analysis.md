## Paper format (confirmed 2016–2026)

- 100 marks, 3 hours.
- **Part I:** Q1, 20 MCQs × 1 mark, OMR sheet, collected after **30 minutes**; "no negative marking" (2024–2026 papers).
- **Part II:** Q2–Q8, seven questions × 20 marks; **attempt any four**. Q8 is usually short notes ("any two of three", 10 each) or, in 2016 and 2018, four 5-mark notes.
- No calculator.

## Patterns

1. **The paper rewards Pakistan.** Most questions ask for Pakistani examples: PEMRA, PECA, PID, talk shows, dramas, journalists' safety.
2. **A stable core.** DSC, PR, laws/PEMRA and one theory appear almost every year. Prepare these four perfectly and you always have four questions.
3. **Digital turn since 2020.** PECA (2020), magic bullet in the digital age, fake news, digital divide (2024), new social media (2025), digital ethics (2024, 2026).
4. **Repeats are common** (diffusion of innovations three years running; Gerbner 2017 and 2025; MacBride three times).
5. **MCQs** test theorists (Noelle-Neumann, Lasswell, Gerbner, McCombs and Shaw, Herman and Chomsky), basics (scoop, feature, editorial, teleprompter, press note), agencies (MENA), Pakistani facts (PEMRA 2002, PECA 2016), and development communication approaches.

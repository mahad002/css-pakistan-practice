## I. Introduction to Mass Communication and Models

**Q1.** The word "communication" comes from the Latin *communis*, meaning:
- A) Message
- B) Signal
- C) Common
- D) Speech
**Answer:** C. It means common or shared.

**Q2.** "Who says what in which channel to whom with what effect?" is the model of:
- A) Harold Lasswell
- B) George Gerbner
- C) Wilbur Schramm
- D) Claude Shannon
**Answer:** A. Lasswell's verbal model.

**Q3.** Lasswell's model of communication was published in:
- A) 1938
- B) 1948
- C) 1956
- D) 1964
**Answer:** B. "The Structure and Function of Communication in Society" (1948).

**Q4.** The Shannon-Weaver model was originally developed to improve:
- A) Classroom teaching
- B) Telephone signal transmission
- C) Political propaganda
- D) Newspaper editing
**Answer:** B. Shannon worked at Bell Laboratories.

**Q5.** The Shannon-Weaver model is also known as a:
- A) Linear model
- B) Helical model
- C) Feedback model
- D) Transactional model
**Answer:** A. It is a one-way transmission model.

**Q6.** Which concept did the Shannon-Weaver model introduce to communication study?
- A) Opinion leaders
- B) Hegemony
- C) Agenda-setting
- D) Noise
**Answer:** D. Noise interferes with the signal in the channel.

**Q7.** Repetition of a message to overcome noise is called:
- A) Encoding
- B) Feedback
- C) Redundancy
- D) Entropy
**Answer:** C. Redundancy counters noise.

**Q8.** The circular model of communication is associated with:
- A) Herman and Chomsky
- B) Osgood and Schramm
- C) Katz and Lazarsfeld
- D) McCombs and Shaw
**Answer:** B. Each communicator encodes, interprets and decodes.

**Q9.** According to Schramm, communication succeeds where the sender and receiver share overlapping:
- A) Channels
- B) Fields of experience
- C) Noise sources
- D) Media owners
**Answer:** B. Shared experience creates shared meaning.

**Q10.** Newcomb's ABX model explains communication as a means of maintaining:
- A) Symmetry between communicators' orientations
- B) Media profits
- C) Government control
- D) Technical accuracy
**Answer:** A. Strain toward symmetry (1953).

**Q11.** In the Westley-MacLean model, the "C" role refers to the:
- A) Advocate
- B) Event
- C) Audience
- D) Channel or gatekeeper
**Answer:** D. C selects and passes messages to B.

**Q12.** Gerbner's general model has two dimensions:
- A) Perceptual and means/control
- B) Vertical and diagonal
- C) Cognitive and affective
- D) Encoding and noise
**Answer:** A. Horizontal (perception) and vertical (means and control).

**Q13.** Gerbner's general model of communication was published in:
- A) 1948
- B) 1953
- C) 1956
- D) 1972
**Answer:** C. 1956.

**Q14.** Communicating within oneself is called:
- A) Group communication
- B) Intrapersonal communication
- C) Mass communication
- D) Interpersonal communication
**Answer:** B. Self-talk and thinking.

**Q15.** The SMCR model was proposed by:
- A) Dean Barnlund
- B) David Berlo
- C) Roman Jakobson
- D) Frank Dance
**Answer:** B. Source, Message, Channel, Receiver (1960).

**Q16.** Who added "entertainment" to Lasswell's three functions of communication?
- A) Walter Lippmann
- B) Harold Innis
- C) Charles Wright
- D) Kurt Lewin
**Answer:** C. Wright, 1959/1960.

**Q17.** Which is NOT one of Deutsch's functions of a communication model?
- A) Predicting
- B) Censoring
- C) Organising
- D) Measuring
**Answer:** B. The four are organising, heuristic, predictive, measuring.

**Q18.** The mass communication audience is typically described as:
- A) Large, heterogeneous and anonymous
- B) Small and homogeneous
- C) Limited to one locality
- D) Known personally to the sender
**Answer:** A. Wright's characterisation.

**Q19.** The transactional model of communication is associated with:
- A) Dean Barnlund
- B) Everett Rogers
- C) Claude Shannon
- D) Harold Lasswell
**Answer:** A. Barnlund, 1970.

**Q20.** In traditional mass communication, feedback is usually:
- A) Face to face
- B) Delayed and indirect
- C) Absent by law
- D) Immediate and direct
**Answer:** B. Ratings, letters, surveys.

**Q21.** The helical model of communication was proposed by:
- A) Theodore Newcomb
- B) Wilbur Schramm
- C) George Gerbner
- D) Frank Dance
**Answer:** D. Dance, 1967.

**Q33.** Uses and gratifications research is most associated with:

**Q34.** Uses and gratifications theory assumes the audience is:

**Q41.** *Diffusion of Innovations* (1962) was written by:

**Q45.** Laggards make up about:

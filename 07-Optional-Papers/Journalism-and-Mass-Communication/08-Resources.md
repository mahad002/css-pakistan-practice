# Resources — Journalism & Mass Communication

Keep it small. The notes in `03-Notes/` cover the syllabus; use these to deepen and to update Pakistani facts. **[FREE]** = free to read online; **[OFFICIAL]** = government, regulator, UN or other primary source.

## 1. Core books (one or two is enough)

| Book | Why | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| Denis McQuail (and Mark Deuze), *McQuail's Media and Mass Communication Theory* (latest edition; FPSC lists the 4th ed., 2000) | The standard theory textbook: normative theories, effects, media and society, centripetal/centrifugal effects | Read chapters on theories of media and society, normative theory, media effects and audience theory. Use for sections II and IV |
| Werner Severin and James Tankard, *Communication Theories: Origins, Methods and Uses in the Mass Media* (5th ed., 1997; FPSC list) | Clear accounts of models and effects theories | Sections I and II |
| Denis McQuail and Sven Windahl, *Communication Models for the Study of Mass Communications* (2nd ed., 1993; FPSC list) | The best source on Lasswell, Shannon-Weaver, Newcomb, Westley-MacLean, Gerbner | Read the model chapters; draw each model yourself |
| Everett Rogers, *Diffusion of Innovations* (5th ed., 2003; FPSC list) | Asked four times since 2021 | Chapters 1, 5 (innovation-decision process), 6 (attributes), 7 (adopter categories) |
| Altaf Ullah Khan, *News Media and Journalism in Pakistan* (2011; FPSC list) | Pakistani media system | Section V; supplement with recent news |
| A good PR text (e.g. Cutlip, Center and Broom, *Effective Public Relations*; or Wilcox and Cameron, *Public Relations: Strategies and Tactics*) | Definitions, history, models, ethics | Section VII chapters on history, process, government PR, ethics |
| Arif Hasan, *Participatory Development* (OUP, 2010; FPSC list) | Orangi Pilot Project and community development | Section VI |

## 3. How to use the resources

1. **Feb–May 2027**: notes + McQuail (theory) and McQuail and Windahl (models).
2. **Jun–Aug 2027**: Pakistani laws from Pakistan Code and PEMRA; Altaf Khan for media history.
3. **Sep–Oct 2027**: DSC and PR (Rogers, Arif Hasan, a PR text); collect three current examples per topic from Dawn.
4. **Nov 2027–Jan 2028**: past papers and model answers; update RSF 2027 rank, PEMRA/PECA status, Economic Survey 2026–27 figures.
5. Never quote a section number or figure you have not seen in the source; write "about" or describe the provision instead.

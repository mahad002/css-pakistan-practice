## How they differ

**Recommended:** the best evidence. Every subject was passable in both years with FPSC data (2023 and 2025), and three of them overlap with the compulsory papers (Political Science and Public Administration with Pakistan Affairs and Current Affairs; Journalism with Current Affairs).

**Maths strength:** less reading, highest ceiling. Applied Maths and Statistics replace Public Administration and Journalism. These papers are calculator-allowed and objective: a well-prepared mathematician can score 70–90 in each. But most candidates fail them, and one bad day costs a lot. **Gate:** choose this only if you score 70%+ on the timed 2024–2026 Applied Maths and Statistics papers before 30 Apr 2027.

**English strength:** uses your reading and writing. English Literature replaces Gender Studies. **Warning:** the data is weak for English Literature. In CSS 2023, 64 candidates took it and only 20% scored 60%+; in CSS 2025 its mean was 32% and none of the 68 candidates who took it passed the written exam. The set-text syllabus is long. Choose it only if you already read literature for pleasure and score well on a timed past paper.

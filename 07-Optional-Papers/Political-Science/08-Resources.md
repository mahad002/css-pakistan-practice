# Resources — Political Science

Few and good. The notes in `03-Notes/` cover the whole syllabus; use books to deepen the most-asked topics and to collect quotations and critics. **FREE** marks free or official sources (with links). Everything else must be bought or borrowed.

---

## 1. The core shelf (buy or borrow 3–4)

| Book | Use | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **George H. Sabine, *A History of Political Theory*** (FPSC list) | The standard history of Western thought from Plato to the 20th century. Read the chapters on Plato, Aristotle, Machiavelli, Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau, Montesquieu, Hegel, Marx | 30–40 hours over Phase 1 |
| **William Ebenstein, *Great Political Thinkers: Plato to the Present*** (FPSC list) | Extracts from the thinkers themselves with short introductions: the best source of accurate short quotations | Dip in per thinker |
| **Andrew Heywood, *Politics* and *Political Ideologies*** (not on the FPSC list) | The clearest modern textbooks for Part B (state, sovereignty, democracy, parties, ideologies) and comparative politics | One chapter per Part B topic |
| **Hamid Khan, *Constitutional and Political History of Pakistan*** (FPSC list) | The best single book for Paper II's Pakistan section (1947–56, the three constitutions, amendments, judiciary) | Phase 2 |
| **H.K. Sherwani, *Studies in Muslim Political Thought and Administration*** (FPSC list) or **Erwin Rosenthal, *Political Thought in Medieval Islam*** | Muslim thinkers in more depth than the notes; Sherwani is shorter and exam-friendly | Phase 1, weeks 10–11 |

Optional for depth: Ernest Barker, *Greek Political Theory: Plato and His Predecessors* (Plato); Hamid Enayat, *Modern Islamic Political Thought* (Afghani, Rida, modern Islamism); Hasan Askari Rizvi, *Military, State and Society in Pakistan*; Abdul Sattar, *Pakistan's Foreign Policy 1947–2019* (latest edition); Ayesha Jalal, *The Sole Spokesman* (interpretation of Jinnah); Almond and Powell, *Comparative Politics Today* (systems approach); Hans Morgenthau, *Politics Among Nations* (national interest, balance of power); Will Kymlicka, *Contemporary Political Philosophy* (Rawls, libertarians, communitarians).

Local CSS guidebooks (several publishers in Lahore) are convenient summaries but often outdated and sometimes inaccurate on current facts. Use them only to check coverage, never as a primary source.

### 2.2 Primary texts of the thinkers (FREE)
**Project Gutenberg** (https://www.gutenberg.org) has free public-domain translations. Search the titles below:
- Plato, *The Republic* (Jowett translation); Aristotle, *Politics* (Jowett)
- Machiavelli, *The Prince*; Hobbes, *Leviathan*; Locke, *Second Treatise of Government*
- Rousseau, *The Social Contract*; Montesquieu, *The Spirit of Laws*
- Kant, *Perpetual Peace*; J.S. Mill, *On Liberty*, *Utilitarianism*, *Considerations on Representative Government*, *The Subjection of Women*
- Marx and Engels, *The Communist Manifesto*

How to use: read 10–20 pages of each key work (e.g. *Leviathan* ch. 13–14 and 21; *Second Treatise* ch. 2, 5, 9, 19; *Social Contract* Book I) to collect two or three exact short quotations per thinker.

**Ibn Khaldun, *The Muqaddimah*** (Franz Rosenthal translation): available free on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org; search "Muqaddimah Rosenthal") — read chapter 2 (asabiyya) and chapter 3 (dynasties).

**Iqbal, *The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam*** (FREE): Iqbal Academy Pakistan, https://www.allamaiqbal.com (works section) and the Iqbal Cyber Library, https://www.iqbalcyberlibrary.net. Read lecture 6 ("The Principle of Movement in the Structure of Islam").

### 2.3 Reference encyclopaedias (FREE)
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy**, https://plato.stanford.edu: authoritative entries on every Western thinker in the syllabus and on al-Farabi, Ibn Rushd, al-Ghazali, Ibn Khaldun. Use the entry's "political philosophy" section; 20 minutes per thinker.
- **Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy**, https://iep.utm.edu: shorter, simpler entries.

### 2.4 Textbooks and courses (FREE)
- **OpenStax, *Introduction to Political Science*** (2022), https://openstax.org/details/books/introduction-political-science: free, peer-reviewed textbook for Part B topics (institutions, participation, comparative politics, IR). FREE, open licence.
- **AIOU (Allama Iqbal Open University) course books** for BA/MA Political Science and Pakistan Studies, https://www.aiou.edu.pk (e-books are published through the university's online portal; availability varies by course). FREE, official Pakistani university material.
- **Open Yale Courses: "Introduction to Political Philosophy" (Steven Smith, PLSC 114)**, https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-114: 24 free lectures on Plato, Aristotle, Machiavelli, Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau, Tocqueville. Listen while commuting.

### 2.5 Constitutions (FREE, official or authoritative)
| Country | Link |
|---|---|
| Pakistan (with amendments) | https://pakistancode.gov.pk and https://www.na.gov.pk (Constitution page) |
| All other syllabus countries in English | **Constitute Project**, https://www.constituteproject.org (USA, UK sources, France, Germany, Turkey, Iran, Malaysia, India, China) |
| USA | https://constitution.congress.gov |
| Germany (official English translation) | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_gg/ |
| France (official English) | https://www.conseil-constitutionnel.fr (English section) |
| India | https://legislative.gov.in (Constitution of India) |

Use: check article numbers you quote (Art. 49.3 France; Art. 67 Germany; Art. 153 Malaysia; Art. 110 Iran; Art. 370 India).

### 2.6 Current affairs for Paper II (FREE)
- **Dawn** (https://www.dawn.com): Pakistan, constitution, foreign policy; editorials and op-eds.
- **Official sites**: UN (https://www.un.org), EU (https://european-union.europa.eu), IMF Pakistan page (https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/PAK), WTO (https://www.wto.org), Ministry of Foreign Affairs (https://mofa.gov.pk).
- **PILDAT** (https://pildat.org): reports on Pakistan's Parliament, elections and civil–military relations.
- The **Current Affairs pack** in `../../06-Compulsory-Papers/4-Current-Affairs/` keeps dated facts for all Paper II countries; read its `10-Keeping-It-Current.md` routine.
